# Welcome

MPFree is free, open-source software that turns an Akai MPC One into a new instrument: a sampler, drum machine, synth, looper and sequencer built for playing live and making beats, with fewer menus than the factory software.

{{shot: play-all-loop | The Play page, MPFree's home screen, in the middle of a jam: six loops going (three playing, two turned off and one recording) with two slots still empty, a beat in the pattern with a 1:2 kick, a 50 % kick and a hat switched off, the Stutter throw held, the XY pad's filter and echo up, and the master meter moving.}}

This manual is for musicians and producers. It assumes you have never used an MPC before. If you have, look for the boxes marked **Coming from an MPC**: they say where MPFree works differently from the factory software, and they are all collected in the appendix [Coming from an MPC](reference/coming-from-an-mpc.md).

> [!WARNING]
> MPFree is early, test software. It runs from a USB stick and leaves the MPC's own system untouched, but there is no public release yet and much of what this manual describes is still being designed. Pages that may change carry a banner at the top.

## How this manual is organised

- **Getting started** walks you from switching on to your first beat and your first loop.
- **Making music** covers each part of MPFree in depth, roughly in the order of the tabs: playing live, the Sequencer, the Pads tab, looping, sampling, synths, clips and arranging, audio tracks, mixing and effects, then other gear, saving and exporting.
- **Reference** lists every button, every setting and every term.
