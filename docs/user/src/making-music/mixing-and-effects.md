# Mixing and effects

The mixer, submixes, effects on a track or the master, Perform FX, and macros.

## Effects and the chain

Each strip in the mixer has a row of insert effects. Tap an effect in that row to open the **chain editor** on it, in the mixer's place; tap the strip's **+** to add an effect, with the list of effects already open. The Q-Links turn the selected effect's first four knobs, and the data wheel works as Q-Link 1. **‹ Mixer** goes back to the mixer as you left it.

{{shot: chain | The chain editor on the drum bus, with Drive selected.}}

## Macros

Macros are knobs that move several settings at once (Wash, Grit and so on). The **Q-LINK** button steps the Q-Links through Macros 1-4, Macros 5-8 and back to the page's own knobs, on the Play page and in the chain editor. Hold a finger still on a macro's card for half a second to open the **macros panel**, where you can see and set up all of them; Back returns to the page you were on.
