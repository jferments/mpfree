# Arranging a song

**Arranging** is turning parts into a song: an intro, a verse, a drop, a breakdown and an ending, in order.

MPFree doesn't have a song timeline yet. Two things you can do today get you most of the way, and the full song tools are coming.

## Today: perform the arrangement

Build the song's sections as clips on the **Arrange** tab, a row per section: for example, row 1 for the intro (just drums), row 2 for the verse (drums and bass), row 3 for the drop (everything). Then play the song by launching clips. Each one waits for the next bar line, so every change lands in time. See [Clips and patterns](clips.md).

On the Play page, the **speaker icons** mute and unmute single pads and loop layers, and the **throws** and **Drop drums** make breakdowns and transitions while you hold them. See [Playing live](playing-live.md).

## Coming

- **Scenes:** launch a whole row of clips with one tap.
- **The song:** a timeline you can record your clip launches into, then edit.
- **Recording a performance:** capture a whole live set, launches, mutes and effects, as a song.

> [!MPC]
> The MPC builds songs from sequences in Song mode, or on its Arranger timeline. MPFree's tracks launch their clips independently, so you can change the bass without changing the drums. Its song timeline is still to come.
