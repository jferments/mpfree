# Glossary

**Arm** — Pick the track that records. On the Arrange tab, tap a track's name.

**Arrange** — The tab where clips are recorded, kept as takes and launched, and laid out as a song.

**Audio track** — A track that records sound from the INPUTS onto the song's timeline, under the clip lanes.

**BPM** — Beats per minute: the tempo.

**Browser** — MPFree's file manager, opened with BROWSE: projects, samples, kits and the rest of the stick.

**Capture** — Keep what you just played as a clip, even though you weren't recording.

**Chain** — The effects on one mixer strip, in the order the sound goes through them.

**Chance** — How likely a note is to play each time round, from 0 to 100 %.

**Chop** — Cut a sample into slices, one per pad.

**Clip** — A recorded or programmed part (a pattern, a bass line) on one track.

**Comping** — Recording a part several times, then playing the best bits of each take, from the take lanes.

**Condition** — A rule for when a note plays, such as every second time round (1:2) or only during a fill (FILL).

**Crossfade** — Blending from one sample into the next where two zones or velocity layers meet, instead of a hard switch.

**CV/gate** — Voltages that play a modular synth: CV sets the pitch or moves a value, the gate says when a note is held.

**Envelope** — How a sound's volume rises and falls each time it plays: attack, hold, decay, sustain, release.

**Euclidean fill** — Spreading a number of hits as evenly as they go over a number of steps, a quick way to write a groove.

**Flatten** — Recording a pad as it sounds now, with all its settings, into a new sound.

**I/O view** — The mixer's view of where everything goes: audio, MIDI, sync and CV.

**Insert** — An effect a sound goes through on its own mixer strip.

**Instrument** — What a track plays: the kit on the pads, one of MPFree's synths, or a plugin synth.

**Keygroup** — A pad that plays one sample across a range of notes, higher and lower, like a keyboard instrument.

**Kit** — A set of drum sounds, one per pad, across the pad banks.

**Latch** — Keep Note Repeat or the arpeggiator playing after you let go of the pads. A sampled pad also has a Latch of its own: a hit starts it and the next hit stops it.

**Layer** — One recording in a loop. The first pass is the first layer; every overdub adds another. A sampled pad's velocity layers are its different sounds for soft and hard hits.

**Loop** — A few bars of audio that play round and round, in time, recorded by the looper.

**Macro** — A knob that moves several settings at once. There are eight.

**Master safety** — The limiter at the end of the master that keeps the output from distorting harshly by accident.

**MIDI** — The language keyboards, synths and drum machines use to send notes, knob moves and timing to each other.

**MIDI clock** — Timing pulses one device sends so another plays at the same tempo.

**MIDI Learn** — Teaching MPFree which knob, fader or pedal on a controller moves a value on the screen.

**Mute group** — Pads that cut each other off, like an open and a closed hi-hat.

**Note Repeat** — Hold NOTE REPEAT and a pad, and the pad repeats in time.

**Nudge** — Move a note a little early or late.

**Overdub** — Record on top of what is there, keeping it.

**Pad bank** — One set of 16 sounds on the pads. There are eight: A to H.

**Pad layout** — How the pads play a synth: Chromatic, In key, or Chord pads.

**Parameter lock** — A setting changed for one step only, such as a filter that opens on one hit.

**Pattern** — A clip shown as a grid of steps on the Play page.

**Pin** — A hit in a warped sound fixed to a beat on the Warp page; the sound between pins stretches to fit.

**Plugin** — An effect or synth made by someone else, in the CLAP format, that MPFree can load from its stick.

**Prefs** — MPFree's settings, opened with SHIFT + MENU.

**Project** — One song, saved with everything it needs: clips, kits, sounds, the mixer, loops and audio tracks.

**Q-Link** — One of the four knobs beside the screen. What they control depends on the page.

**Ratchet** — A step that plays its note several times in quick succession.

**Region** — A block of recorded audio on an audio track.

**Resample** — Record MPFree's own sound (the whole mix, a track or a pad) back onto a pad or into a loop.

**Scene** — A row of the clip grid, launched all at once. The Sample tab also has two scenes of the whole kit to morph between.

**Send** — How much of a sound goes to one of the four shared effects (send returns A to D).

**Skip back** — Keep what came into the Rec page in the last seconds or bars, without having pressed Record.

**Song** — Clips laid out on a timeline on the Arrange tab, one lane per track, with the audio tracks under them.

**Submix** — A mixer strip that groups several sounds, so one fader and one set of effects serve them all.

**Swing** — Delaying every other step a little, for a shuffled feel.

**Take** — One recording. UNDO takes back the newest; SHIFT + UNDO brings it back.

**Throw** — A short performance effect that plays while you hold it.

**Track** — A lane of clips that plays one clip at a time, with its own sound.

**Tracker** — A view of a clip as a list of steps from top to bottom, every step's settings in a row.

**Tuning file** — A Scala file (`.scl`, with an optional `.kbm`) that tunes the synths to a scale of your own.

**Velocity** — How hard a pad is hit, from 1 (softest) to 127 (hardest).

**View** — One of the Play page's four layouts: All, Beats, Loop and Drum.

**Warp** — Playing a sample in time with the project's tempo: Stretch keeps its pitch, Repitch changes it like a record.

**16 Levels** — One sound spread over all 16 pads, each a step of velocity, tune, cutoff, decay or start.
