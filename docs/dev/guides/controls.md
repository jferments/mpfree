# Controls catalogue

Every control on MPFree's screen: what it is for, how to use it, and an example of it in use. **Status:** built, 2026-11-13 (the control audit, Jay's ask of 13 Nov).

A control is named by the key the smoke tests use, `widget/id`: the widget's name in the page layout, then the id its `targets()` gives the control. A control that repeats (the 16 pads, a list's rows, a strip on every track) has one entry for all of them. `‹` and `›` are the left and right halves of a setting's value: a tap steps it down or up.

**Test:** names the test that runs the example, where one does. Every control is also pressed by the sweep, `every_control_on_every_screen_does_something` in [`smoke.rs`](../../../rust/mpfree-app/src/smoke.rs), which fails on a control that does nothing, and the random walks in `smoke/walk.rs` press them in random orders ([Testing and CI](testing-and-ci.md#gui-smoke-tests)). `every_swept_control_is_in_the_catalogue` (in [`smoke/catalogue.rs`](../../../rust/mpfree-app/src/smoke/catalogue.rs)) fails when the sweep finds a key this page doesn't list: add the entry in the same pull request.

A control that can't do anything right now is drawn faint, and a tap on it says why on the hint line; a lit tab or a picked row is "already chosen", and a tap on it changes nothing. The sweep counts both as "off". For musicians, the [user manual's screen page](../../user/src/getting-started/the-screen.md) and [buttons page](../../user/src/reference/controls.md) cover the same ground in plain words.

## Everywhere: the top bar, the master bar, the Q-Links

- **`header/tab`**: the page tabs (Play, Sequencer, Looper, Sample, Synth, Arrange, Pads, Mix, in Jay's order of 18 Nov; past six, each as wide as its name). Tap one to go to that page; the lit tab is the page you are on. Mix opens the mixer as you left it; TRACK MIX opens it on Track Mix and goes back again. *Example:* tap Arrange to see the clips, then Play to get back to the pads. *Test:* `the_mode_buttons_pick_play_views_and_sample_editors`.
- **`header/folder-icon`**: the folder at the top bar's right edge: opens the browser over the page, as BROWSE does, and closes it again; it is lit while the browser shows. The I/O view is the Mix tab's (its I/O chip), and the tempo chip opens it too. *Example:* tap the folder, pick Kick 01.wav, tap the folder again to close. *Test:* `the_folder_icon_opens_the_browser_and_the_tempo_chip_opens_the_io_view`.
- **`header/chip`**: the chips at the right of the top bar (there is no kit chip since the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign's step 4: `play-transport/pads` and `smp-pads-bar/kit` open the list of kits). The Full or Half level chip turns that level off; the tempo chip, while another device's clock sets the tempo, opens the I/O view at Sync; the arp chip opens the arp panel; the MAKE or PERFORM chip, the leftmost, switches between Make and Perform (sequencer S4). On Play the loop keys have no chip (the loop row's Pads: loop keys switch gives the pads back); on another page of the Perform set the chip is Pads: Perform B (its letter) and a tap turns the set off (`FromUi::PerformOff`). While the synth is chosen for the pads (the instrument redesign's step 3), on every page, the chip is Pads: Poly (the synth's or kit's name) and a tap opens What the pads play over the Synth tab, whose first row holds Follow track (sampler step 15e; `FromUi::PadMapOpen`, `App::open_pad_map`; before it, a tap was Follow track). The status chip, always the rightmost, shows the CPU and the RAM ("CPU 24 %  RAM 38 %", as wide as "CPU 100 %  RAM 100 %", `Chip::room`), green, yellow or red by them and the problems on the status board, as a ring while the colour comes only from problems that ended unseen; a tap opens System status and a tap again closes it (`FromUi::StatusChip`, `App::toggle_status`; System status phase 2, Jay's spec of 22 Nov, [status.md](../design/status.md)). The time-left and 16 Levels chips only show: a tap says on the hint line what the chip shows. A fixed-width chip doesn't nudge the chips beside it as its numbers change (Jay, 18 Nov; test `a_reading_that_changes_width_moves_no_chip`). *Example:* press FULL LEVEL, play a pad softly (it plays at full velocity), tap the Full level chip: the pads follow your hits again. *Tests:* `a_tap_on_the_level_chip_turns_that_level_off`, `the_status_chip_opens_system_status_and_red_problems_reach_the_hint_line`, `the_quick_band_comes_with_note_repeat_and_the_chip_opens_the_panel`, `make_and_perform_switch_from_the_chip_and_shift_main`, `the_pads_switch_and_chip_turn_the_loop_keys_on_and_off`, `the_synth_chosen_for_the_pads_is_named_and_follow_track_gives_them_back`.
- **`power/button`**: the buttons of the power card a short press of the power button opens over any page (power.rs): Cancel, Restart and Shut down; over unsaved work Cancel, Don't save and Save and shut down (or restart); OK when it can't shut down from here. A tap chooses, as the wheel's push does on the outlined one. *Example:* press the power button briefly, tap Cancel: back on the page. *Tests:* `a_short_press_of_the_power_button_opens_the_power_card`, `shut_down_and_restart_go_to_the_systems_file`.
- **`textpad/key`**: a key of the text keyboard that a tap on a name opens over the page (the Sample tab's pad bar title): a letter, digit or symbol types at the cursor; Shift gives the next one its other character; #+= turns the letter keys into more symbols (! @ # $ % ^ & * and the rest) until ABC turns them back; Delete takes off the one before the cursor; Clear empties the name; Space; Cancel closes it and changes nothing; Enter keeps the names typed. A key that can't type now (the name is full) is drawn as a button that can't be used. *Example:* tap the pad bar's title, tap Clear, Shift, K, i, c, k, Enter: the pad is called Kick. *Tests:* `a_tap_on_the_pad_bars_title_names_the_pad_and_its_sound`, the `textpad` unit tests (`the_symbols_key_turns_the_letter_keys_into_symbols_until_abc`).
- **`textpad/field`**: the switch at the text keyboard's top right when there are two names to type (Pad and Sound): tap one to type that name; each keeps what was typed in it, and Enter keeps both. *Test:* `the_switch_picks_which_name_the_keys_type_and_each_keeps_its_own`.
- **`kits/row`**: a kit in the list of kits (from Play's PADS PLAY or the Pads tab's Kit); the synth comes after the kits, then **New kit** while an empty user kit is left (it puts one on the armed track). Tap one to load it on the pads; the list closes and the page comes back. *Example:* tap PADS PLAY on Play, tap Boom Bap: the pads play Boom Bap and PADS PLAY says so. *Tests:* `what_the_pads_play_and_the_pads_tabs_kit_open_the_list_of_kits`, `new_kit_puts_an_empty_kit_on_the_track_and_a_shared_kit_loads_into_one`.
- **`kits-card/button`**: Back on the kit list's card: closes the list without changing the kit (a tab closes it too). *Test:* `what_the_pads_play_and_the_pads_tabs_kit_open_the_list_of_kits`.
- **`lanes/row`**: a setting on the Lanes list (the Sequencer's **+ Lane**, the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign's step 5): the pad hit last's tune, decay, filter and level (and **Slice** on a sliced pad), then the armed track's volume, pan and sends A to D (a send named by its return's first effect, "Delay send"), each saying what it is on and "showing" when its lane is there already. A tap closes the list and gives that setting a lock lane with no locks (`PlayPage::add_lock_lane`), the Steps swiping to show it. *Example:* hit Kick, tap + Lane, tap Decay: a Decay lane under Kick. *Test:* `the_sequencers_lock_lanes_lock_turn_and_come_off`. Since step sequencer refactor D3 a drum track's list starts with the pad hit last's **Velocity** and **Chance** ("Kick · the pad hit last · drawn with a finger"): a tap gives that sound a value lane (`PlayPage::add_value_lane`, `steps/value`). *Test:* `a_velocity_lane_is_drawn_with_a_finger_in_one_edit`.
  On a synth track (scale rows) the list has the synth's first Q-Link set and each synth setting with a lane (`OnTrack::Inst`, "Track 1 · the synth"), then the track's. On a track that plays outside gear (a MIDI section Tracks out, step 5b, board 4) it has only the gear's controls (`OnTrack::Midi`): the CCs its profile names, else `common_cc_name`'s (CC 0, 6, 32, 38 and 96 to 101 left out), then Bend and Pressure, each "Mono synth · CC 43" (the profile's name, else the port and channel). The data wheel moves the row picked (the list follows it past the rows a finger reaches) and its push adds that row's lane. *Tests:* `a_synth_tracks_lock_lanes_hang_under_its_scale_rows`, `a_gear_tracks_lock_lanes_send_its_ccs`.
- **`lanes-card/button`**: Back on the Lanes list's card: closes the list, nothing added (a tab closes it too). On a gear track a second button, **Another CC**, closes the list and opens the number pad (0 to 119, `TypeTo::LaneCc`); Enter adds that CC's lane. *Tests:* `the_sequencers_lock_lanes_lock_turn_and_come_off`, `a_gear_tracks_lock_lanes_send_its_ccs`.
- **`edit-clips/row`**: a row of the Clips list (the Sequencer's clip chip on the Steps heading, step sequencer refactor C2): **What plays** first, then each clip of the selected track, saying "plays", "starts next" or "editing" beside its row number. A tap, or the wheel's push on the row the wheel moved to, closes the list and makes the Sequencer edit that clip without launching it (`FromUi::Time(TimeAction::EditClip(Some(slot)))`), or with What plays the clip that plays again (`EditClip(None)`); nothing starts or stops. *Example:* Pattern 1 plays on Drums; tap the chip, tap Pattern 3, tap steps: they go into Pattern 3 while Pattern 1 plays on and the heading says "Pattern 1 plays". *Tests:* `the_clip_chip_lists_the_tracks_clips_and_picks_one_to_edit`, `a_clip_picked_to_edit_takes_the_steps_and_nothing_starts`.
- **`edit-clips-card/button`**: Back on the Clips list's card: closes the list, the clip edited unchanged (a tab closes it too). The card says the Sequencer edits the clip picked, nothing starts or stops, and picking another track goes back to what plays. *Test:* `the_clip_chip_lists_the_tracks_clips_and_picks_one_to_edit`.
- **`step-size/row`**: a row of the Step size list (the Sequencer's step size chip, left of the length on the Steps heading, step sequencer refactor D1): 1/4, 1/8, 1/8T, 1/16, 1/16T and 1/32, each with the steps the clip would have in it, its own marked "now". A tap, or the wheel's push on the row the wheel moved to, closes the list and cuts the clip into steps of that size (`FromUi::Time(TimeAction::StepSize(ticks))`), a clip setting UNDO takes back. The clip keeps its length and every note its time; only the grid, STEP SEQ's pages and the nudges shown change. *Example:* a 1-bar beat, tap the chip, tap 1/32: 32 steps, the hats still on the 1/16s, and a roll can go between them. *Tests:* `the_step_size_chip_lists_the_sizes_and_picks_one`, `the_step_size_cuts_the_clip_into_other_steps_and_no_note_moves`.
- **`step-size-card/button`**: Back on the Step size list's card: closes the list, the step size unchanged (a tab closes it too). The card says each step lasts the size picked, no note moves, the clip keeps its length and UNDO puts the size back. *Test:* `the_step_size_chip_lists_the_sizes_and_picks_one`.
- **`master/level`**: the master level bar on the Play page; drag it for the level of everything (it follows the finger from where it lands, so a tap leaves it; `the_master_bar_follows_a_drag_from_where_it_lands`), the same level as the mixer's master fader. *Example:* drag the bar down to -6 dB, open the mixer: its master fader is at -6 dB too. *Test:* `the_master_bar_and_the_mixer_master_are_one_level`.
- **`qlinks/knob-card`**: the four Q-Link cards in the right-hand column. Each shows what its knob turns on this page; drag a card to turn it from the screen. *Example:* on the Sequencer's Steps, tap a step, then drag the Velocity card: the step plays softer. With notes picked in the note editor the column is the note inspector, under a Note edit header: Position, Length, Semitones and Velocity (`EditAction::Knob`, one take a turn; piano roll plan 1C.1). *Tests:* `play_has_one_view_and_the_sequencer_switches_steps_and_notes`, `beats_picks_a_step_for_the_q_links`, `the_note_inspector_turns_notes_one_turn_at_a_time`.
- **`guide/button`**: Skip and Done in the button guide (it walks through the hardware buttons the first time, and from Prefs). Skip moves on to the next button, leaving this one unlearned; Done ends the guide. *Example:* the guide asks for REC and you want it later: tap Skip.

## Play page

- **`play-transport/view`**: the view switch on the Sequencer tab: Steps and Notes (its Steps are Beats' pages in Play's frame). Steps shows already; Notes opens the note editor on the clip the steps show (`EditAction::Open`). Play has one view and no switch (the looper redesign's step 5: the loop row took the Loop view's place), so its card has the bar dots beside what the pads play. *Tests:* `play_has_one_view_and_the_sequencer_switches_steps_and_notes`, `the_switch_taps_give_the_views`.
- **`play-transport/pads`**: what the pads play, at the right end of Play's transport ("PADS PLAY" over "Track 1 · Classic House · A": the track Play's rows show, its kit and the bank; or the synth's line). A tap opens the list of kits over the page. While controller mode is on it says what the Controller chip says ("Controller → Juno-X · ch 3 · A"), with its dot in warn, and a tap says the chip gives the pads back. *Example:* press PAD BANK B: it reads "… · B". *Tests:* `what_the_pads_play_and_the_pads_tabs_kit_open_the_list_of_kits`, `play_names_the_kit_and_the_header_shows_ram`.
- **`play-transport/rec`**: the REC chip between the tempo and the bar dots: what REC records (the armed track's clip or the picked loop), grey while armed, outlined red while ready, solid red while recording. A tap opens the target list in place of the view, and closes it ([REC records what is armed](../design/rec-arm.md)). *Example:* tap the loop row's Arm: the chip reads "REC → Loop 1". *Tests:* `rec_records_what_is_armed_and_play_starts_a_ready_take`, `arm_pills_and_the_target_list_move_the_arm_and_pin_it`.
- **`rec-list/row`**: a row of the REC chip's target list (each track's notes in track order, then the loop; in two columns with many tracks): a tap arms it, pinned or not (a track's row: that track alone, as picking it); on the armed one it says what REC records; SHIFT + a tap on the loop's row arms both (or, both armed, lets go of the one tapped), and on a track's row records that track too, or lets it go (several tracks in one take, [rec-arm.md](../design/rec-arm.md#several-tracks)). *Test:* `shift_and_a_track_row_record_several_tracks_in_one_take`. **`rec-list/switch`**: Rec song (the grid into the song) and Pin the arm (only a tap on Arm moves it). **`rec-list/close`**: closes the list. *Test:* `arm_pills_and_the_target_list_move_the_arm_and_pin_it`.
- **`play-transport/tempo`**: the tempo. Tap it in time, four times or more, to set the tempo (as TAP TEMPO). *Example:* tap along with a record at 92 BPM: the tempo reads 92. *Tests:* `a_tap_on_plays_tempo_taps_it`, `the_tempo_tapped_on_play_sets_the_transport`.
- **`steps/step`**: one step of a drum lane (the Sequencer's Steps, and Play's rows). Tap an empty step to put a note there, and a lit step to take it out with its locks (`StepAction::Tap`, one take for UNDO), as a hit on a step pad does (Jay's device run, 20 Nov 2026, replacing the redesign's step 3b). A tap on a step switched off by the inspector brings it back (`Change::On`). On the Steps a step's tap acts when the finger lifts, and a finger held on a lit step for 0.4 s picks it instead (for the inspector and the Q-Links: velocity, length, nudge, chance; `StepGrid::press`, `Player::step_hold_tick`). ERASE held + a tap takes the step out too. On Play's rows the tap acts when the finger lifts, and a finger that moves swipes the rows instead (`Step { index: ROWS, delta }`). *Example:* tap Kick steps 1, 5, 9 and 13 and press PLAY: four on the floor. *Tests:* `steps_tapped_on_beats_play_in_time`, `beats_picks_a_step_for_the_q_links`, `play_rows_show_the_bank_in_order_and_a_swipe_moves_them`, `a_tap_takes_a_step_out_and_a_hold_picks_it`, `a_slow_tap_still_toggles_and_an_empty_step_held_gets_a_note`.
- **`steps/mute`**: a lane's speaker: mutes and unmutes that pad's lane. It is the same mute as the pad's M in the pad mixer. *Test:* `a_speaker_on_play_and_m_in_the_pad_mixer_are_one_mute`.
- **`steps/arm`**: the Arm pill at the left of the pattern's heading: grey "Arm" when REC records the loop (a tap arms the notes), red "Armed", "Ready" or "Rec" when it records them (a tap says so). SHIFT + a tap adds the notes to the loop, so REC records both; with both armed a plain tap keeps only the notes. *Test:* `arm_pills_and_the_target_list_move_the_arm_and_pin_it`, `shift_arm_records_the_notes_and_a_loop_in_one_pass`.
- **`steps/head`**: the pattern's head: ‹ and › turn its pages of 16 steps, - halves the steps (down to 16) and + doubles them (up to 128, the new steps repeating the old). Each length change is one take, so UNDO brings the steps back. Faint when it can't act; a tap then says why. On a synth track's scale rows it also has **Fold** (only the notes the clip uses) and the octave's ‹ and › round "Oct 3" ([step-pads.md](../design/step-pads.md#scale-rows-on-beats-s3)). *Test:* `the_patterns_head_turns_pages_and_doubles_or_halves_its_steps`, `beats_on_a_synth_track_shows_scale_rows_that_fold_and_play`. On Play's rows the head is Play's own ([layout spec](../design/layout-spec.md) section 4, All): **Previous track** and **Next track** (each half of the track chip; `FromUi::Time(TimeAction::Arm(Some(t)))`, Loop tracks skipped), **Bank A** to **Bank H** (`FromUi::PadBank`, the bank on the pads faint), ‹ and › for the page, and **Follow** (the page follows the playhead); no length, and no Sequencer tab button since 18 Nov (the tab is in the top bar; its room went to 60 px page arrows and wider banks). *Test:* `play_shows_the_armed_tracks_pads_a_bank_at_a_time`. On the Sequencer's Steps the head also has **+ Lane** (a drum track's, and since step 5b a synth or gear track's) (`Tab(HEAD_LANE)`, faint until the track has a clip): it opens the Lanes list (`lanes/row`). *Test:* `lock_lanes_hang_under_their_sounds_and_a_drag_turns_a_lock`. Since C2 the pattern's name there is a chip (`Tab(HEAD_CLIP)`): it opens the Clips list (`edit-clips/row`). *Test:* `the_clip_chip_lists_the_tracks_clips_and_picks_one_to_edit`. Since D1 a step size chip sits left of the length (`Tab(HEAD_SIZE)`, faint until the track has a clip): it opens the Step size list (`step-size/row`). *Test:* `the_step_size_chip_lists_the_sizes_and_picks_one`. Since composing step 4, on a kit track's clip the head also has **Continue** between the track's name and + Lane (`Tab(HEAD_CONTINUE)`): it opens Continue on Steps in the step editor's place and asks the time engine at once to carry the beat on (`ContinueAction::Ask`, the kit's drums read from its pads' names sent just before it, `ContinueAction::Parts`); while it is open the head shows the bar the grid shows and what it does ("Bar 5 · land home · fill") in place of the size, the length and + Lane. *Test:* `continue_on_steps_asks_for_a_beat_and_its_buttons_answer`.
- **`steps/length`**: the length on the Sequencer's Steps head ("16 steps", between – and +; step sequencer refactor D2). A tap puts the outline on it, grabbed (`Tab(HEAD_LENGTH)`), and the wheel or – and + then add or take one step a detent or press (`Step { index: LENGTH_TURN }`, `FromUi::Steps(StepAction::LengthBy { steps })`, worked out from the clip's own length so fast detents all count): up to 128 steps, down to one, a longer recorded clip never cut by a +. New steps are empty; steps that go take their notes, and each step is one take UNDO brings back. SHIFT + – or + (or SHIFT + the wheel with a finger on it) doubles or halves it as the head's + and – do (`LENGTH_TWICE`, `StepAction::Length`). The push, or a tap elsewhere, lets go. Faint with no clip; a tap then says to tap a step first. *Example:* a 16-step bass, tap "16 steps", turn the wheel back three: 13 steps against the 16-step beat. *Tests:* `a_tap_on_the_length_puts_the_wheel_on_it_a_step_at_a_time`, `the_length_goes_a_step_at_a_time_and_a_long_clip_is_never_cut`.
- **`steps/lock`**: a step of a lock lane on the Sequencer's Steps (a drum track; the redesign's step 5, board 3). A lane shows for every setting the clip locks and for each added with + Lane, under its sound (the track's after all the sounds), its name in a dashed purple box over "lock · Kick". A tap (on the lift) locks the step at the sound's own value (`StepAction::Lock`), or takes its lock off (the lane stays); a drag turns it a detent each 8 px (SHIFT fine), and so does the wheel while a finger holds it (`Step { index: LOCK_LANE + lane * 16 + step }`). ERASE held + a tap takes that lock off. A sound's lock on a step with no hit waits for one (the hint says so). With more rows than fit, the rows swipe (`Step { index: ROWS }`, `PlayPage::steps_top`) and a rail shows where. On a synth or gear track's scale rows the lanes go under the notes, as many as leave three notes, and a swipe shows the others (step 5b, board 4); a gear's CC reads 0 to 127 and turns 4 a detent (1 with SHIFT), the bend reads with its sign and turns 256 (16). *Example:* lock Kick's Decay on step 7 and drag it down to 180 ms. *Tests:* `lock_lanes_hang_under_their_sounds_and_a_drag_turns_a_lock`, `lock_lanes_lock_turn_and_come_off`, `the_sequencers_lock_lanes_lock_turn_and_come_off`, `lock_lanes_go_under_the_scale_rows_and_swipe`, `a_gear_lock_reads_its_midi_value_and_turns_a_detent`.
- **`steps/lock-lane-off`**: a lock lane's ×, in place of the mute: the lane goes with all its locks (one take; UNDO brings them, and with them the lane, back). Perform's guard holds it (a hold presses it). On a Velocity or Chance lane (`steps/value`) the × only takes the lane away; the steps keep their values and nothing is sent (`PlayPage::clear_value_lane`). *Tests:* `the_sequencers_lock_lanes_lock_turn_and_come_off`, `a_velocity_lane_is_drawn_with_a_finger_in_one_edit`.
- **`steps/value`**: a step of a Velocity or Chance lane on the Sequencer's Steps (step sequencer refactor D3), added from the Lanes list under its sound: a bar on each step with a note, as high as the note's velocity (1 to 127) or chance (1 to 100 %), its value over it; a step switched off draws faint, a picked one outlined. A finger dragged across the lane sets each step it crosses to the height under it (between two samples the steps in between follow the line), the bars following the finger; only its lift sends anything: every step it changed in one `StepAction::Values { pad, from, kind, values }` (`Tab(VALUE_DONE)`, the changes in `StepGrid::stroke_done`; `PlayPage::value_stroke`), which `TimeControl` makes one edit (`Grid::change_each`), so one UNDO takes the whole stroke back. A tap is a stroke of one step. A second finger on the Steps calls the stroke off and nothing is sent. The wheel while a finger holds a step sets it 5 a detent (1 with SHIFT; `Step { index: LOCK_LANE + lane * 16 + step }`, `PlayPage::turn_value`). The bars are drawn by the shared lane (`mpfree_time_ui::lane::Lane`). *Example:* add Hat's Velocity lane and drag a finger along it in a wave: soft and loud hats in one stroke. *Tests:* `a_velocity_lane_is_drawn_with_a_finger_in_one_edit`, `a_lanes_stroke_sets_each_step_its_own_value_in_one_undo`.
- **`steps/sound-lane`**: one of Continue's lanes on the Sequencer's Steps (composing tools S3, board B), after the lock lanes and the Velocity and Chance lanes, drawn in the theme's green with a bar on each step where it holds its knob, valued as the knob's lock lane is (`PlayPage::sound_lane`, from `StepClip::sound_lanes`: `time::SoundSteps`, heard from the knob where it is now). Read only: no ×, and a tap anywhere on its name (`Toggle(LOCK_LANE + lane)`) or on a step (`Cell`, on the lift; no drag), the wheel on it, or ERASE + a tap only says what it does and that Notes turns it on or off or takes it out (`PlayPage::sound_lane_says`); nothing is sent. Off, it is faint with "off: your knob plays as it is set". *Example:* keep a Continue try with Sound on, open the Sequencer's Steps: "Cutoff" over "Continue · under your locks", green bars on the new bars. *Tests:* `continues_lane_on_the_steps_says_where_it_changes`, `continues_lane_is_read_only_on_the_steps`, `continues_lanes_show_after_the_lock_lanes_read_only`.
- **`steps-continue-bars/bar`**: a bar in Continue on Steps' bar strip (composing tools, step 4), in the timing strip's place while Continue is open: your bars and each new one as a small picture of its hits (a row per sound, in the grid's colours; the new bars faint), its number and job above it ("1 yours", "4 vary", "5 land home · fill"). A tap pages the grid to that bar (`Tab(bar)`); the bar shown is outlined and can't be tapped. *Example:* tap 5: the grid shows the last bar's fill and the head reads "Bar 5 · land home · fill". *Test:* `continue_on_steps_shows_the_new_bars_and_pages_to_them`.
- **`steps-continue/row`**, **`steps-continue/less`**, **`steps-continue/more`**: Continue on Steps' settings, in the step editor's place: **Style** (Minimal, Groove, Jazz-ish), **Bars** (+1, +2, +4, +8 or +16) and **Ending** (Home, Open, Loop), the note editor's Continue rows in the same words. A tap on a name picks the row the wheel steps (`Select(row)`; the wheel's push picks the next); ‹ and › step its value (`Step { index: row, delta }`), which asks for a new try at once (`ContinueAction::Ask`), the last one playing on until it is ready. *Example:* tap Bars' ›: "+8 bars", and eight new bars follow yours. *Test:* `continue_on_steps_asks_for_a_beat_and_its_buttons_answer`.

## Looper tab

The Looper tab as an instrument (17 Nov redesign; [the manual](../../user/src/making-music/looping.md#the-looper-tab), layout spec 4a): the deck of 8 loops across the top, the picked loop under it, the Q-Links at the right.

- **`ldeck/ring`**: a loop's ring. A tap is the loop's next step, on the press: an empty loop records from the next bar line (or PLAY), a waiting one cancels, a first pass ends on the next bar line, a loop that plays overdubs, an overdub ends, a muted loop plays (`LoopAct::Tap`); a capturing one calls its Capture off; a free loop at rest while the free loops are stopped (faint, ▶ in its middle) plays the free loops (`Command::FreePlay`: at once with the song stopped, else on the song's next bar line, the middle saying "on the bar" until then, when a tap calls it off). It also picks the loop. Held for 1 s it clears the loop, the arc draining in red (`LoopAct::Clear`); let go sooner and nothing changes. With ERASE held a tap clears the loop at once. UNDO brings a cleared loop back (its empty ring says "Cleared · UNDO"). *Example:* tap Loop 1's ring, play four bars on the pads, tap it again: it plays; tap once more to overdub. *Tests:* `the_looper_tab_plays_loops_and_works_layers`, `a_ring_shows_the_first_pass_recording_through_its_bars`, `once_solo_and_a_clear_that_undo_brings_back`.
- **`ldeck/pads-keys`**: the deck's Pads as loop keys switch: makes the 16 pads the loop keys (top two rows: each loop's key, as a tap on its ring; bottom two rows: each loop's mute, SHIFT + a mute solos) or the pads again (`LoopAct::PadKeys`, as SHIFT + SAMPLER on the Looper tab). It lights while page A of the Perform set (the loop keys) is on the pads; on another page (PAD BANK B-H) it is dark and a tap brings page A back. *Example:* tap Pads as loop keys, press pad 13: Loop 1 records from the next bar line; press PAD BANK B and hold pad 2: Reverb wash plays. *Tests:* `the_pads_switch_and_chip_turn_the_loop_keys_on_and_off`, `the_pads_as_loop_keys_record_overdub_mute_and_clear`.
- **`ldeck/capture`**: the bar over the deck's Capture: the last bars played become a loop, the picked one if empty, else the first empty one, for its Length's bars (Until tap: 4), as SHIFT + REC on the Looper tab (`LoopAct::Capture`, `FromUi::LoopCapture`). It says how many ("Capture last 2 bars"), is faint with no whole bar heard, and lit ("Capturing") while the picked loop captures, when a tap calls it off. *Example:* press PLAY, play the pads for three bars, tap Capture: bars 2 and 3 become Loop 1 and play in time. *Tests:* `capture_in_the_header_asks_for_the_last_bars`, `capture_turns_what_you_played_into_a_loop`.
- **`ldeck/records-from`**: the deck's Records into chip, over the loops (layout spec 4a, board 5; Records from until 4e.5): where the next loop goes and the input it records, its track's ("Records into: Audio 1 · row 1 · Pads · Kit 1"), with the input's meter; a tap says on the hint line that a loop records its track's input, changed under the track's name on Clips (`gui::ON_TRACKS_INPUT`). While a take holds another source than the one picked, it says THIS TAKE · NEXT: (the pick) over what the take records (`LooperView::taking_from`). *Example:* tap it, tap Master: the next loop records everything but the loops. *Tests:* `a_take_holds_its_source_and_instrument_to_its_end`, `the_looper_records_the_pads_synth_while_rec_records_its_notes_and_the_drums_play_on`.
- **`leditor/capture`**: an empty loop's What you played: the button over the bars Capture would take ("Capture bars 10 and 11"), shown when Capture goes into this loop; a tap is Capture. *Tests:* `the_strip_shows_what_the_moment_needs`, `capture_turns_what_you_played_into_a_loop`.
- **`ldeck/loop`**: a loop's name: picks it without starting anything (with ERASE held, clears it). *Test:* `the_looper_tab_plays_loops_and_works_layers`.
- **`ldeck/loop-solo`**: a loop's S beside its speaker: its track's solo (board 5, since 4e.5), without picking the loop (`LoopAct::Solo`, `app::Effect::TrackMute`); a loop on no track yet solos itself, the others playing silent in their places, their rings dim with "by solo". It lights when on. *Tests:* `the_looper_tab_plays_loops_and_works_layers`, `once_solo_and_a_clear_that_undo_brings_back`.
- **`ldeck/loop-mute`**, **`ldeck/loop-level`**: a loop's speaker (its track's mute since 4e.5, without picking the loop; a loop on no track yet mutes itself, and stays silent through STOP and PLAY) and its level bar (drag from where the finger lands, so a tap leaves it, or a finger resting on it gives the wheel; -24 to +6 dB). *Tests:* `the_looper_tab_plays_loops_and_works_layers`, `the_q_links_turn_the_loops_levels_then_the_picked_loop`.
- **`leditor/layer`**, **`leditor/layer-mute`**, **`leditor/layer-solo`**, **`leditor/layer-delete`**: a layer's lane: a tap on the wave picks it, on the name mutes it, S solos it, × deletes the picked layer (on the only layer it clears the loop). A finger resting on a lane gives the wheel its level (SHIFT: pan). A press and hold of 0.4 s lifts the layer as a card (a finger that moves sooner swipes the lanes): the deck's loops that can take it light, empty ones too, and it drops on the one under the lift (`LoopAct::DropLayer`, `FromUi::LoopCopy`): it moves there, or with SHIFT a copy goes; onto an empty loop it becomes a loop of its own. Keeps the list as it shows now (only what the chips and lights let through), oldest first in columns with a heading, as a new text file on the stick's data partition, `files/midi-logs/midi-log-<date>-<time>.txt`; a second save in the same minute gets `-2`, so nothing is replaced. The line under the screen says which file it wrote. Greyed without a data partition. *Example:* a synth plays a wrong note now and then: tap the keyboard's channel light, play until it happens, tap Save log, and copy the file off the stick to compare. *Test:* `save_log_asks_the_app_and_says_its_answer` (mpfree-midi-ui), `a_saved_log_is_a_new_file_in_the_stores_folder` (mpfree-app).
- **`mixer/monitor-view`**: **List | Now** at the Monitor's top left. Now shows what one port and channel hold this moment instead of the messages: keys down on a keyboard with their velocities, the controls that moved and their values, the clock's timing, bend and pressure, and the notes still on going out. *Example:* a pad sound hangs: tap Now and see whether its key is still down or the sustain pedal is held. *Test:* `the_view_follows_the_newest_port_and_channel_and_says_what_is_held` (mpfree-midi-ui).
- **`mixer/monitor-stream`**, **`mixer/monitor-channel`**: in Now, a port's chip picks the port and way shown, and ‹ › (or the data wheel) the channel; until one is tapped, Now follows whichever sent something last. *Example:* a keyboard and a drum machine both send: tap the drum machine's chip and step to channel 10 to see its pads. *Test:* `the_view_follows_the_newest_port_and_channel_and_says_what_is_held` (mpfree-midi-ui).
- **`mixer/monitor-note-offs`**: **Send note-offs to MIDI out**, under Now's notes still on. Ends only the notes MPFree sent out of the 5-pin jacks and has not ended (the same as STOP's panic does for MIDI out), never an "all notes off" to the whole synth. Greyed while MIDI out is off in the run or no note is on. *Example:* a synth on MIDI out drones after a cable was pulled mid-note: Now says the note has been on for 41 s, stuck, and one tap ends it. *Test:* `the_monitors_note_offs_are_taken_once` (mpfree-app).
- **`browser/filter`**, **`browser/key`**, **`browser/confirm-yes`**, **`browser/confirm-no`**: the browser's type filters, the search keyboard's keys, and a confirm dialog (moving a file to the Trash).
- **`browser/plugins-view`**, **`browser/plugins-filter`**, **`browser/plugins-search`**, **`browser/plugins-sort`**, **`browser/plugins-row`**: BROWSE's **Plugins** place (Open MPC plugins; [design](../design/mpc-plugin-store.md)). The views pick what the list shows: Catalog (the Open MPC catalog's plugins that run on this MPC), Installed, On the stick (release zips copied into `files/plugins/`). The chips under it show All, Instruments or Effects; Search looks in names, makers and tags (an on-screen keyboard opens); the order chip steps Most used (the catalog's download count), Newest, A to Z, favourites always first. A tap on the view or chip already on takes the list back to its top. A tap on a row picks it; on the picked row, the next picture. *Example:* BROWSE, Plugins, Effects, tap Tape Echo, read its CPU line, Install. *Tests:* `the_plugins_place_lists_the_stick_and_deletes_a_zip_on_the_second_tap` (mpfree-app gui.rs) and `orders_filters_and_search` (plugin_page.rs).
- **`browser/plugins-button`**, **`browser/plugins-picture`**: the Plugins place's buttons and the picked plugin's picture. **Install** downloads the catalog's zip over the Ethernet cable, checks it against the catalog's SHA-256 and unpacks it into `files/plugins/`; the plugin is offered at once on the Synth tab (an instrument) or in the chain editor (an effect). **Update** does the same for a newer version, keeping the musician's own files in the folder. **Install from zip** unpacks a zip copied to the stick. **Remove** and **Delete zip** ask again on the button (Tap again to remove); Back takes the question back. **Cancel** stops a download. **Pictures**, or a tap on the picture, steps through the plugin's own screen (each tab drawn as on the MPC, once installed) and the catalog's picture. **Update list** fetches the catalog again. *Example:* Installed, pick Mono Bass with Update 1.3.0 on it, Update, then play it from the Synth tab's Instrument row. *Tests:* `installed_and_older_says_update_and_remove_asks_twice`, `a_zip_on_the_stick_installs_from_the_zip_and_a_strange_one_is_flagged` (plugin_page.rs), `lists_a_zip_on_the_stick_and_refuses_one_that_is_not_a_plugin` (plugin_store.rs).
- **`browser/sets-row`**, **`browser/sets-back`**, **`browser/sets-add`**: BROWSE's **Sets** place (live shows B1; [design](../design/live-shows.md), layout spec 4p). The list shows every set with how many songs it holds and its last check as a chip (READY, LIMITS, NOT READY, NOT CHECKED). A tap on a set picks it, and a second tap opens it in the Set editor, where the list shows the set's songs in order ("Stopped · Let it finish" and each song's chip) and a tap picks a song. The **‹** on the title line goes back to the sets (from Add songs, back to the set). In **Add songs** the list shows Projects and a tap on a row's **+** puts that song after the song picked, or at the end; a song can be in a set more than once. In the check view a tap picks a song; while Soundcheck runs the song being checked stays shown and a tap says "Soundcheck is running: Stop soundcheck first". *Example:* BROWSE, Sets, New set, type "Friday", Add songs, + on Night drive and Sunday chops, Done. *Tests:* `a_new_set_is_named_filled_edited_and_saves_itself_after_each_change`, `the_sets_place_draws_and_answers_touches` (mpfree-browser-ui tests/sets.rs); `the_sets_screens_controls_do_something` (mpfree-app smoke.rs: the four Sets sample screens swept on their own).
- **`browser/sets-button`**, **`browser/sets-value`**, **`browser/sets-notes`**, **`browser/sets-need`**: the Sets place's buttons, a set song's values, its Notes box and its Needs list. On the list's title line: **New set** (asks for a name on the text keyboard and opens the empty set), **Add songs**, **Check set**, **Done**, and in the check view **Check every file** (reads every file in full). A set's card: **Play set**, **Edit set**, **Check set** (opens the check view, live shows B2: every part reads, every file its manifest lists is there and unchanged, the keyboards it needs are connected, its plugins are on the stick, its memory beside the next song and the stick's room; each song keeps its quick result in the set), **Soundcheck** (live shows B2b: asks first, every song, Out loud or Muted, then plays each song's busiest part and its change into the next, keeping what it measured in the set), **Rename**, **Copy** and **Delete** (asks first; the set goes to the Trash and its songs stay in Projects). The check view's song card: **Soundcheck this song** and **Open in Make**. Soundcheck's question: the ‹ and › halves of **Sound** (Out loud, Muted), **Start soundcheck** and **Cancel**; while it runs, **Stop soundcheck** (on the title line and the card; STOP does the same) and **Skip this song** (its part stops where it is, and its change starts); if the app has no Soundcheck running behind the screen, either puts the check view back (`show: no soundcheck is running`); at the end, **Done**. A song's card: the ‹ and › halves of **Starts** (Stopped, Playing, Count-in then play), **Ends** (Let it finish, Fade, Cut), **Fade** (1 to 30 s, faint unless Ends is Fade) and **Needs** (either half opens the list of the rig's keyboards; a tap goes Needed, Nice to have, not needed); a tap on a value also gives it to the dial. **Notes** opens the text keyboard (240 characters, the field shows three lines). **Move up**, **Move down**, **Open in Make** (opens the song as a plain project), **Remove from set** (asks again on the button). A song that changed while a set played lists its recovery copies under **Changed on stage**, with **Keep the changes** (the newest copy goes into the song, which keeps a version of what it was; older copies go to the Trash) and **Throw them away** (every copy to the Trash). **Play set** opens the Show view on Arrange (`show/…`). The set saves itself after every change. *Example:* Edit set, pick Night drive, Ends ›, Fade › twice, Needs, tap Keystep 37 (Needed), Done, Check set. *Tests:* `a_songs_needs_come_from_the_rig_and_the_check_marks_every_song`, `the_check_view_shows_every_songs_checks`, `soundcheck_asks_runs_and_says_how_it_went`, `rename_copy_delete_and_play_a_set` (mpfree-browser-ui tests/sets.rs); `a_set_is_soundchecked_song_by_song` (mpfree-app gui.rs and soundcheck.rs).
- **`browser/export`**: the Export panel, which **Export** in BROWSE > Projects opens over the browser. It turns the song into files: tick Mixdown, Stems and MIDI file, pick Stems by (Track, the default: one stem per track, the range playing again for each track that shares a channel, as the row says; or Channel: one per mixer channel in one pass), pick the range (Arrangement, or Bars with - and + for how many), check the File row (24-bit, 16-bit or 32-bit float, and 44.1 or 48 kHz; it starts as Prefs > Export file and Export rate have it, and a pick here is for this export only), then Start.
