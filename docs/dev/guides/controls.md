# Controls catalogue

Every control on MPFree's screen: what it is for, how to use it, and an example of it in use. **Status:** built, 2026-11-13 (the control audit, Jay's ask of 13 Nov).

A control is named by the key the smoke tests use, `widget/id`: the widget's name in the page layout, then the id its `targets()` gives the control. A control that repeats (the 16 pads, a list's rows, a strip on every track) has one entry for all of them. `‹` and `›` are the left and right halves of a setting's value: a tap steps it down or up.

**Test:** names the test that runs the example, where one does. Every control is also pressed by the sweep, `every_control_on_every_screen_does_something` in [`smoke.rs`](../../../rust/mpfree-app/src/smoke.rs), which fails on a control that does nothing, and the random walks in `smoke/walk.rs` press them in random orders ([Testing and CI](testing-and-ci.md#gui-smoke-tests)). `every_swept_control_is_in_the_catalogue` (in [`smoke/catalogue.rs`](../../../rust/mpfree-app/src/smoke/catalogue.rs)) fails when the sweep finds a key this page doesn't list: add the entry in the same pull request.

A control that can't do anything right now is drawn faint, and a tap on it says why on the hint line; a lit tab or a picked row is "already chosen", and a tap on it changes nothing. The sweep counts both as "off". For musicians, the [user manual's screen page](../../user/src/getting-started/the-screen.md) and [buttons page](../../user/src/reference/controls.md) cover the same ground in plain words.

## Everywhere: the top bar, the master bar, the Q-Links

- **`header/tab`**: the page tabs (Play, Pads, Sequencer, Arrange, Synth, Looper, Sample, Mix; past six, each as wide as its name). Tap one to go to that page; the lit tab is the page you are on. Mix opens the mixer as you left it; TRACK MIX opens it on Track Mix and goes back again. *Example:* tap Arrange to see the clips, then Play to get back to the pads. *Test:* `the_mode_buttons_pick_play_views_and_sample_editors`.
- **`header/folder-icon`**: the folder at the top bar's right edge: opens the browser over the page, as BROWSE does, and closes it again; it is lit while the browser shows. The I/O view is the Mix tab's (its I/O chip), and the tempo chip opens it too. *Example:* tap the folder, pick Kick 01.wav, tap the folder again to close. *Test:* `the_folder_icon_opens_the_browser_and_the_tempo_chip_opens_the_io_view`.
- **`header/chip`**: the chips at the right of the top bar (there is no kit chip since the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign's step 4: `play-transport/pads` and `smp-pads-bar/kit` open the list of kits). The Full or Half level chip turns that level off; the tempo chip, while another device's clock sets the tempo, opens the I/O view at Sync; the arp chip opens the arp panel; the MAKE or PERFORM chip, the leftmost, switches between Make and Perform (sequencer S4). The CPU, RAM, session, time-left and 16 Levels chips only show: a tap says on the hint line what the chip shows. *Example:* press FULL LEVEL, play a pad softly (it plays at full velocity), tap the Full level chip: the pads follow your hits again. *Tests:* `a_tap_on_the_level_chip_turns_that_level_off`, `the_quick_band_comes_with_note_repeat_and_the_chip_opens_the_panel`, `make_and_perform_switch_from_the_chip_and_shift_main`.
- **`power/button`**: the buttons of the power card a short press of the power button opens over any page (power.rs): Cancel, Restart and Shut down; over unsaved work Cancel, Don't save and Save and shut down (or restart); OK when it can't shut down from here. A tap chooses, as the wheel's push does on the outlined one. *Example:* press the power button briefly, tap Cancel: back on the page. *Tests:* `a_short_press_of_the_power_button_opens_the_power_card`, `shut_down_and_restart_go_to_the_systems_file`.
- **`textpad/key`**: a key of the text keyboard that a tap on a name opens over the page (the Sample tab's pad bar title): a letter, digit or symbol types at the cursor; Shift gives the next one its other character; #+= turns the letter keys into more symbols (! @ # $ % ^ & * and the rest) until ABC turns them back; Delete takes off the one before the cursor; Clear empties the name; Space; Cancel closes it and changes nothing; Enter keeps the names typed. A key that can't type now (the name is full) is drawn as a button that can't be used. *Example:* tap the pad bar's title, tap Clear, Shift, K, i, c, k, Enter: the pad is called Kick. *Tests:* `a_tap_on_the_pad_bars_title_names_the_pad_and_its_sound`, the `textpad` unit tests (`the_symbols_key_turns_the_letter_keys_into_symbols_until_abc`).
- **`textpad/field`**: the switch at the text keyboard's top right when there are two names to type (Pad and Sound): tap one to type that name; each keeps what was typed in it, and Enter keeps both. *Test:* `the_switch_picks_which_name_the_keys_type_and_each_keeps_its_own`.
- **`kits/row`**: a kit in the list of kits (from Play's PADS PLAY or the Pads tab's Kit); the synth comes after the kits, then **New kit** while an empty user kit is left (it puts one on the armed track). Tap one to load it on the pads; the list closes and the page comes back. *Example:* tap PADS PLAY on Play, tap Boom Bap: the pads play Boom Bap and PADS PLAY says so. *Tests:* `what_the_pads_play_and_the_pads_tabs_kit_open_the_list_of_kits`, `new_kit_puts_an_empty_kit_on_the_track_and_a_shared_kit_loads_into_one`.
- **`kits-card/button`**: Back on the kit list's card: closes the list without changing the kit (a tab closes it too). *Test:* `what_the_pads_play_and_the_pads_tabs_kit_open_the_list_of_kits`.
- **`master/level`**: the master level bar on the Play page; drag it for the level of everything, the same level as the mixer's master fader. *Example:* drag the bar down to -6 dB, open the mixer: its master fader is at -6 dB too. *Test:* `the_master_bar_and_the_mixer_master_are_one_level`.
- **`qlinks/knob-card`**: the four Q-Link cards in the right-hand column. Each shows what its knob turns on this page; drag a card to turn it from the screen. *Example:* on the Sequencer's Steps, tap a step, then drag the Velocity card: the step plays softer. *Tests:* `one_tap_switches_the_play_view_and_the_q_links_follow_it`, `beats_picks_a_step_for_the_q_links`.
- **`guide/button`**: Skip and Done in the button guide (it walks through the hardware buttons the first time, and from Prefs). Skip moves on to the next button, leaving this one unlearned; Done ends the guide. *Example:* the guide asks for REC and you want it later: tap Skip.

## Play page

- **`play-transport/view`**: the view switch: All and Loop on Play, Steps and Notes on the Sequencer tab (whose Steps are Beats' pages in Play's frame). Tap one; the Q-Links follow the view. On the Sequencer, Notes opens the note editor on the clip the steps show (`EditAction::Open`). *Test:* `one_tap_switches_the_play_view_and_the_q_links_follow_it`.
- **`play-transport/pads`**: what the pads play, on Play's transport before the view switch ("PADS PLAY" over "Track 1 · Classic House · A": the track Play's rows show, its kit and the bank; or the synth's line). A tap opens the list of kits over the page. *Example:* press PAD BANK B: it reads "… · B". *Tests:* `what_the_pads_play_and_the_pads_tabs_kit_open_the_list_of_kits`, `play_names_the_kit_and_the_header_shows_ram`.
- **`play-transport/rec`**: the REC chip between the tempo and what the pads play (on the Sequencer, the bar dots): what REC records (the armed track's clip or the picked loop), grey while armed, outlined red while ready, solid red while recording. A tap opens the target list in place of the view, and closes it ([REC records what is armed](../design/rec-arm.md)). *Example:* open the Loop view: the chip reads "REC → Loop 1". *Tests:* `rec_records_what_is_armed_and_play_starts_a_ready_take`, `arm_pills_and_the_target_list_move_the_arm_and_pin_it`.
- **`rec-list/row`**: a row of the REC chip's target list (the notes, the loop): a tap arms it, pinned or not; on the armed one it says what REC records; SHIFT + a tap arms both (or, both armed, lets go of the one tapped). **`rec-list/switch`**: Rec song (the grid into the song) and Pin the arm (only a tap on Arm moves it). **`rec-list/close`**: closes the list. *Test:* `arm_pills_and_the_target_list_move_the_arm_and_pin_it`.
- **`play-transport/tempo`**: the tempo. Tap it in time, four times or more, to set the tempo (as TAP TEMPO). *Example:* tap along with a record at 92 BPM: the tempo reads 92. *Tests:* `a_tap_on_plays_tempo_taps_it`, `the_tempo_tapped_on_play_sets_the_transport`.
- **`steps/step`**: one step of a drum lane (the Sequencer's Steps, and Play's rows). Tap to turn it on or off, and on the Steps to pick it for the Q-Links (velocity, length, nudge, chance). On Play's rows the tap acts when the finger lifts, and a finger that moves swipes the rows instead (`Step { index: ROWS, delta }`). *Example:* tap Kick steps 1, 5, 9 and 13 and press PLAY: four on the floor. *Tests:* `steps_tapped_on_beats_play_in_time`, `beats_picks_a_step_for_the_q_links`, `play_rows_show_the_bank_in_order_and_a_swipe_moves_them`.
- **`steps/mute`**: a lane's speaker: mutes and unmutes that pad's lane. It is the same mute as the pad's M in the pad mixer. *Test:* `a_speaker_on_play_and_m_in_the_pad_mixer_are_one_mute`.
- **`steps/arm`**: the Arm pill at the left of the pattern's heading: grey "Arm" when REC records the loop (a tap arms the notes), red "Armed", "Ready" or "Rec" when it records them (a tap says so). SHIFT + a tap adds the notes to the loop, so REC records both; with both armed a plain tap keeps only the notes. *Test:* `arm_pills_and_the_target_list_move_the_arm_and_pin_it`, `shift_arm_records_the_notes_and_a_loop_in_one_pass`.
- **`steps/head`**: the pattern's head: ‹ and › turn its pages of 16 steps, - halves the steps (down to 16) and + doubles them (up to 128, the new steps repeating the old). Each length change is one take, so UNDO brings the steps back. Faint when it can't act; a tap then says why. On a synth track's scale rows it also has **Fold** (only the notes the clip uses) and the octave's ‹ and › round "Oct 3" ([step-pads.md](../design/step-pads.md#scale-rows-on-beats-s3)). *Test:* `the_patterns_head_turns_pages_and_doubles_or_halves_its_steps`, `beats_on_a_synth_track_shows_scale_rows_that_fold_and_play`. On Play's rows the head is Play's own ([layout spec](../design/layout-spec.md) section 4, All): **Previous track** and **Next track** (each half of the track chip; `FromUi::Time(TimeAction::Arm(Some(t)))`, Loop tracks skipped), **Bank A** to **Bank H** (`FromUi::PadBank`, the bank on the pads faint), ‹ and › for the page, **Follow** (the page follows the playhead), and **Sequencer tab** (`FromUi::Tab`); no length. *Test:* `play_shows_the_armed_tracks_pads_a_bank_at_a_time`.
- **`steps/note`**: a synth track's scale row's name: its note plays while a finger is on it (`FromUi::Audition`); with ERASE held it clears that note's steps. *Example:* on Beats with the Poly synth in C minor, hold C3's name to hear the root. *Test:* `beats_on_a_synth_track_shows_scale_rows_that_fold_and_play`.
- **`step-inspector/bank`**: the step inspector's Step, Trig and Sound (Beats, under the pattern while a step is picked or held): which of Q-LINK's banks the Q-Links show for that step. Step turns velocity, length, nudge and chance; Trig the condition, ratchet and nudge in ticks; Sound locks the pad's tune, decay, filter and level, and only while the step's pad is held (STEP SEQ). Q-LINK steps them too. *Example:* tap the kick's step 7, tap Trig, turn Q-Link 1 to 1:2: the step plays every second time round. *Test:* `beats_inspects_the_step_picked_and_its_banks_pick_the_q_links`.
- **`step-inspector/close`**: × at the right of the inspector's head, for a step picked on the screen: picks it off and keeps it, so the throws come back and Q-LINK reaches the macro banks again. A step held on the pads has none: it goes when you let go. *Test:* `beats_inspects_the_step_picked_and_its_banks_pick_the_q_links`.
- **`step-inspector/unlock`**: a lock on the step (purple), with its ×: a tap takes that lock off every step held (or the step picked) and keeps the note. UNDO brings it back. *Test:* `the_step_inspector_takes_locks_off_copies_and_clears_a_step`.
- **`step-inspector/copy`**: Copy: the next step tapped on the same lane takes a copy of this one (note, settings and locks); any other tap lets it go. *Example:* tap step 7, Copy, tap step 15: both play the same hit. *Test:* `the_step_inspector_takes_locks_off_copies_and_clears_a_step`.
- **`step-inspector/clear`**: Clear: the step comes out, its locks too. One take, so UNDO brings it back. *Test:* `the_step_inspector_takes_locks_off_copies_and_clears_a_step`.
- **`throws/throw`**: the performance throws (Delay throw, Reverb wash, Stutter, Tape stop). Hold one: the effect plays while you hold it and lets go when you lift. *Example:* hold Tape stop on the last beat of a bar for a slow-down into the next. *Test:* `the_pattern_throws_and_xy_pad_reach_the_engines`.
- **`xy/xy`**: the XY pad (a DJ filter with an echo). Drag across it: across sweeps the filter, up adds echo. *Test:* `the_pattern_throws_and_xy_pad_reach_the_engines`.
- **`play-loop/arm`**: the loop strip's Arm pill, as `steps/arm` for the loop. *Test:* `arm_pills_and_the_target_list_move_the_arm_and_pin_it`, `shift_arm_records_the_notes_and_a_loop_in_one_pass`.
- **`play-loop/button`**: the Loop view's Record, Overdub, Play and Stop for the looper. *Example:* tap Record, play four bars on the pads, tap Overdub to layer on top. *Test:* `the_loop_strips_buttons_carry_their_symbols_and_play_and_stop_the_transport`.
- **`play-loop/layer-mute`**: a loop layer's speaker on the Loop view: mutes that layer. *Test:* `a_loop_layers_speaker_mutes_it`.
- **`levels-panel/button`**: the 16 Levels panel's Pick pad, Close and Off (16 LEVEL opens it on Play). Pick pad lights: hit the pad the levels play. Close hides the panel and leaves 16 Levels on; Off turns them off. *Example:* press 16 LEVEL, tap Pick pad, hit pad 3: all 16 pads play pad 3. *Tests:* `sixteen_level_turns_levels_on_with_the_panel_and_off_again`, `sixteen_levels_make_every_pad_play_the_pad_and_hits_pick_it`.
- **`levels-panel/kind`**: what the 16 levels change (velocity, tune, cutoff, decay, start, transpose); the wheel steps them too, and the lit one is chosen. Transpose (sequencer S4) makes the pads move the armed track's notes live (pad 9 as written), Pick pad faint, and Tune's Q-Link turns it. *Example:* tap Tune: pad 1 plays 8 semitones down, pad 9 as recorded, pad 16 7 up. With the synth on the pads, 16 LEVEL opens Transpose: hit pad 14 and the playing bass line moves up a fourth from its next note. *Tests:* `sixteen_level_turns_levels_on_with_the_panel_and_off_again`, `sixteen_level_on_the_synth_transposes_the_armed_track`.
- **`arp-mini/open`**, **`arp-mini/slower`**, **`arp-mini/faster`**, **`arp-mini/latch`**: the quick band that comes with NOTE REPEAT: the mode chip opens the arp panel, ‹ slower and faster › change the rate, Latch keeps the notes going after you lift. *Example:* hold NOTE REPEAT, hold a pad, tap faster ›: 1/16 becomes 1/32. *Test:* `the_quick_band_comes_with_note_repeat_and_the_chip_opens_the_panel`.

### The arp panel and workspace

- **`arp-modes/segment`**: the mode (Direct, Repeat, Arp, Pattern). Tap one; the panel shows that mode's settings.
- **`arp-list/row`**: a setting of the mode (the arp's direction, octaves, the pattern). Tap to pick it; the wheel and - / + change it.
- **`arp-qlinks/slider`**: Rate, Gate, Swing and Octaves, as sliders (the Q-Links turn the same). Drag one.
- **`arp-steps/step`**: the steps of a pattern. In Pattern, tap a step to edit it; in the other modes they show the notes as they play, and a tap says to pick Pattern.
- **`arp-ws-grid/cell`**: a cell of the pattern workspace: tap to put a note on the step or take it off. *Test:* `the_workspace_edits_by_touch_and_shows_what_the_pads_write`.
- **`arp-ws-tools/button`**: the workspace's Back, Step time, Free time and Undo. *Test:* `the_workspace_edits_by_touch_and_shows_what_the_pads_write`.
- **`pads/pad`**: the workspace's pads (1st note, 2nd note, ...): tap one to write that note into the step. *Test:* `the_workspace_edits_by_touch_and_shows_what_the_pads_write`.

## Pads tab

- **`smp-pads-bar/kit`**: Kit and the kit's name, with a chevron, left of Browse for: opens the list of kits over the page. *Example:* on the Pads tab, tap Kit Breaks, tap Boom Bap: the selected track plays Boom Bap. *Test:* `what_the_pads_play_and_the_pads_tabs_kit_open_the_list_of_kits`.
- **`smp-pads-bar/button`**: Browse for (pad): opens the browser over the page to find a sound for the picked pad; its Load then says Load on (pad). *Example:* pick A5, tap Browse for A5, pick Kick 01.wav, tap Load on A5. *Test:* `the_pads_tab_picks_copies_and_clears_pads_and_undo_takes_it_back`.
- **`smp-pads-map/pad`**: the 16 pads of the bank. Tap a pad to pick it; tap it again to hear it. *Example:* tap A5, look at its Sound and Mode rows, tap A5 again to hear it. *Test:* `the_pads_tab_picks_copies_and_clears_pads_and_undo_takes_it_back`.
- **`smp-pads-kits/track`**: a track on the Pads tab's Kit of row, with the kit it plays. Tap it to arm the track: the pads play its kit. *Example:* tap Track 3 (Boom Bap): the pads play Boom Bap and the chip says so. *Tests:* `the_kit_of_row_shows_the_kit_tracks_and_answers_taps`, `the_pads_tabs_kit_of_row_arms_a_track_and_kit_track_adds_one_with_a_new_kit`.
- **`smp-pads-kits/add`**: + Kit track: a new track at the end, armed, with a new empty kit. *Example:* tap + Kit track, load a sound onto A1, record a bar on the new track. *Tests:* `the_kit_of_row_shows_the_kit_tracks_and_answers_taps`, `the_pads_tabs_kit_of_row_arms_a_track_and_kit_track_adds_one_with_a_new_kit`.
- **`smp-pads-map/bank`**: the kit's banks A to H over the pads, a dot for each pad with a sound, the bank on the pads outlined. Tap a bank to put it on the pads, as PAD BANK does. *Example:* tap B, hit pad 5 to pick B5, copy the kick onto it. *Tests:* `the_bank_strip_shows_each_banks_sounds_and_a_tap_turns_the_bank`, `the_pads_tabs_strip_turns_the_bank_and_its_taps_play_that_bank`.
- **`smp-pads-buttons/button`**: Copy, Paste, Clear and Map to keys. Copy the picked pad, pick another and Paste it there; Clear empties the pad; Map to keys makes the bank one instrument with each pad on its note. Faint buttons say why. *Tests:* `the_pads_tab_picks_copies_and_clears_pads_and_undo_takes_it_back`, `map_to_keys_makes_the_bank_one_instrument_and_the_play_tab_plays_its_keys`.
- **`smp-pads-list/row`**, **`smp-pads-list/row-less`**, **`smp-pads-list/row-more`**: the pad's settings (Sound, Mode, Polyphony, Mute group, Level, Pan, Tune). Tap a row to pick it; ‹ and › step it. A setting at its end says so; Sound on the kit's only sound says to load more. *Example:* pick the hi-hat pad, step Mute group to 1, do the same on the open hat: the closed hat cuts the open one.
- **`smp-pads-list/row-button`**: the Effects row's button: "+ FX", or the effects on the pad's mixer channel. Tap it for the pad's chain in the Mix tab (the picker on an empty chain); ‹ comes back. Pads past the first 16 have no channel of their own and show "—". *Test:* `the_pads_tab_effects_row_opens_the_pads_chain_in_mix`.
- **`smp-pads-sounds/row`**: the kit's sounds. Tap one to choose it for Put.
- **`smp-pads-put/button`**: Put on (pad): puts the chosen sound on the picked pad, whole. If the pad already plays all of it, it says so.

## The browser

BROWSE, the folder icon and the Pads tab's Browse for open it over the page.

- **`browser/place`**: where the list looks: Samples, Projects, Instruments, Patterns, Kits, Recordings, Plugins (MPC plugins, below), Factory, Recent, Favourites and Trash. A picker (a pad's sound, a preset, Open project) shows only its own.
- **`browser/row`**: a file, folder or kit. One tap opens a folder; anything else is picked by the first tap (a sound plays once) and opened or loaded by the second (a project opens, a sound loads onto the pad shown, a kit loads in place of the kit the pads play). *Example:* tap Kits, tap Boom bap, tap it again: the pads play Boom bap. *Tests:* `folders_open_with_one_tap_or_the_push_and_minus_goes_back`, `a_kit_loads_whole_and_plus_shows_its_sounds` (mpfree-browser-ui), `a_kit_saves_as_a_folder_and_loads_back` (mpfree-app gui.rs).
- **`browser/star`**: the star at a row's right end: adds the item to Favourites, or takes it out (lit when it is a favourite). There is no Favourite button. *Example:* tap the star on Kick 01.wav, then Favourites: it is there. *Tests:* `a_tap_on_a_rows_star_toggles_the_favourite`, `taps_and_drags_on_the_widget`.
- **`browser/button`**: for a sound, Load on (pad) (onto the pad picked; a pad hit picks another and the button follows), Next pad hit (onto the next pad you hit) and Audition; for a kit, Load (in place of the kit the pads play); Save kit at the top of Kits (the kit the pads play, saved as a kit folder there). There is no Open button: a tap opens. With nothing picked, a button says to pick something first. *Example:* tap Kick 01.wav, Audition to hear it, Next pad hit, hit pad A1. *Tests:* `load_names_the_pad_picked_and_next_pad_hit_waits_for_a_hit` (mpfree-browser-ui), `load_puts_a_sound_on_the_pad_shown_and_load_to_pad_on_the_next_hit`.
- **`browser/back`**: up a folder.
- **`browser/search`**, **`browser/sort`**: search by name (an on-screen keyboard opens), and the sort order.

## Sample tab

The Sample tab edits the pad hit last, with an editor per tab (Wave, Warp, Chop, Play, Grain, Rec, Auto, FX). Some controls are the same on every editor:

- **`smp-bar/tab`**, **`smp-warp-bar/tab`**, **`smp-chop-bar/tab`**, **`smp-play-bar/tab`**, **`smp-grain-bar/tab`**, **`smp-rec-bar/tab`**, **`smp-auto-bar/tab`**: the editor tabs. Tap one to switch editor; FX opens the chain editor on the pad's own channel. *Tests:* `the_sample_page_edits_the_pad_hit_last_and_sends_one_view_at_a_time`, `the_fx_tab_asks_for_the_chain_editor_on_the_pads_channel`.
- **`smp-bar/name`**, **`smp-warp-bar/name`**, **`smp-chop-bar/name`**, **`smp-play-bar/name`**, **`smp-grain-bar/name`**: the pad bar's swatch, title and subtitle, while the pad has a sound (a dotted line under the title). Tap it to type the pad's name, and its sound's, on the text keyboard. *Example:* tap "Pad A5 · Break 92", type " kick", tap Enter: the title and the Play page's pad say "Break 92 kick"; UNDO brings the old name back. *Test:* `a_tap_on_the_pad_bars_title_names_the_pad_and_its_sound`.
- **`smp-q1/card`**, **`smp-q2/card`**, **`smp-q3/card`**, **`smp-q4/card`**, **`smp-chop-q1/card`**, **`smp-chop-q2/card`**, **`smp-chop-q3/card`**, **`smp-chop-q4/card`**, **`smp-warp-q1/card`**, **`smp-warp-q2/card`**, **`smp-warp-q3/card`**, **`smp-warp-q4/card`**, **`smp-play-q1/card`**, **`smp-play-q2/card`**, **`smp-play-q3/card`**, **`smp-play-q4/card`**, **`smp-grain-q1/card`**, **`smp-grain-q2/card`**, **`smp-grain-q3/card`**, **`smp-grain-q4/card`**: the Q-Link cards of each editor, down the right-hand column as on every page. Tap a card to give the wheel to it; drag it up or down, or turn its knob on the MPC, to change it. A faint card says why (no loop, the filter off, no pin). *Test:* `the_sample_tabs_q_links_sit_in_the_right_hand_column_and_a_drag_turns_one`.
- **`smp-rec-qlinks/knob-card`**, **`smp-auto-qlinks/knob-card`**: the Q-Link column on Rec and Auto-sample, where the Q-Links turn nothing yet: four blank cards, so the frame is the same as on the other editors.

## Looper tab

The Looper tab as an instrument (17 Nov redesign; [the manual](../../user/src/making-music/looping.md#the-looper-tab), layout spec 4a): the deck of 8 loops across the top, the picked loop under it, the Q-Links at the right.

- **`ldeck/ring`**: a loop's ring. A tap is the loop's next step, on the press: an empty loop records from the next bar line (or PLAY), a waiting one cancels, a first pass ends on the next bar line, a loop that plays overdubs, an overdub ends, a muted loop plays (`LoopAct::Tap`). It also picks the loop. Held for 1 s it clears the loop, the arc draining in red (`LoopAct::Clear`); let go sooner and nothing changes. With ERASE held it says a whole loop has no undo yet. *Example:* tap Loop 1's ring, play four bars on the pads, tap it again: it plays; tap once more to overdub. *Tests:* `the_looper_tab_plays_loops_and_works_layers`, `a_ring_shows_the_first_pass_recording_through_its_bars`.
- **`ldeck/loop`**: a loop's name: picks it without starting anything. *Test:* `the_looper_tab_plays_loops_and_works_layers`.
- **`ldeck/loop-mute`**, **`ldeck/loop-level`**: a loop's speaker (mute or unmute it without picking it; a muted loop stays silent through STOP and PLAY) and its level bar (drag, or a finger resting on it gives the wheel; -24 to +6 dB). *Tests:* `the_looper_tab_plays_loops_and_works_layers`, `the_q_links_turn_the_loops_levels_then_the_picked_loop`.
- **`leditor/layer`**, **`leditor/layer-mute`**, **`leditor/layer-solo`**, **`leditor/layer-delete`**: a layer's lane: a tap on the wave picks it, on the name mutes it, S solos it, × deletes the picked layer (on the only layer it clears the loop). A finger resting on a lane gives the wheel its level (SHIFT: pan). A press and hold of 0.4 s lifts the layer as a card (a finger that moves sooner swipes the lanes): the deck's loops that can take it light, empty ones too, and it drops on the one under the lift (`LoopAct::DropLayer`, `FromUi::LoopCopy`): it moves there, or with SHIFT a copy goes; onto an empty loop it becomes a loop of its own. A loop of another length, or a busy one, says why (`LoopAct::NoDrop`). On the picked layer the wheel's push picks it up, a turn points it at the next loop and a push drops it (`Target::moves`; Jay, 14 Nov 12:03Z). ERASE + a lane deletes the layer. *Tests:* `the_looper_tab_plays_loops_and_works_layers`, `a_dragged_layer_goes_onto_a_loop_of_its_length_or_an_empty_one`, `a_layer_dragged_onto_a_loop_moves_or_copies_and_another_length_says_why`.
- **`leditor/layer-map`**: the map of every layer down the lanes' right, when there are more than four: a tap jumps the lanes there.
- **`leditor/input`**, **`leditor/send`**, **`leditor/send-to`**, **`leditor/clear`**: the picked loop's header: the input it records from (a tap steps Pads, Line in, Loops), Send (what to do with the whole loop, in the lanes' place: Show in Clips opens Arrange with the loop's cell in view; To a pad, To an audio track and Save WAV are faint and say they are coming), and Clear (the whole loop). *Test:* `faint_looper_tools_say_why`.
- **`leditor/feedback`**, **`leditor/reverse`**, **`leditor/half`**, **`leditor/double`**, **`leditor/merge`**: a loop that plays: Feedback (Fade old: drag, or the wheel), Reverse, ½, × 2 and Merge layers. Tapped while the loop records, each says why. *Test:* `faint_looper_tools_say_why`.
- **`leditor/undo`**, **`leditor/redo`**, **`leditor/history`**, **`leditor/history-step`**: Undo and Redo for the loop, the history chips between them (a tap opens the undo history in the lanes' place, and closes it), and a step of that history (a tap goes back or forward to just after it).
- **`leditor/nudge`**, **`leditor/trim-start`**, **`leditor/trim-end`**: drag the ruler sideways to nudge the loop by 1/16 notes; drag a grip at either end of the ruler to trim it, on release.
- **`leditor/source`**, **`leditor/monitor`**, **`leditor/bars`**, **`leditor/then`**: an empty loop's recording: Records from (Pads, Line in, Loops: Loops records the other loops as heard), Monitor (the line input through the outputs), Length (Free, 1, 2, 4, 8 bars) and Then (Play or Overdub).
- **`lqlinks/knob-card`**: the looper's Q-Link cards, by bank (Q-LINK steps them): Loops 1-4's levels, Loops 5-8's levels, the picked loop (Feedback, the picked layer's level and pan, the seam fade). *Test:* `the_q_links_turn_the_loops_levels_then_the_picked_loop`.

## Prefs, options and the debug pages

- **`options/row`**, **`options/row-less`**, **`options/row-more`**: a row of Prefs or a page's options (MENU): tap to pick it (on the lift), ‹ › to change it (at once). A finger dragged up or down a long list scrolls it and picks nothing; the view follows the picked row a row at a time, with a scroll bar (`OptionList`, every option list alike). **`options/button`** and every other list's `button`: a button under the rows (the clip card's Save state and Reload). *Tests:* `appearance_switches_the_theme_live`, `a_long_option_list_scrolls_a_row_at_a_time_and_with_a_finger` (mpfree-ui).
- **`controls/row`**: Map controls: a hardware button's row; tap it to learn that button again. **`mapcard/button`**: Stop listening, Skip, Clear.
- **`dtabs/segment`**: the debug pages' tabs (MIDI, Performance, CPU by engine, Processes, ...).
- **`midimon/monitor-source`**, **`midimon/monitor-way`**, **`midimon/monitor-hold`**, **`midimon/monitor-clear`**, **`midimon/monitor-port`**, **`midimon/monitor-light`**: the Dev tab's MIDI page, the I/O view's Monitor in debug mode. Its chips pick sources rather than kinds: the ports' messages, the MPC's own controls (pads, buttons, Q-Links, the wheel), the lights and pad colours MPFree writes, CV out, MPFree's own events (a USB device plugged in or gone, MIDI out stopping), clock, and the keep-alive. In, Out, Hold, Clear, a port's name and a channel light work as in the I/O view. Each line says where its message went, as there. *Example:* a pad seems dead: open Dev › MIDI, hit the pad, and the list shows whether the MPC sent it ("Pad 6 down · vel 87") and whether its light came back. *Test:* `debug_mode_lists_the_mpc_and_mpfrees_events_by_source` (mpfree-midi-ui).
- **`calibrate/mark`**, **`calibrate/button`**: touch calibration: tap each mark; Start again, Exit. *Test:* `calibrate_touch_opens_from_prefs_and_takes_every_finger`.
- **`touchlab/button`**: the touch lab's Exit, Start, Skip. *Test:* `the_touch_lab_opens_from_prefs_and_exit_or_a_page_change_closes_it`.
- **`ttest/area`**, **`ttest/mode`**: the touch test area, and its modes (Free, Targets, Swipes, Clear; a mode that would change nothing is faint).
- **`ttune/row`**, **`ttune/row-minus`**, **`ttune/row-plus`**, **`ttune/action`**: the touch tuning rows (shift and stretch) and Use suggested, Defaults, Save. *Test:* `the_touch_page_tunes_every_touch_and_saves_on_request`.

## Controls the sweep doesn't reach

These show only in a state no sample screen starts in (a picker, a menu, a dialog open). The workflow tests reach some of them.

- **`chain/pick`**, **`chain/cancel`**: the effect picker: tap an effect to add it at its defaults (a hold opens its presets while the finger is still down); Cancel adds nothing. *Test:* `the_effect_pickers_cancel_adds_nothing`.
- **`chain/section`**, **`chain/star`**, **`chain/presets`**, **`chain/preset`**, **`chain/presets-back`**, **`chain/page-prev`**, **`chain/page-next`**: the picker's groups down the left (Favourites, Recent, then each category; the open one is unavailable), an effect's star (favourite or not), its presets line (opens its presets when the finger lifts on it), a preset in that list (Defaults first; adds the effect set that way when the finger lifts on it, so a slip adds nothing), the way back to the effects, and the pages of a long group. *Tests:* `stars_keep_favourites_and_favourites_open_first`, `a_hold_or_the_presets_line_opens_presets_and_a_tap_adds_one`, `a_hold_opens_presets_with_the_finger_down_and_rows_act_on_the_lift`, `a_long_section_pages` (mpfree-chain-ui), `the_sample_tabs_fx_shows_the_pad_then_the_track`.
- **`chain/scope`**: on the Sample tab's FX, the pad's chain or its track's. *Test:* `the_sample_tabs_fx_shows_the_pad_then_the_track`.
- **`chain/depth`**, **`chain/remove-row`**: in the mod matrix rows over the page (opened from the Modulate row): drag a row's bar to set its depth, x removes the row. A touch outside the rows shuts them. *Test:* `an_lfo_on_a_knob_gets_a_row_and_comes_off`.
- **`chain/preset-load`**: a preset in the list the effect's name opens (Defaults, then its own; a family's, the mode's): loads it, all but Mix, Mix curve and Quality, and shuts the list; the one it plays is unavailable. *Test:* `the_effects_name_opens_its_presets` (mpfree-chain-ui).
- **`chain/mode-pick`**: a mode in the list the Mode chip opens: switches to it and shuts the list. *Test:* `the_mode_chip_opens_the_list_of_modes` (mpfree-chain-ui).
- **`macros/editor-done`**, **`macros/editor-range`**, **`macros/editor-curve`**, **`macros/editor-flip`**, **`macros/editor-remove`**: a macro target's editor: its range, curve, flip, remove it, Done.
- **`mixer/menu`**: a strip's menu. **Freeze** records the next pass of the playing loop through the strip's effects and plays that from then on, so its effects (and a track's own instrument) rest and free the CPU; **Thaw** goes back to the live effects, and an edit to what the strip plays thaws it by itself. **Print to a new sample** records the next pass with its effects and their tail into Samples › Prints. While a take waits or records, the menu offers only **Cancel the freeze** (or the print), which changes nothing. *Example:* a song plays and the CPU chip is high: tap Drums, tap Drums again, Freeze; a bar or two later the hint says "Drums: frozen" and the CPU drops. *Tests:* `a_strips_menu_freezes_the_playing_loop_and_thaws_it`, `a_selected_strips_name_opens_its_menu_for_freeze_and_print` (mpfree-mixer-ui), and in mpfree-app's `takes.rs` `freeze_takes_the_pads_loop_then_plays_it_in_place_of_the_synth` and `a_print_is_saved_in_samples_prints`.
- **`mixer/output-choice`**: the output picker's choices.
- **`mixer/row`**, **`mixer/field`**, **`mixer/picker-done`**, **`mixer/picker-choice`**: an I/O section's rows (MIDI, Sync, CV): pick a row, tap a field to change it (a faint field says why), and a field's picker. *Test:* `the_io_view_follows_its_section_and_says_why_a_setting_stays`.
- **`mixer/monitor-kind`**, **`mixer/monitor-way`**, **`mixer/monitor-hold`**, **`mixer/monitor-clear`**, **`mixer/monitor-port`**, **`mixer/monitor-light`**: the I/O view's Monitor section, which lists the MIDI going in and out as it happens. The chips pick which kinds (Notes, CC, Program, Bend & pressure, Clock, SysEx) and ways (In, Out) are listed; Hold stops the list so you can read it, Hold again catches up; Clear empties it. A tap on a port's name lists only that port and way, a tap on one of its channel lights only that channel, and the same tap again lists everything. Each line also says where its message went: the armed track (and the pad its note plays with a kit), MIDI Learn and what it moved, the clock follower, nothing, or "ignored"; a line going out says which track sent it. *Example:* a keyboard plays nothing on a track: open the Monitor, play a key, and the keyboard's in row lights on the channel it sends on, so you know which channel to set. *Test:* `chips_and_lights_pick_what_is_listed`, `the_list_holds_while_scrolled_back_and_follows_at_the_top` and `each_line_says_where_its_message_went` (mpfree-midi-ui).
- **`mixer/monitor-save`**: the Monitor's **Save log**. Keeps the list as it shows now (only what the chips and lights let through), oldest first in columns with a heading, as a new text file on the stick's data partition, `files/midi-logs/midi-log-<date>-<time>.txt`; a second save in the same minute gets `-2`, so nothing is replaced. The line under the screen says which file it wrote. Greyed without a data partition. *Example:* a synth plays a wrong note now and then: tap the keyboard's channel light, play until it happens, tap Save log, and copy the file off the stick to compare. *Test:* `save_log_asks_the_app_and_says_its_answer` (mpfree-midi-ui), `a_saved_log_is_a_new_file_in_the_stores_folder` (mpfree-app).
- **`mixer/monitor-view`**: **List | Now** at the Monitor's top left. Now shows what one port and channel hold this moment instead of the messages: keys down on a keyboard with their velocities, the controls that moved and their values, the clock's timing, bend and pressure, and the notes still on going out. *Example:* a pad sound hangs: tap Now and see whether its key is still down or the sustain pedal is held. *Test:* `the_view_follows_the_newest_port_and_channel_and_says_what_is_held` (mpfree-midi-ui).
- **`mixer/monitor-stream`**, **`mixer/monitor-channel`**: in Now, a port's chip picks the port and way shown, and ‹ › (or the data wheel) the channel; until one is tapped, Now follows whichever sent something last. *Example:* a keyboard and a drum machine both send: tap the drum machine's chip and step to channel 10 to see its pads. *Test:* `the_view_follows_the_newest_port_and_channel_and_says_what_is_held` (mpfree-midi-ui).
- **`mixer/monitor-note-offs`**: **Send note-offs to MIDI out**, under Now's notes still on. Ends only the notes MPFree sent out of the 5-pin jacks and has not ended (the same as STOP's panic does for MIDI out), never an "all notes off" to the whole synth. Greyed while MIDI out is off in the run or no note is on. *Example:* a synth on MIDI out drones after a cable was pulled mid-note: Now says the note has been on for 41 s, stuck, and one tap ends it. *Test:* `the_monitors_note_offs_are_taken_once` (mpfree-app).
- **`browser/filter`**, **`browser/key`**, **`browser/confirm-yes`**, **`browser/confirm-no`**: the browser's type filters, the search keyboard's keys, and a confirm dialog (moving a file to the Trash).
- **`browser/plugins-view`**, **`browser/plugins-filter`**, **`browser/plugins-search`**, **`browser/plugins-sort`**, **`browser/plugins-row`**: BROWSE's **Plugins** place (Open MPC plugins; [design](../design/mpc-plugin-store.md)). The views pick what the list shows: Catalog (the Open MPC catalog's plugins that run on this MPC), Installed, On the stick (release zips copied into `files/plugins/`). The chips under it show All, Instruments or Effects; Search looks in names, makers and tags (an on-screen keyboard opens); the order chip steps Most used (the catalog's download count), Newest, A to Z, favourites always first. A tap on the view or chip already on takes the list back to its top. A tap on a row picks it; on the picked row, the next picture. *Example:* BROWSE, Plugins, Effects, tap Tape Echo, read its CPU line, Install. *Tests:* `the_plugins_place_lists_the_stick_and_deletes_a_zip_on_the_second_tap` (mpfree-app gui.rs) and `orders_filters_and_search` (plugin_page.rs).
- **`browser/plugins-button`**, **`browser/plugins-picture`**: the Plugins place's buttons and the picked plugin's picture. **Install** downloads the catalog's zip over the Ethernet cable, checks it against the catalog's SHA-256 and unpacks it into `files/plugins/`; the plugin is offered at once on the Synth tab (an instrument) or in the chain editor (an effect). **Update** does the same for a newer version, keeping the musician's own files in the folder. **Install from zip** unpacks a zip copied to the stick. **Remove** and **Delete zip** ask again on the button (Tap again to remove); Back takes the question back. **Cancel** stops a download. **Pictures**, or a tap on the picture, steps through the plugin's own screen (each tab drawn as on the MPC, once installed) and the catalog's picture. **Update list** fetches the catalog again. *Example:* Installed, pick Mono Bass with Update 1.3.0 on it, Update, then play it from the Synth tab's Instrument row. *Tests:* `installed_and_older_says_update_and_remove_asks_twice`, `a_zip_on_the_stick_installs_from_the_zip_and_a_strange_one_is_flagged` (plugin_page.rs), `lists_a_zip_on_the_stick_and_refuses_one_that_is_not_a_plugin` (plugin_store.rs).
- **`browser/export`**: the Export panel, which **Export** in BROWSE > Projects opens over the browser. It turns the song into files: tick Mixdown, Stems and MIDI file, pick Stems by (Track, the default: one stem per track, the range playing again for each track that shares a channel, as the row says; or Channel: one per mixer channel in one pass), pick the range (Arrangement, or Bars with - and + for how many), check the File row (24-bit, 16-bit or 32-bit float, and 44.1 or 48 kHz; it starts as Prefs > Export file and Export rate have it, and a pick here is for this export only), then Start. While it runs, Cancel stops it and removes its files; when it is done, Close, or Show in BROWSE to open the new folder in Recordings. *Example:* open a song with an arrangement, BROWSE, Projects, Export, untick MIDI file, Start, then Show in BROWSE once it says Exported. *Test:* `the_export_panel_answers_taps` and `export_asks_the_app_and_its_panel_takes_the_screen` (mpfree-browser-ui, the Stems by and File rows' buttons and picks), `the_format_starts_as_prefs_has_it_and_changes_for_one_export` and `stems_by_track_or_channel_say_how_many_passes` (its `src/export.rs`), and in mpfree-app `the_pass_plays_the_range_then_puts_the_transport_back` and `stems_by_track_replay_the_range_with_each_track_alone`.
- **`smp-grain-cloud/play-as-grains`**: on a pad that doesn't play as grains yet, makes it play as grains.
