# The XY controller: one engine for the XY pad

**Status:** the XY controller plan was approved by the owner on 2026-11-22 and phase 1 is being built: a held setting always lets go ([modulation.md](modulation.md), "Let-gos are never lost"), who holds a setting (the same page, "Who holds a setting"), and this page's engine, [`rust/mpfree-xy`](../../../rust/mpfree-xy), with the Play page's ten ready-made layouts on it. They send exactly what they sent before. Phase 2 (any setting on X and Y: your own layouts) has the owner's go and his approval of its screens (23 Nov 2026 10:34Z, written into the [layout spec](layout-spec.md), section 4o); its engine and sound loop side and its screens are below; a synth's and a pad's own settings, the Arp's, Learn's XY row and the browser's layouts come in its third pull request. Later phases each need their own go: phase 3 records gestures into clips and the song, phase 4 is the full XY page and the XY FX page, phase 5 other controllers, phase 6 notes in key and corners.

## What it is

One model for the XY pad, whatever plays it and whatever it plays. Pure logic: no I/O, no threads, `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, and nothing allocated once a `Compiled` layout or a `Pad` is made, so the sound loop can play it.

- **`XyInput`** (`input.rs`): one shape for every controller's input: the device (`Screen`, `Midi`), which touch on it, a count (`seq`) so an input out of order is known, a time in microseconds (`at`), x and y from 0 to 1, a pressure if the controller knows, and its phase (`Begin`, `Move`, `End`, `Cancel`). Today the mixer builds them from `PlayFx::Xy` with `at` at 0; a gesture's own time arrives with phase 3's recording.
- **`Gesture`** (`gesture.rs`): who owns the pad. The first touch owns it until it ends; another touch on the same device is ignored by its id, never "whichever finger moved last". A `Begin` from another device takes the pad (the newest gesture wins, as for any setting held by hand, [modulation.md](modulation.md)); the gesture that lost it is ignored to its end, and a new one from that device takes the pad back. Only the owner's `End` or `Cancel` lets go. An input older than the last from its device is dropped (the count wraps). Counts what it ignored, took and dropped.
- **`Layout`** (`layout.rs`): what the pad plays. A name; two `Axis` values, across and up, of up to `TARGETS` (8) `Target`s each (a setting's `ParamAddr`, its value at the two ends, a `MapCurve` between: Linear, Exp, Log or S, and `How`: `Set`, the pad decides where it is, with values in the setting's units, or `Add`, the pad pushes it from wherever it is, with pushes in knob travel from -1 to 1); where it rests; what it does when the finger lifts (`Lift::GoBack { snap_ms }` or `Lift::Stay`); an optional gate, a setting that is 1 while a gesture holds the pad and 0 when it lets go (an XY FX's Active); whether Hold starts on; where a touch puts the pad (`Start::Jump`, at the finger, or `Start::FromHere`, moved from where it is by as much as the finger moves); and whether it follows the selected track. `Layout::new` is an empty layout of yours (Go back over 120 ms). `Layout::put` puts a setting on a direction, moving it there from the other one (a setting goes on a layout once), `way_of` and `take_off` find and remove one. `Layout::compile` checks it (too many settings on a way, a value that isn't a number, a rest off the pad, a glide outside 0 to 2 s, a setting on it twice or also its gate, an Add push past the whole travel, each with a message) and resolves it into a `Compiled`.
- **`Compiled`**: the layout as the sound loop plays it: fixed arrays, `Copy`, nothing to look up. `values` gives each setting's value for a place on the pad (across, then up, then the gate); `each` gives each setting with its place (`SLOTS`: 0 to 7 across, 8 to 15 up) and its value, for state the sound loop keeps per setting; `position` reads back where the pad stands from its first setting each way (each curve has an exact inverse; the S-curve's is found by halving); `plays` says whether it plays a setting; `mapped` points every setting through a function without allocating (a layout following the selected track, fixed when a finger lands). `Compiled::new` makes one from slices without allocating, for a layout chosen on the sound loop.
- **`Pad`** (`pad.rs`): where the pad is: under the finger, gliding back to where it rests after a lift (about `snap_ms` to cover 63 % of the way, landing within a hundredth in five times that), or staying. No values here; a `Compiled` turns the place into values.

## In the app

- **The ten ready-made layouts** (`rust/mpfree-app/src/xy_layouts.rs`): Filter + echo (the Perform FX's DJ filter across from -100 to 100, its echo up from 0 to 100, resting in the middle at the bottom and gliding back there) and the nine XY FX (the master's XY FX insert, X across and Y up from 0 to 100, Active the gate, the dot staying where the finger lifts and starting in the middle a quarter of the way up). The settings are named by the insert's slot id on the master, so they follow the insert if it moves. Which of the nine plays is the insert's Effect, set when it is picked (`PlayFx::XyEffect`).
- **The mixer** (`MixHost::xy` in `mixing.rs`) keeps the `Gesture`. The screen's moves come through `play_screen` (`Device::Screen`), a learned XY across and up's through `play` (`Device::Midi`). Each controller's own gesture begins with its first held move and ends with its first not held; the gesture that owns the pad plays its layout's values on the inserts (`set_insert`, as a turn sets them). A move not held outside a gesture is the pad gliding home after a lift: it plays only while no gesture holds the pad. A tap quicker than a period still plays for one period (`xy_late`), and the screen's slot (`xy_slot.rs`) still lets the screen's gesture go if its lift action was lost. `xy_at` (for a learned control that moves one way) reads the pad's place back through the layout. Opening a project clears the gesture.
- **The Play page** (`play_page.rs`) keeps a `Pad` built from the pick's layout (`xy_layouts::pad`), and its widget (`play_widgets.rs`, `XyPad`) lets only the first finger move it: a second finger does nothing until the first lifts.
- **The Perform FX page** (`mpfree-fx-ui`, `perform.rs`) plays the same DJ filter and echo with its own `Pad`; its Hold is `Lift::Stay`. Its view already followed only the first finger.
- **A layout of yours** (phase 2; `xy_play.rs`, `MixHost::xy_yours`) plays through the hand layer instead, so the pad never overwrites a setting's own value. Its **Set** settings are held by the pad's hand (`ModMix::pad_touch`: always a `Touch`, owner `Hand::Pad` in `hand.rs`, beating a lane always and a step's lock while recording); its **Add** settings are pushed by a mod matrix row each from a host source of the pad's own (`mpfree_mod::XY_SOURCES`, one per place, so a layout's sixteen always find one), made when the layout loads and taken out when another takes its place; a row the engine refuses is counted and pushes nothing. When the finger lifts: with **Hold** on nothing lets go until Hold is off; **Go back** lets each Set setting glide back to its own value as it is by then (`ModEvent::LetGo`, over the layout's 0 to 2 s); **Stay** makes each Set setting's value its own, as a turn of its knob (`ModMix::pad_keep`), and hands the turns over for one UNDO step (`MixHost::take_xy_kept`), except while recording, when it lets go instead; an Add setting's push always glides away (over the glide, or 120 ms with Stay). Switching layouts (to another of yours or a ready-made one) and opening a project let go of everything at once, even with Hold on. What a gesture plays is fixed when it begins: a layout that follows the selected track (`Layout::follow`) has its synth settings moved to the selected track's synth (`xy_play::followed`, the track from `MixHost::set_xy_track` each loop) when the finger lands; the rest stays where it is. A learned XY across and up hold a layout of yours while they move and let go `HAND_S` after their last move, having no lift of their own. A synth's and a pad's own settings (`Owner::Inst`, `Owner::Pad`) live outside the mixer, so `ModReach` hands their moves to `xy_own.rs` instead: an `OwnHands` table in the mixer, one entry a setting, with room for a layout's settings twice over, which the sound loop takes each period before the sounds play (`MixHost::take_xy_own`, `OwnHands::apply` with `OwnReach` in main.rs, routed as a clip's lock is: a track's own instrument, else the synth on the pads, a kit pad's own setting on its track's kit). There they gain the hand layer: in `locks.rs` (`Under::press` and `Under::release`: the pad's value plays, the value under the locks is left alone, a step's lock plays over the pad unless a track records, a turn by hand takes the knob from the pad, and nothing times out) and in the sampler engine (`Event::PadHand`: a layer over the pad's own, its live value and its lock, over the lock only while recording, kept through a new view). Go back glides from where the pad left the setting to what is underneath by then (a lock, or its own value; `Synth::pad_beneath`, `Player::pad_beneath`) over the layout's glide, then lets go. Stay sets the setting as a turn of its knob would (the Synth tab's turn; a pad's own setting on its kit by the screen, `ToUi::PadsLearned`), its turn joining the lift's UNDO step (`MixHost::put_xy_own`), and keeps the hand on until the own value has arrived (a pad's comes back with the kit's next view), for 1 s at most. A let-go is a state on the setting's entry, never a message, so it always goes through. Add can't push these settings yet: they don't go through the mod engine (`NO_ADD`). Whether a track records reaches the mixer each loop (`MixHost::set_recording`). The status line's `XY pad:` part says whether a layout of yours plays and counts refused rows and settings it couldn't reach.

- **Your layouts in the song** (phase 2; `xy_song.rs`): `XySong` keeps the song's layouts of yours (each a `Mine`: the `Layout`, its glide, the names of its settings as last seen, so one that went away keeps its name, and the ready-made XY FX effect it was copied from), which one plays, and the waiting banner (`Wait`, with the layout as it was before, for Cancel). It lives in the sound loop (`main.rs`, and the smoke session as main.rs), not the mixer, so a `Look` (`xy_seen` in main.rs: a setting's info, name, place, dot and whether it is a synth's or a pad's own) can read the mixer while an action changes the song; `XySong::act` takes a `FromUi::Xy(XyAction)` and gives back a `Done` (a layout to load, Hold) for `MixHost::xy_done`. Play, New, a copy of a ready-made layout (`xy_layouts::layout`, by `MixHost::xy_ready` on its insert's slot id; an XY FX's copy keeps its gate and sets its effect when it plays), Delete, the names, a setting's range (Set: the knob's travel, 0 to 1; Add: the push, -1 to 1), curve, Set or Add (Add refused on a synth's or a pad's own setting until the mod engine reaches them: `NO_ADD`), take off, Let go, the glide (10 ms steps), A touch, Follow, and the banner: `Wait` raises it, `Join` (SHIFT + a touch while it waits, the setting `desk::learn_addr` would learn) puts the setting on that direction over its whole range, Even, Set, moves it from the other direction, or refuses a button, a switch or a ninth, the banner's faint line saying why; `WaitEnd` keeps or puts the layout back. After each action the loop sends `ToUi::Xy(XyView)`: each layout's tile line and Setup rows (a setting's from and to as Setup shows them, its value texts, why Add is greyed, or why it went away), which plays, Hold, and the banner. The song saves them as the project's `xy` part ([browser.md](browser.md#the-parts)); an open lets go of the layout playing before the mixer changes and loads the song's once its mixer is in (`MixHost::opening`). Stay's UNDO: `kept_turn` makes one `undo::Turns` entry of up to 16 settings from `MixHost::take_xy_kept` each loop (`UndoOn::Xy`, its rows "Stay: Cutoff, Drive, … (n settings)"); UNDO and redo set them as a turn does (a synth's or a pad's own setting through `OwnReach`, as Stay sets it). STOP while stopped turns Hold off.
- **The screens** (phase 2; layout spec section 4o): the list (`xy_list.rs`) has the ready-made tiles, then YOURS · IN THIS SONG with a tile each and + New layout. The full-screen pad's page is `col(xy:*, xy-bar:52)` in the Play frame: `XyBar` (`xy_widgets.rs`) is Hold (greyed on a ready-made layout, which tells why), Let go (a tap switches it; on a ready-made layout a copy becomes yours with the other choice) and Setup (on a ready-made layout, a copy first). Setup (`xy_setup.rs`, `XySetup` on `XY_SETUP_PAGE`) keeps what a finger does as `SetupAct`s that `gui.rs` turns into `XyAction`s; a dragged handle and the glide show at once, and the loop's view replaces them. Its Q-Links: Q1 and Q2 the picked row's ends, Q3 its curve, Q4 the glide (`XySetup::qlink`, `qlink_cards`). The name opens the text keyboard with three fields (`TypeTo::XyNames`). The banner (`XyBanner`) is a float on every page over the hint line and the margins round it (`Ui::set_float`), so no page loses room; while it is up `learnable_at`'s value goes to `XyAction::Join` instead of MIDI Learn. On a layout of yours the Play page's `XyPad` shows its name on the chip, its directions' names on the axes and its tile line under the dot (`PlayPage::set_yours`), and the dot rests where the layout rests and glides back over a fifth of its glide (a time constant) or stays. UNDO on the full-screen pad and Setup is `UndoOn::Xy`.

This replaced `mpfree_fx::XyPad`, the fixed X, Y and Active of the XY FX in `MixHost::play_xy`, and the shared "a finger holds the pad" flag, under which whichever controller moved last played.
