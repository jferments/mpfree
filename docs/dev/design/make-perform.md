# Make and Perform

**Status:** sequencer roadmap S4, parts one to nine (`rust/mpfree-app`, `rust/mpfree-seq`, `rust/mpfree-play`, `rust/mpfree-perform`, `rust/mpfree-time-ui`, `rust/mpfree-ui`, `rust/mpfree-scale`, `rust/mpfree-sampler-ui`, 2026-11-17). Jay's sequencer decisions 4 and 5 (16 Nov 2026): Make and Perform as designed, with mutes landing on the bar in Perform. Design: the sequencer review (mpfree-claude `sequencer/rethink/README.md`, "Make and Perform: the toggle and when mutes land", mockups C and K). Not run on the MPC One yet.

## The rule

Make and Perform change how MPFree answers your hands, never what is in the project or what is playing. Make is for building: everything acts at once, and UNDO is the safety net. Perform is for a stage: changes land on the bar, one stray touch can't delete anything, and the screen shows what comes next.

## The model

- `mpfree_seq::Project::perform` (false for Make). The clips part saves it as a `perform` line before the tracks, and only in Perform, so a Make project's file is as before and an older build skips the line.
- `Project::launch` (sequencer S4's second part) saves as a `launch now|ticks N|bars N` line in the same place, only when it isn't one bar; a clip's own `launch` and `legato` lines go in its body. An older build skips them all.
- `TimeAction::Perform(bool)` sets it on the control half, which says what changed in place of the hint (`perform_line`). Switching changes nothing playing, waiting or recorded, and is not an undo step.
- `TimeState::perform` for the screens, `TimeHost::perform` for the app: the main loop and the smoke session pass it to `App::set_perform` each period, after `set_rolling`.

## What it switches, as built

| | Make | Perform |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The top bar's chip | MAKE | PERFORM, the second accent colour's dot (`Tone::Accent2`) |
| MAIN | Play; on Play, the next view | Arrange's Clips grid; on Arrange, the next view: Clips, Song, the song list (`Effect::ArrangeView`, `ToUi::ArrangeView`) |
| The arm | Moves as [rec-arm.md](rec-arm.md) says | Pinned: `App::set_perform` turns `RecArm::pinned` on when Perform starts and off when Make does, so the target list's Pin the arm still works by hand between switches |
| TRACK MUTE's pads | At once; SHIFT waits for the launch line | Wait for the launch line; SHIFT acts at once (below) |
| Rec song | Off until turned on | On with REC's take (below) |
| Delete and clear buttons | One tap; UNDO brings it back | A half-second hold (Perform's guard, below) |
| The Clips grid | 8 compact rows (`ROW_MIN_H_MAKE`): names and lengths, a small ring | 5 big rows (`ROW_MIN_H`), and a queued cell's countdown (below) |
| The transport bar | As before | NEXT after WAITING (below) |

The switch: a tap on the chip (`header/chip`, the leftmost; `perform_chip` in `gui.rs`) or **SHIFT + MAIN** (`App::mode_button`). Both send `TimeAction::Perform` with the other mode; the chip follows the next `TimeState`, and the hint line says what changed (`perform_line`: "Perform: mutes land on the bar, deletes need a hold, MAIN goes to the Clips grid").

## When mutes land, as built (the third part)

TRACK MUTE's pads (layout spec section 6) mute or solo a track at once or on the launch line:

- **The timing.** `App::mute_tap` gives each tap a `MuteTap`: `wait` is Perform with SHIFT's other timing (`perform != shift`), and `solo` is TRACK MUTE held. `main.rs`'s `mute_key` hands it to `TimeHost::mute_or_solo` (`TimeControl::mute_or_solo`). A tap acts at once when it doesn't wait, when the transport is stopped, or when Launch is at once.
- **The queue.** A tap that waits joins `TimeControl::mute_queue`: an id, and a `MuteWait` with the line (the project's Launch when the queue starts, kept if Launch changes) and each track's wanted mute and solo. A tap that brings a track back to what it has now takes its change back, and an empty queue goes. Each view compiled carries a `MuteCue { silent, line, id }` for each track the queue changes (`PlayView::set_mute_cue`), with its silence worked out after the queue, solo included.
- **Frame-exact landing.** The player (`Player::play_span`) splits a track's span at its mute cue's line (`mute_due`), plays the part before, lands the mute (`land_mute`: a track going silent has its sounding notes end on the line, its hits, touch and locks let go) and plays the rest. It reports `MuteLanded { track, silent, at, id }` (`ToControl::MuteLanded`, sent by `TimeAudio` after the started cues). Its `muted` holds a landed cue while the view has the same id; where a scene's cue mute also switches the track, the later tick wins.
- **The control half** applies the whole queue on the first landing with its id (`land_queue_now`) and compiles. Rec song takes each landing as a silence (`SongRecorder::mute`, a gap in the placements), and a Loop track's loops go on or off on the landing tick (`loop_at`, as a cue's do). **STOP lands whatever waits**: a queue with the transport stopped lands in the next period.
- **Solo.** `Track::solo`, saved as a `solo` line after `mute` when on. `Project::silent(t)` is the one rule (muted, or another track soloed and not this one), used by the compile, the Loop tracks (`loops::playing`), song mode's free tracks and Rec song. A scene's cue mutes are made solo-aware in `TimeControl::view` (a track not soloed stays silent while any track is).
- **The screens.** `TimeState::mute_wait` carries the queue; `mute_waiting(t)` gives the grid header's line ("mutes in 2 beats", rounded up to beats under a bar and bars past one), and `waiting_reading` the transport bar's WAITING item ("Hats, Vox at 18.1"), which goes first in `bar_of`. `GridTrack` has `solo` (outlined in the accent) and `waiting` (the header hatched, `widgets::hatch`, and outlined in amber, its line shortened to fit by `waiting_fit`); `mute` is the track's silence. Sample screens: `clips-mutes` (Perform, two changes waiting) and `clips-solo`.
- **The pad lights** (`mute_colours`, from `TimeHost::mute_light`'s `MuteLight`): amber blinking twice a second while waiting, the hit of blue while soloed, red while silent (muted or by solo), green lit while a clip plays, green with nothing launched. Akai's app was never seen sending a dim red, so silent by solo is the muted red.
- **Hold for solo** (`App::mute_hold`): TRACK MUTE pressed without SHIFT toggles the mode as before and remembers the mode it had. A pad tapped while it is held solos (turning the mode to Tracks for the lights if the press had turned it off), and the release puts the mode back (`track_mute_up`). `App::pads_mute` routes pads to the mute keys while the mode is on or TRACK MUTE is held.
- **The kit's pads** (SHIFT + TRACK MUTE: kit 1's, whose mutes are the mixer's pad strips) take the same timing and the same queue. `mute_key` hands `TimeHost::pad_mute_or_solo` the pad's mute and solo from the mixer (`MixHost::pad_mutes`, `pad_solos`); it acts at once (`PadTap::Now`, which `mute_key` plays as `PlayFx::PadMute` and `PlayFx::PadSolo`) or puts the change in the queue's `MuteWait::pads` (`PadTap::Waits`), sharing the line with the tracks'. The mixer has no line to wait for, so the control half lands the pads in the period before theirs (`pads_due`: the line within the next period's ticks, or at once once stopped) into `take_pad_landings`, and `land_pads` gives them to the mixer after it has mixed the period: it has them from the period that holds the line, or the one before when the line falls within a tick of that period's start (the period's ticks round up): never after the line, and at most a period and a tick before it (about 3 ms at 128 frames and 120 BPM), as Reload clip lands. The log says "pad mute: at 2.1, pad 3 muted". A track on another kit has no pad mutes, as before.
- **Pad solo.** TRACK MUTE held over the kit's pads (`MuteHold::before` is Pads) solos a pad and leaves the pads the kit's. The mixer's `pad_solos` silences every other pad through its strip's mute (`solo_silent`, beside the pads' own mutes and Drop drums'); the speakers and UNDO keep reading each pad's own mute. It saves as a `padsolos` line in the mixer's part. The lights (`pad_light`): the hit of blue while soloed, red while muted or silent by a solo, amber blinking while waiting. WAITING names a pad by number ("Pad 3 at 9.1").
- **Not undo steps.** A mute or solo changes no take, as before.
- **Not built yet:** during count-in a change lands with the queue's line as the player gives it (the first bar line after it), not checked on the MPC. A tap taking a change back in the period between its landing and the landing's report acts at once.

## Rec song with REC, as built (the fourth part)

The design's "Rec song is on with REC in Perform":

- **The take.** The host says each period whether a take records (`rec_arm::Now::taking`: the notes, their count-in, or a loop's first pass or overdub; `desk::rec_now`), through `TimeHost::take_follows`, called by the main loop and the smoke session after `App::set_perform`. In Perform, a take starting with Rec song off turns it on (`SongTake::Armed`, "rec song: on with REC (Perform)"); the take ending turns it off and keeps what it recorded in the song ("rec song: off with the take, 3 placements ..."). Rec song on by hand isn't touched.
- **"+ Rec song".** `TimeState::song_with_take` says the take recording, or the next, brings it; the REC chip's `what` then ends "+ Rec song" (`Gui::rec_chip`, in Perform with Rec song on as well). The target list's switch reads **Rec song with REC** in Perform (`RecList::perform`), and `gui::rec_song_switch` picks what a tap does: `TimeAction::RecSongWithTake(false)` takes it off for the take recording or the next (`SongTake::Off`; a take's Rec song stops there and keeps what it had), `RecSongWithTake(true)` puts it back (arming it at once during a take), and Rec song on by hand goes off as before. `RecSong(false)` during a take that turned it on is off for that take too. After the take, `Off` goes back to `Idle`.
- **Make** is as before: a take changes nothing, and `RecSongWithTake` is refused.

## Perform's guard and big state, as built (the fourth part)

- **The guard.** In Perform a finger down on a button that deletes or clears is kept from it (`Gui::watch_guard`, before MIDI Learn and the widgets): `guarded(widget, target)` lists the Song view's Delete and an audio region's Erase, the note editor's Delete, the Looper tab's Clear, a layer's × and ½, the step inspector's Clear (a ring's clear on Play's loop row is a second's hold already) and the song list's Remove. The hint line says "Hold to delete" (`GUARD_HINT`), and `Ui::set_hold_fill` draws a red fill (the theme's `bad`, part see-through) crossing the button from the left, drawing that widget again whole as it moves. `Gui::guard_tick`, from `tick`, fills it over `GUARD_HOLD` (0.5 s), then presses the button: the touch's Down and Up go to the widget where the finger landed, as a tap would, and the rest of that finger's touch is the guard's (`guard_done`). Lifted early, or slid more than 20 px off the button, nothing happens and the hint says to hold. The wheel's push and ERASE + a tap are not guarded. The design's Delete clip has no on-screen button (ERASE + a clip), and its Halve is the clip card's SHIFT + ‹, two hands.
- **The grid's rows.** `ClipGrid::big` (set from `TimeState::perform` by `TimePage::update`, before it counts the rows in view, keeping the first row within the slots): Perform's rows are at least `ROW_MIN_H` (76 px, 5 on the MPC's screen), Make's `ROW_MIN_H_MAKE` (44 px, 8). A cell under `COMPACT_H` (64 px) is drawn compact (`paint_compact`): its name, then its length, or its foot while queued ("from 7.1", amber), or a loop's wave; a playing clip's ring is `SMALL_RING` (18 px) at its right.
- **The countdown.** In Perform, while the transport rolls, a queued cell's `GridCell::progress` is how near its line is over its launch grid's span (`TimeState::queued_foot`; a launch with no grid, a bar), at least 1/64 so it shows from the tap; `paint_cell` draws it as an amber bar along the cell's foot. The foot still says where it starts ("from 6.1", as mockup C).
- **NEXT.** `TimeState::next_reading` (Perform, rolling): a scene cued (its name, at its cues' line or the project's Launch), else the cues (the first clip's name or "Bass stops", "+N" for more, at the soonest line), else `TimeState::follow_next` (the scene playing's follow action: `Perform::scene_follow` gives its row, point and follow while no scene is cued; `follow_words` names the row a Next, Previous, First or Last plays with `follow_target`, "row 1 again", "Stop all", "Any scene", "Another scene", "Drop or stay" with a chance between, nothing for Stay), else in song mode the next marker. Each says how soon with `countdown` (beats rounded up under a bar, then bars; the mute header's words too). `bar_of` puts it after WAITING with `Tone::Accent`, and the transport bar cuts its first reading to fit rather than leaving it out.

## Back to song and Reload clip, as built (the fifth part)

- **Back to song.** `TimeAction::BackToSong`, from the grid's button (`ClipGrid::back_to_song`, `BACK_TO_SONG`, shown while `TimeState::over_song`: song mode on and a clip launched or cued). `TimeControl::act` takes it with Stop all: `Perform::stop_all` cues every busy track's stop on the launch line (`When::Cued`) or clears the launches at once while stopped, and song mode goes on; as each stop lands its track is free and `PlayView::set_song` plays the song's blocks there. A compile that fails puts song mode back with the rest. Works the same in Make and Perform.
- **Save state.** `TimeAction::SaveState` keeps a copy of the clip in `TimeControl::saved` (track, slot, `Clip`), one a slot, in place of an earlier one; not for a loop's cell. It lives while the project is open: a project opened makes a new `TimeControl`. `forget_saved` drops a slot's state with ERASE + the clip, with a drop on the grid (both slots) and with a row moved or copied (all of them).
- **Reload.** `TimeAction::Reload`: with no state it says so; the same clip as saved, nothing; while recording it refuses (the take's notes would land in a clip that is gone). While the clip is launched or cued, the transport rolls and its launch grid (`Clip::launch`, else `Project::launch`) is not at once, it waits as a `ReloadWait` for `LaunchGrid::next(position, bar)`, unless that line falls inside the next period. `TimeControl::period` calls `reloads_due` with the period's frames: a wait whose line is within the next period's ticks (`period_ticks`: `ticks_in` at the tempo, rounded up), or every wait when the transport stops or jumps back (a loop, a locate), goes in, logged later as "reload: Verse back as saved on bar 5" or "(STOP)"; one due while recording is dropped and says so. The view compiled at the end of that period plays the saved state from the period's start, so it lands within one period before the line (2.9 ms at 128 frames), and the notes on the line are the saved ones. Asked again while it waits, it is taken back.
- **The edit.** `Takes::reload_clip` swaps the clip as a take of its own (`Take::reloaded`, the clip it replaced in `removed`, the saved state in `made`), so UNDO puts back the clip as it was and REDO the saved state, like any take. The takes list calls it "Reload 3 · saved state".
- **On screen.** The clip card's buttons are `OptionList::buttons` (`OptionButton`: label, enabled, waiting), under the rows, giving `Toggle(i)`; `card_buttons` makes them. `MorphBar` has a fourth button, Reload clip (`Tab(3)`, `reload` and `reload_waiting`). NEXT names a waiting reload after the clips cued ("Verse reloads in 2 beats").

## The song list, as built (the sixth part)

The review's "song list for MPC thinkers" (mockup D): Arrange's third view. The review drew it as writing the scenes' follow actions; it is its own engine instead, since a scene can come twice in a list ("Drop, Break, Drop") and a row's follow action names one next row.

- **The model.** `mpfree_seq::SongList` (`scenes.rs`) in `Arrangement::list`: `steps` (`ListStep { row, times }`, at most `MAX_LIST_STEPS` 128, times 1 to `MAX_TIMES` 99) and `looped`; `SongList::check` (from `Arrangement::check`, `BadScene`) and `after(step)` (the next step, the first again when looped, else None). Being in the arrangement, every list edit is a Song view edit for undo (`Takes::song_edit` with `SongChange::List` or `ListToSong`), and a row drop moves the steps with their rows (`takes/drops.rs` `follow_rows`, as the morph's rows). Saved as `list <row> <times>` lines and `listloop` after the morphs (`save.rs`).
- **Its spans.** `arrange::list_spans(project, bar)`: each step's start from 0 and length, its scene's length (`scene_length`: the follow's bars, else the longest clip in whole bars) times its times. `list_to_song(project, at, bar)` lays the steps with `scenes_to_song`, a scene that comes twice twice.
- **The engine.** `Perform::play_list(project, step, when, ..)` launches the step's scene as `launch_scene` does and keeps `ListPlaying { step, start, out }`: the line its scene starts on (set by `started` for a cued launch), and while it waits the step playing out with its start. `Perform::due` calls `list_due` first: in the bar before the step's end (start plus its span) the next step's scene is cued for that line (the next bar line if the check comes late), with every cue's line set to a bar so the project's Launch doesn't push it; after the last step, `stop_all` on the line. An empty row with nothing to cue becomes a silent step from its line. While the list plays, `scene_follow` and the scenes' follow actions wait. `stop_all` ends it; the host ends it (`end_list`) before a scene launched by hand; `list_edited(at, inserted)` keeps the step playing in place as steps go in or out (taking it out ends the list). `list_pass(now)` gives the step sounding, its pass and how far through the step; `list_waiting(now)` the step waiting; `list_next` the step after and its line, for NEXT.
- **The control.** `TimeControl` keeps `list_view` and `list_pick`; `TimeAction::ListView`, `ListPick`, `ListPlay` (with `LaunchScene`'s timing: on the launch line while rolling, at once on the bar while stopped), and the edits `ListTimes`, `ListScene` (steps through the rows with a clip, `next_filled`), `ListAdd` (after the pick, the next row with a clip after its scene, once), `ListRemove`, `ListLoop`, `ListToSong` (onto the song's end, bar-aligned) in `edit_list`. `state()` fills `TimeState::list` (`ListRow`: the scene's name or "Row 3", row, times, bars, starts, playing, waiting), `list_pick`, `list_looped` and `list_next`.
- **On screen.** `mpfree_time_ui::list::SongListView` on `LIST_LAYOUT` / `LIST_CARD_LAYOUT` (`song-list:*` in the grid's and morph's place). Its touches: `Step { index: k }` times, `Step { index: SCENE_STEP + k }` scene, `Select(k)` a pick on the lift (a drag scrolls), `Toggle(LIST_*)` for the loop switch, Clips, Song, the add row and the three buttons. The pick scrolls into view, the last step bringing the add row. The Song view's List button is `Tab(song::LIST)`. MAIN in Perform cycles Clips, Song, List (`ToUi::ArrangeView(None)`). Remove is guarded in Perform. `list_title` counts steps, bars and the time at the tempo. NEXT names the next step after a reload and before a follow action. Sample screen `arrange-list`.

## The scene editor, as built (the seventh part)

The review's scene settings (mockup D's side card: Rename, Mutes in this scene) and the follow actions, which a saved project could hold but no screen set. Layout spec section 4l.

- **The model.** `mpfree_seq::SceneSettings { name, mutes, follow, programs }` (`scenes.rs`; `programs` from S5, below), what a scene's editor sets: `Scene::settings()` and `Scene::set_settings`, with `SceneSettings::check` (the follow's, the mutes within `MAX_TRACKS`, a name of at most `MAX_SCENE_NAME` 24 characters with no control characters; `BadScene`).
- **Undo.** `Takes::set_scene(project, row, to, join)` (`mpfree-play` `takes.rs`) keeps the scene's settings before and after (`SceneEdit` on `Take::scene`); a call with the same `join` on the same row, with nothing undone, joins the last take, so the wheel through a row is one undo. UNDO and REDO put a side back only while the scene is as the take left it. Putting a scene back to plain trims the plain rows at the end of `Project::scenes`, so a project saves as before.
- **The control.** `TimeControl::scene_pick` (set by `SceneFocus`, `LaunchScene`, `ListPick`, and a `SceneSettings`); `state()` gives `TimeState::scene_focus` (the pick, else the scene playing, else 0) and `scene` (its settings). `TimeAction::SceneSettings { row, card_row, settings }` joins with `SCENE_JOIN + card_row`, compiles, and says the row's new value ("scene 2 · Follow Next (UNDO takes it back)", "scene 2 · named Drop"). `TakeRow::scene` names the take "Scene 7 · row 2".
- **On screen.** `SCENE_CARD`, an `OptionList` with the Record panel's tabs (tab 2), on `CLIPS_SCENE_LAYOUT`, `SONG_SCENE_LAYOUT` and `LIST_SCENE_LAYOUT` (`scene-card:*` in place of `record:*`); `TimePage::scene_card` beside `clip_card`. `OptionList::tab_widths` gives the first two tabs their widths (`PANEL_TAB_WIDTHS`, 66 and 104 px), the last taking the rest. `scene_rows`, `scene_step` (`Step` on the rows: the Scene row's `SceneFocus`, Follow through `FOLLOWS`, After, Chance in tens, Or through `OTHERS` below 100 %, Mutes none ↔ as they are, and S5's rows below) and `scene_buttons`; `Toggle(SCENE_MUTES_NOW)` gives the mutes as they are. Rename (`Toggle(SCENE_RENAME)`) is the app's: `Gui` opens the text keyboard with `TypeTo::SceneName(row)`, and Enter sends `SceneSettings` with `card_row: SCENE_NAME`. `SCENE_HINT` while the tab shows on the grid. Sample screen `clips-scene`.
- **Program changes** (sequencer S5, [scenes.md](scenes.md)). Three more rows: **Program** (`SCENE_PROGRAM`) and **Bank** (`SCENE_BANK`), the program change the scene sends the armed track (as the Record tab's Transpose and Follows rows are the armed track's), shown and stepped as the clip card's (`program_name`, `program_next`, `bank_next`, the last two shared with the clip card's Bank), "—" with no track armed; and **Sent to**, the tracks the scene sends one, named as Mutes names them (`programs_words`), which only says why when stepped. `scene_action(st, index, delta, shift)` is the step with SHIFT: Bank by a whole CC 0, as on the clip card (`Gui` sends SHIFT + the scene card's arrows there). The control says the row's value as for the others ("scene 2 · Program 12"). Sample screen `clips-scene-program` (the card scrolled to them).

## One-shot clips, as built (the eighth part)

The review's third launch mode (restart, legato, one-shot; the clip card's Mode row, layout spec 4j).

- **The model.** `Clip::once` (and `ClipSettings::once`), saved as a `once` line in the clip. Legato and once are separate flags; the card's Mode row (`MODES`, `mode_of`) makes them one choice of three.
- **The stop.** `Perform::due` cues a one-shot's stop (`Cue { slot: None }`) in the bar before the end of the pass its launch started in (`once_end`: passes counted from the launch's origin, so a legato one-shot ends with the pass it took over), on that tick exactly: `LaunchGrid::At(tick)`, a cue line that is never a setting (`LaunchGrid::ok` refuses it) and whose `next` is the tick, or at once when past. The player lands it as any stop: the track's notes end there, and in song mode the track goes back to the song. The clip's follow waits; a cue already waiting on the track wins, as for a follow. While stopped nothing is cued; the stop is cued once the transport rolls.

## Live transpose, as built (the ninth part)

Jay's card of 17 Nov, "16 Levels gets a Transpose kind", with the changes he agreed to (21:43Z): the pads step In key when they play In key, the Record tab's row and the Q-Link reach two octaves, and a track can follow another's transpose.

- **The shift.** `mpfree_scale::Shift { by, key }` (`shift.rs`): `apply(note)` moves a note `by` semitones, or with a key `by` notes of that scale going up (both ways, so a melodic minor moves as it rises), a note outside the key moving with the key note below it and keeping its distance; None off the keyboard, so the note is left out. Within `MAX_SHIFT` 24 either way. `Held` remembers what each note started by number became, counted, so each of a note's ends ends one start whatever the shift is by then (256 bytes, nothing allocated).
- **The model.** `Track::transpose` (`Transpose { by, in_key }`, within `MAX_TRANSPOSE` 24, `ModelError::BadTranspose`) and `Track::follows` ("Follows transpose"), saved as `transpose <by>[ key]` and `follows` lines in the track (`save.rs`). It is a performance setting: no clip changes, and 0 plays as written.
- **The control.** `TimeAction::Transpose { track, to }` sets the track and every track that follows to `to` ("transpose: Bass +5 st, with Keys"); `TimeAction::FollowsTranspose { track, on }`. `Tracks::of` gives each track's `TrackSet::transpose` to the audio half, and `TimeHost::transposes()` hands them to the pads each period.
- **Where notes move.** In `SeqPads` (`main.rs`), after the pad layout, where each track's pads become notes: `SeqPads::shift(track)` is the track's transpose as a `Shift`, with the synth's key (`Synth::transpose_key`: its scale while the pads play In key or chords) when it moves in key, and none for a track that plays a kit (`TrackKits`: only a track that plays the synth, or its own instrument, plays notes), the Drums engine or loops. A clip's note-on on the synth is started by the pads' track-first `hit_at` with `Synth::note_on_shifted`, kept under its track (`synth::Player::Track`, so two tracks on one pad never end each other's notes); on a track's own instrument by `Instruments::pad_on_shifted`; on MIDI Out, CV and an export's tap as the pad's notes moved (`shifted`). Each remembers what a pad started, so a note-off and pressure reach those notes: **a note already sounding keeps its pitch** and the next note moves. The arps' notes by number (a synth pad layout) go through each track's `Held` (`SeqPads::sounding`), sounding where the shift took them and recording as written, so a take plays back as it was heard; one the shift takes off the keyboard sounds nowhere and still records (`live_unheard`). A panic clears them.
- **The pads.** 16 Levels' kinds gain Transpose (`levels_panel::Kind`, the column's last), and with the synth on the pads 16 LEVEL opens it at once (a kit's levels can't be of a synth). `App::levels_shown(on, open, transpose)` and `App::pads_transpose`; while it is the kind, a pad is `transpose_pad` (`main.rs`): the armed track to `Levels::semitones(pad)` (-8 to +7, pad 9 as written), in key when the synth has a key, latched until another pad. A track that plays no notes stays and the log says why. The pads light the step the armed track is on (`transpose_colours`). The panel heading names the track, the tracks that follow it and where it is ("Bass, with Keys · +5 st", "-3 in C Minor"); Pick pad is faint.
- **The Record tab.** Rows 9 and 10, Transpose and Follows, for the armed track (`TRANSPOSE_ROW`, `FOLLOWS_ROW`, `transpose_step`): – / + a semitone or a note of the key, within 24; "–" with none armed or a track that plays a kit.
- **The Q-Link.** On Play, while 16 Levels shows Transpose, Tune's Q-Link is Transpose (`Knob::Transpose`, `PlayPage::transpose`), the armed track's, two octaves either way.
