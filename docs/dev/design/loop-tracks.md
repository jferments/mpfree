# Loop tracks: loops in the Clips grid

**Status:** sequencer roadmap S2, Jay's sequencer decision 3 (16 Nov 2026, "Loop tracks in the Clips grid, as designed"). Parts 1 to 5 are built: the looper launches a loop on or off from a bar line, on the frame it falls on (`rust/mpfree-looper`); the model, the grid's loop launches, recorded loops landing on Loop tracks and saving (`rust/mpfree-seq/src/loops.rs`, the time engine, the app); the grid drawing Loop tracks, with loops named by their place; recording a loop into the cell tapped; song mode playing the Loop tracks' blocks; and the same loop in more than one row, with a copy onto another Loop track as a new loop (2026-11-17; a block's own origin and Print to audio track are still to come). Design: the sequencer review (mpfree-claude `sequencer/rethink/README.md`, "Loop tracks: loops in the grid", mockup J).

## The rule

**A loop is a clip on a Loop track.** It launches, stops, sits in scenes and goes into the song like any clip, while its audio, layers, edits and undo stay in the looper ([looper.md](looper.md)). Nothing moves audio: the grid holds references to the looper's loops.

The grid plays one clip per track at a time, so loops sit on Loop tracks, as audio clips sit on Live's audio tracks: one loop plays per Loop track, and loops on different Loop tracks play together, as they do in the looper today.

## The model (part 2)

- **A track's kind.** `mpfree_seq::Track::kind` is `TrackKind::Notes` (the tracks of before) or `TrackKind::Loops(input)`, a Loop track with what its new loops record (`LoopInput`: Pads + synth, the looper's source today; Line in; Resample, the other loops as heard; part 4 records from it). `Track::new_loops` makes one; its `dest` is unused.
- **A loop cell is a clip** whose `Clip::looped` names a looper loop (0 to `LOOPS - 1`, 8) and which has no notes, locks, pressure or moves (`Clip::new_loop`: "Loop 2", as long as its loop). `Project::check` keeps loop cells on Loop tracks and note clips off them (`ModelError::BadLoop`). Because a cell is a clip, it moves with every edit that moves clips (drops, rows, undo), and the launches, cues, scenes, follow actions, Stop and Stop all already work on it. A loop may sit in more than one row of its Loop track (part 6 builds on it); a copy onto another Loop track is a new loop (part 6).
- **Which loops play** (`loops::playing`): a loop plays while one of its cells is launched on a Loop track that isn't muted; a loop in no cell plays, as every loop did before. The time engine gives it as a plan each period (`TimeControl::loop_plan`, `LoopPlan`): each loop on or off, and the bar line a change lands on, which is the song tick of the cue that started on the loop's Loop track in this period (`Started::at`), or none (at once: a launch while stopped, TRACK MUTE, a cell emptied).
- **The looper follows the plan** (`LoopHost::follow_plan`): before its period (the time engine's period runs first in the main loop and the smoke session), each loop not where the plan has it is launched (`Looper::launch`), so a change from a cue lands on the cue's own frame, in the same period the clips switch. A loop already cued the same way, or empty, is left; a full command queue tries again next period. Nothing allocates unless a launch is made.
- **A loop recorded from Play's strip or the Looper tab** (`TimeControl::loops_are`, fed `LoopHost::grid_now` each period): when a loop gets a first pass (or an open brings one) and is in no cell, it lands on the first Loop track with nothing playing or cued whose cell in the row of the scene playing (row 1 if none) is empty, or on a new Loop track at the end ("Loops A", "Loops B" ...: `loops::place`), and is launched at once, since it plays already. So loops recorded one after another still play together. A loop's cells follow its length (a first pass, Half, Double, Trim) and empty when it is cleared or its first pass undone; a redo of that pass (the same length from the same place) goes back to the cell it left, if that is free and its Loop track plays nothing. None of this is an undo step: the looper's undo takes a loop back, and its cell goes with it. While an open's loops go out and come in, the grid waits (`grid_now` is None), so the old loops fading out don't empty the new project's cells. A free loop (Sync: Free, [looper.md](looper.md#free-loops)) never lands: `grid_now` tells it as having no first pass, so it stays in no cell, the plan leaves it on, and the looper alone says when it plays (its bars are the free clock's, not the song's, so cues and blocks on the song's bar lines would be wrong for it). Audio tracks' phase 4b gives every loop a cell.
- **The pads never play a Loop track**: `TimeAction::Arm` of one keeps the armed track, `NewClip` on one says where to record a loop, a drop between a Loop track and a note track is refused, and PLAY with only Loop tracks launched still starts the Play page's pattern. The mixer has no strip per track, so nothing changes there; the Loop tracks share today's Loop strip. The Clips grid draws them as part 3 says.
- **Saved projects** (`mpfree_seq::save`): a Loop track has a `loops <input>` line and each cell a `loop <n>` line; a build before Loop tracks skips both. A project saved before Loop tracks opens with each loop on a Loop track of its own, in row 1, all playing as before (they land as recorded loops do). A project saved with Loop tracks opens with nothing launched, as its clips do. A new project still has no Loop track: the first loop recorded adds Loops A, and SHIFT + "+ Track" adds an empty one (part 4).
- **The looper still holds the audio:** 8 loops in a project, one memory.

## Building it

1. **Launches in the looper** (built). `Looper::launch(n, on, at)`: on or off from a bar line's frame, or at once, fading as a mute does; an off loop keeps its place in the song, so a launch brings it back in step. `Status::on` and `Status::cue`.
2. **The model and launching** (built): the track kind, loop cells, the time engine's loop plan followed by the looper, loops recorded landing on Loop tracks, saving and opening (with the old projects' loops each on a Loop track). The Clips grid shows a loop cell as a clip named after its loop.
3. **The Clips grid** (built). A Loop track's header has a wave mark before its input, in the grid's short words (`LoopInput::short`: "Pads", "Line in", "Resample"; the column is 86 px). A loop cell (`GridCell::wave`) draws the loop's small wave under its name, in the detail's place, with the detail ("2 bars", "overdub", "until 9.1") or the foot ("from 5.1") under it; playing, the wave lights up to the playhead with a line there, in place of the clip's ring, and it is red while the loop overdubs. The small waves (`CELL_WAVE`, 24 columns, each the peak of every layer's frames there, 0 to 255) are made by `LoopHost::wave_step` on the loop wave's own budget, a loop at a time, whenever a loop's frames, layers or `Status::version` change and the picked loop's wave is done (a loop recording or overdubbing keeps the one it had); `LooperView::cell_waves` carries them, `LoopsInfo` gives them to the grid. With Loop tracks the grid's title is the gauge, "Loops 4 of 8 · memory 38 %" (the pager takes the legend's place past 8 tracks, so it can't go there). `view::loop_places` names each loop by its place, from the first row it is in: "Loops B · Loop 2 · Verse" (the row's scene name, or "row 2"), as Play's strip heading, the Looper tab's editor title, the undo history and the REC chip say it; the Looper tab's list says "Loops B · 2 bars" (its layers are in the editor). The Looper tab's To a clip is **Show in Clips** (`USE_SHOW`), lit while the loop is in a cell: the Arrange tab, out of the Song view, with the cell's page of tracks and row in view (`TimePage::show_cell`).
   Moved to part 4, where they are of use: + Track making a Loop track (an empty Loop track only takes the next loop recorded until a cell can record). The layers are not on the cell: at 86 px there is room for the wave and one line.
4. **Recording from the grid** (built; the REC model, [rec-arm.md](rec-arm.md)): a tap on an empty Loop cell arms it ("REC → Loops C · Drop"); REC readies it, PLAY (stopped) or the next bar line (playing) starts the first pass, REC again closes it on the bar line, and it plays. OVERDUB adds layers to the loop playing on the armed Loop track. With 8 loops in use or the memory full, REC says why. A new loop records its Loop track's input, never the other loops unless that input is Resample.
   As built: SHIFT + "+ Track" adds an empty Loop track at the end (`TimeAction::AddLoopTrack`, `Takes::add_track_as`; an edit UNDO takes away; the pads stay on their track), and a tap on a Loop track's sound line steps what its new loops record, Pads, Line in, Resample (`TimeAction::LoopInput`). A tap on an empty Loop cell is `TimeAction::LoopCell`: the time engine keeps the cell (`TimeControl::loop_cell`, while it is still an empty Loop cell; a second tap lets go, and so does arming the notes alone), and the desk (`desk::arm_loop_cell`) arms the loop for REC as a tap on its Arm pill does (refused while a take records), picks an empty loop for it (`LoopHost::free_loop`: the picked loop if it is empty, else the first; `LoopHost::select_for_cell`, on the song's clock whatever Sync was last set to, as a free loop has no cell, and kept there through REC while it stays picked, with Sync left as set for the loops picked after it; Sync: Free set while a cell is armed lets the cell go, saying why, `desk::cell_lets_go`) and sets the looper to the track's input (`App::loop_input`; Line in switches the line input on as its Settings row does). The REC chip names the cell, and the cell says "Armed", "Ready" while the first pass waits for PLAY or the bar line, and "REC" while it records (`LoopsInfo::waiting` and `recording`, in short words for the 86 px column). The loop lands in the armed cell (`loops_are`: after a redo's own cell, before `loops::place`), playing, and the loop that played on that Loop track stops: one plays per Loop track. With a cell armed, REC's Record (`desk::loop_take`) goes into an empty loop, a song loop picked as the tap picks it, as Resample on a Resample track, and OVERDUB picks the loop playing on the armed Loop track; a first pass or an overdub under way takes REC and OVERDUB as the looper always has. With 8 loops in use, or less than a second of memory left (a first pass with no memory is thrown away), the tap and REC say why and arm or record nothing.
   Not built: a new project's empty Loops A. A new project has 8 tracks, so a ninth column would put the pager on every new project's grid; SHIFT + "+ Track" makes one when it is wanted, and the first loop recorded still makes Loops A.
5. **The song** (built, but for a block's origin and Print): a lane per Loop track in the Song view, Rec song recording loop launches as blocks linked to their cells, song mode playing them, and a block keeping the place in the loop it started at (an origin per block, a small looper change: a launch from a given place). **Print to audio track** bounces a loop's blocks into regions on an audio track (the looper's bounce already renders a loop as heard).
   As built: the Song view already had a lane per track, so a Loop track has one, its input under the name; Rec song already recorded a cell's launches as blocks linked to it, and Scenes → song puts a row's loop cells in with its clips. Song mode now plays them: each period, `loops::song_plan` turns a loop on while a block of it covers the song (on a Loop track with no cell launched and not muted, as a track's clips leave the song to a launch), with the tick in the period that a block starts or ends on, so the looper turns it there; the time engine keeps it (`TimeControl::song_loops`, worked out after the period's cues) and `loop_plan` adds it to the launches' plan. The looper does the rest through `follow_plan`, as for a launch, so a block's loop comes in and goes out on its tick's frame. Over a jump (a loop range, a locate) the period's last span says what plays; stopped, the block under the playhead plays when PLAY does. A loop's block stays on its Loop track's lane: `Arrangement::move_placement` refuses (`BadLoop`) a move or copy to another track when either is a Loop track, and `Arrangement::check` refuses a block whose clip is a loop off a Loop track, or a clip on one (the grid's drops already keep loops and clips apart; a copy to another Loop track is part 6's).
   Not built yet: **a block's own origin.** A block plays the loop in step with the loop's origin, as a launch does, so a block that starts a bar into the loop's pass starts in the loop's second bar. Rec song's blocks sound as they were played; a block dragged a bar along sounds a bar shifted. Starting a block from the loop's top needs the looper to launch from a given place (an origin per launch, kept apart from the loop's own origin, which its overdubs and undo use). **Print to audio track** waits on it. The song's blocks don't draw the loop's wave (the grid's cells do).
6. **The same loop in more than one row** (built): COPY a loop cell to another row of its Loop track and both play the same loop (a scene change between them keeps it playing without a break); a copy onto another Loop track makes a new loop, a copy of the audio, if memory and a free loop allow.
   As built: the grid's copy is SHIFT as the dragged cell lifts (the grid has no COPY gesture of its own). Into another row of its own Loop track it is the clip copy the grid already made (`DropClip` with `copy`), linked to the same loop; a scene change from one of its rows to the other keeps the loop on in every period's plan, so `follow_plan` sends the looper nothing and it plays on without a fade. Onto an empty cell of another Loop track the desk (`desk::copy_loop_cell`) takes an empty loop (`LoopHost::free_loop`, which now passes over a loop being copied or loaded into), the time engine puts it in that cell (`TimeAction::LoopCopy`, checked: a loop cell to an empty cell of another Loop track, a loop in no cell), and the looper copies the loop in (`LoopHost::copy_loop`): launched off first, so it stays silent until its cell is launched, at the source's level, then each layer with its mix, one at a time, through `Looper::copy_layer` (the Looper tab's layer copy, in time with the song, in the background while the loops play). `LoopHost::tend_copy` asks for the next layer once the last is in, asks again for one that didn't start (the loops busy or fading) for up to two seconds, and stops if the copy's loop is cleared or undone, or the source stops playing. `LoopHost::copy_ended` says how it ended: all in; some layers in (the cell keeps what it has); or none, when `TimeAction::LoopCopyOff` empties the cell. While it copies the cell's foot says "copying in" (`LoopFacts::copying`, from `LooperView::copy_into` and `Status::copying`). The copy is the looper's, not a take: the looper's UNDO takes its layers back (and its cell goes with its last), as a recorded loop's do. Refused, saying why: no free loop, memory for its layers and a second more, a source recording or changing, another copy still running.

## Edge cases

- **On the frame:** a launch lands on the bar line's exact frame (part 1): the time engine's period, where the cue starts, runs before the looper's in the same period. A looper command otherwise applies at the start of the next period. If the control half ever runs behind the audio half (time-threads.md, step 3), a line already past lands on the next one.
- **While recording:** a loop recording its first pass can't be launched elsewhere or copied; its cell shows it recording.
- **Undo:** a launch isn't an undo step, as for clips; recordings and edits keep the looper's undo.
- **Another tempo:** prints keep the pitch, as today.
- **The mixer:** the Loop tracks share today's Loop strip. A strip per Loop track, with its own effects, needs the looper to send each loop out on its own: later engine work.
