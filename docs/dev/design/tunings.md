# Tunings for keygroups and every instrument

**Status:** 2026-11-23, plan approved as written by the owner; T1 to T3 (phase 1) built; T4's mockups wait for the owner; T5 (each instrument's own tuning, under the screens) built; T6's plugin half (CLAP synths, the sandbox, plugins that can't follow) built, its screens waiting on T4. T2 (the sampler engine) is described in [Sampler architecture](sampler-architecture.md#kits-in-a-tuning-tunings-t2-mpfree-sampler). The plan, its research (Scala and MTS-ESP, Kontakt, Ableton Live 12, Pianoteq, Surge XT, the MIDI Tuning Standard) and the four decisions are in the Claude Doc [Microtonal tunings for keygroups and every instrument](https://claude.ai/artifact/4x2unuJnGr322L9XHd4Xfy). How a `Tuning` works today, and the Synth tab's Tuning rows, are in [Synths](synths.md#tunings-and-per-note-expression).

Keygroups get the tunings the synths already have, and every instrument can have a tuning of its own that starts as the song's. Later phases bring plugin synths and outside gear into the tuning (MPE, MTS, CV), and teach the pads, the arp and the scales about tunings that don't have 12 notes.

## The rules

- **Notes follow the tuning, hits don't.** Keygroup notes (from the pads playing notes, a keyboard, a clip, the arp or a MIDI route), 16 Levels set to Tune, glide and legato, warped and grain keygroups follow the tuning. A pad hit, a sliced pad, slices put on keys (Chop to keys) and the Drums synth play as tuned on the pad, as drum hits do everywhere.
- **A keygroup's root stays at its 12-TET pitch.** Key *n* sounds at the tuning's pitch for *n*: the voice adds `Tuning::offset_cents(n)` to its Tune. A4 = 432 moves a keygroup by the same -31.8 cents it moves a synth.
- **Bends move along the tuning's keys**, as the synths' bends already do: a bend of *b* keys plays the tuning's pitch at *note + b*.
- **The song has one tuning, and each instrument has Song's or its own.** It belongs to the instrument (by its id in the project's [instrument list](instruments.md)), not the track. Duplicate copies the pick; a preset doesn't carry one; a patch never changes it ([Live shows](live-shows.md)).
- **Auto-sample renders our own synths in 12-TET**, so the samples' roots are clean and the kit's tuning applies once.
- **Nothing new allocates or runs per sample on the audio thread**: a key's offset is worked out when its note starts or the tuning changes.

## T1 as built

- **`Tuning::offset_cents(note)`** (`rust/mpfree-tuning/src/lib.rs`): how far a note, whole or fractional, sounds from where 12-TET at A4 = 440 Hz puts it, in cents, so that a sound playing note *n* at `offset_cents(n)` cents above its 12-TET pitch sounds at `hz(n)`. It is exactly 0.0 for the standard tuning at every note (the equal branch checks 12 steps on key 69 and returns the A4 shift, `1200 * log2(440 / 440)`, which is 0.0), so a keygroup in 12-TET will play to the bit as it does now. An equal tuning works it out from its formula; a table tuning from its own log2 pitch, interpolated as `hz` interpolates it. A note that isn't finite gives 0.0. It never allocates.
- **`TuningPick` and `TuningFile`** (`rust/mpfree-tuning/src/pick.rs`), with `A4_MIN` and `A4_MAX` (380 and 480 Hz): moved from `mpfree-app/src/synth.rs` unchanged, so the sampler, MIDI out and the app share one type. The app re-exports them from `synth`, so its code and tests didn't change. Note names come from `mpfree-scale`, now a dependency of `mpfree-tuning` (neither has other dependencies). `MAX_TUNING_FILES` stays in the app, which reads the tunings folder.
- **Tests:** a note moved by its offset sounds at the tuning's pitch (within 0.02 cents) for every built-in tuning on four roots at four A4s and 19-TET, from key -64 to 191 in steps of 0.07, and agrees with `cents_off` at every key; the standard tuning's offset is 0.0 to the bit at every note, out of range and not a number; A4 = 432 is -31.77 cents, a quarter tone above A4 is 50 under A#4, and just on C puts the third at -13.69 and the fifth at +1.96; a pick makes its tuning and its label, clamps A4, plays a file instead of its built-in, ignores root and A4 for a mapped file and falls back when the file isn't offered. The `no_alloc` test adds picks (built-in, file, missing file) and offsets.

## T3 as built

- **Every kit plays the song's tuning** (T5 then gave each kit its own or the song's: [below](#t5-as-built)). `Player::set_tuning` (`rust/mpfree-app/src/sampler.rs`) hands one `Tuning` to every kit place of the engine (`Engine::set_tuning`, cheap for a kit already in it). The pad player's loop calls `kits_follow_tuning` (`rust/mpfree-app/src/main.rs`) every period, after the tempo: it compares the Synth tab's `TuningPick` with the one handed over last and, only when they differ, makes the tuning (`Synth::tuning_now`) and hands it over. The Console line ("sampler: the kits' keygroups play Just (5-limit) on D (pad hits and slices as tuned on the pad)") comes with each change but not for 12-TET at the start. The pick is the song's (saved with the project, as before), so a project opened in a tuning plays its kits in it from the first period. Until T5 every kit plays the song's tuning; T5 resolves each instrument's own.
- **Auto-sample records in 12-TET.** It plays `synth::fresh_node`, a copy made from the patch's values and design. A tuning is handed to the engines by `Synth::retune`, outside their parameters, so the copy starts in 12-TET whatever the song plays in, and a keygroup made from it plays the tuning once. Gear on MIDI plays as it is tuned (the manual asks for standard tuning).
- **Tests:** `the_kits_follow_the_songs_tuning_and_say_so_on_a_change` (every kit gets the pick, once, with its line; the same pick again does nothing; 12-TET at the start says nothing; back to 12-TET is said; T5 replaced it with `each_instrument_plays_its_own_tuning_or_the_songs`), `an_auto_sample_copy_plays_in_12_tet_whatever_the_tuning` (for each of the 12 engines with A4 = 432: the patch's values and design don't change, the pads' engine plays the tuning but Drums, and the copy plays to the bit as the engine did in 12-TET), and the plan's whole-session proof, `smoke::song_workflows` `a_song_in_just_on_d_plays_its_keygroups_tuned_and_keeps_it`: Tuning and root picked on the Synth tab's options reach the kit with the Console line, a keygroup's F#4 sounds 13.7 cents under 12-TET (within 1 cent of the tuning's pitch, measured from the session's output), the project saves `tuning = just` and `tuning-root = D`, New project puts 12-TET back, and opening the song again tunes the kit again. The smoke session hands the kits the tuning as `main.rs` does.
- **The screens and the manual:** the Synth tab's Tuning row's help says kits' keygroups play the tuning too, and drums, pad hits and slices keep their own pitch (`app/synth_pads.rs`). In the manual, the Synth tab's Tuning section says which parts of a kit follow, Playing a kit on keys says where a keygroup's root sits, and Auto-sample and 16 Levels say what they do in a tuning.

## T5 as built

Each instrument plays its own tuning or the song's. There is no new screen yet: the Synth tab's Tuning rows still set the song's tuning, so a musician sees no change until T6 gives each instrument its Tuning row. The manual is unchanged.

- **The model** (`rust/mpfree-app/src/tunings.rs`, `Tunings`): the song's `TuningPick`, and the picks of the instruments that have their own, by their lasting id in the [instrument list](instruments.md#tunings-tunings-step-t5). An instrument without one plays the song's ("Song's"), so a change to the song's tuning moves every instrument on Song's and none with its own. The storage is fixed, with room for every instrument the list holds at once (`MOST`: the shared synth, 128 tracks' own, 16 free places, 128 kit places) and no heap, so the audio loop reads it each period. `follow` lets go of the picks of instruments the list hasn't got any more (ids are never given again, so a new instrument starts on Song's), and `copy` is Duplicate's.
- **The app** (`rust/mpfree-app/src/app/tuning_picks.rs`) keeps the model with its copy of the list. The Synth tab's Tuning, Tuning root and A4 rows set the song's pick (`app/synth_pads.rs`), as do the Synth settings when they are put back. The shared synth (instrument 1) plays its own or the song's through the pads' settings (`App::shared_tuning_follows` into `PadSettings::tuning`, which `Synth::set_settings` hands to every engine), so the synth itself didn't change. `App::set_own_tuning` gives an instrument its own or puts it back on Song's, with a Console line ("tunings: Kit 2 plays its own tuning, Meantone 1/4 comma on C, A4 415 Hz"); T6's screens will call it, and the tests do now. **Duplicate** (the Instrument row's "Copy of Track 3's FM", `App::copy_track_sound`) copies the pick: at once when the track has its own instrument already, else once the list gives the copy its id (`App::tunings_follow_list`, from `set_instrument_list`).
- **The audio loop** (`TuningsHanded` in `rust/mpfree-app/src/main.rs`, mirrored by the smoke session) works out every period what each instrument plays in and hands the tuning only to those whose pick changed: each holder's own instrument or free place's synth (by its id and pick: `Instruments::set_tuning`), each kit place's keygroups (`Player::set_kit_tuning`) and each synth pad's copy, which plays its kit's tuning (`PadSynths::set_tuning`, by slot; before T5 the copies played the Synth tab's). An instrument or copy put in since the last period (`Instruments::puts`, `PadSynths::puts`) is handed its pick again, so a new sound in a track with its own tuning plays it. One `Tuning` is made per pick handed in a period. It replaced T3's `kits_follow_tuning` and the tuning copies in `own_follow_synth` and after the Synth tab's options; the Console line when the song's tuning changes is T3's, with "; 1 with a tuning of their own keep it" when kits have their own. Nothing allocates but that line.
- **Saved:** the `tunings` part, written only while an instrument has its own: `mpfree-tunings 1`, then `instrument 5 root C a4 415 meantone` (or `... file werckmeister 3`, the tuning last so a file's name may have spaces) for each, by id. The song's tuning stays in the synth part, where phase 1 kept it. While the part is written, `project.txt` says `needs = own-tunings` ([Project safety](project-safety.md#opening-is-all-or-nothing-01-as-built)), so an older build refuses the project instead of playing those instruments in the song's tuning and dropping them at its next save. **A choice made in building:** the plan moved the song's tuning into this part and made the project format 5. A `needs` word does the same safety job: projects without own tunings still open in older builds, and T5 didn't have to wait for the sampler's step 15b (format 4). The synth part's tuning lines are the song's, read first; the tunings part is read after the list of instruments (`App::open_tunings`).
- **Refused and said:** `project::check` reads the part before anything changes, so a line it can't read or an instrument given twice stops the open ("its tunings can't be read (...)"), as for the list of instruments. A tuning file this stick hasn't got is said and that instrument plays the song's; an instrument the list hasn't got is said and left out. The Console says which instruments have their own when a project opens.
- **Tests:** in `tunings.rs`, Song's until an instrument has its own, the song's change moving only those on Song's, Duplicate's copy, an instrument taken out losing its tuning, room for every instrument the list holds and no more, the part read back, a missing tuning file, and every line that doesn't read refused; in `app.rs`, `each_instrument_has_its_own_tuning_or_the_songs` (the shared synth's own, the song's rows beside it, the part saved and opened in a new app, an instrument gone said) and `a_copy_takes_the_tuning_of_the_instrument_it_copies`; in `main.rs`, `each_instrument_plays_its_own_tuning_or_the_songs` (two tracks' own Polys and the kits, a song change moving only those on Song's, a new sound handed its tuning, an instrument taken out and its next on Song's); and the plan's whole-session proof, `smoke::song_workflows` `instruments_play_their_own_tunings_and_the_songs_at_once`: two kits each play a keygroup and Track 2 its own Poly; the song goes to just on D on the Synth tab and kit 2 gets its own meantone at A4 = 415, and each kit's F#4 sounds within 1 cent of its own tuning's pitch, measured from the session's output; the song back to 12-TET moves kit 1 and the Poly but not kit 2; the save has the part and the needs word; New project puts every instrument on Song's; the song opened again gives kit 2 its own; and a part that doesn't read is refused.
- **Next (T6):** the approved screens (an instrument's Tuning row, a kit's, the Tunings page if the owner picks it), and the plugins ([below](#t6-as-built-the-plugins)).

## T6 as built: the plugins

Plugin synths play the tuning they are handed, as our own synths do; one that can't says so. The screens half of T6 (each instrument's Tuning row, which also shows "plays 12-TET" for a plugin that can't follow) waits on T4's approval.

- **CLAP synths** (`rust/mpfree-clap`, `ClapNode::set_tuning`): each note's tuning expression adds how far the tuning puts it from 12-TET at A4 = 440 Hz, in semitones (`Tuning::offset_cents / 100`), on top of its slide and the bend that goes as tuning. The offset is worked out at the moved pitch (key plus bend plus slide), so a slide moves along the tuning's keys: D4 slid up two keys sounds at the tuning's E4. A new tuning reaches held notes at the next block's start; in 12-TET at 440 the host sends exactly what it sent before (0, so nothing, for a plain note). A plugin that takes MIDI bends with MIDI pitch bend, in its own 12-TET steps from the tuned note; that is the bend's only approximation.
- **Plugins that can't follow:** a CLAP plugin whose note port takes MIDI and not CLAP's own notes now gets its notes as MIDI (note on and off, poly pressure, the bend; no slide or tone, which MIDI hasn't without MPE) and plays 12-TET. So does an MPC plugin instrument. Both say so through `Node::follows_tuning` (new, true by default; the instrument wrappers pass it on). The audio loop's resolver (`TuningsHanded`) says it once in the Console when such an instrument is handed a tuning other than 12-TET at 440: "tunings: Track 3's own instrument, Surge XT, takes only MIDI notes, so it plays 12-TET, not Just (5-limit) on D". It says it again only for a new pick or a new plugin there, not when another track's sound changes.
- **The sandbox** (`rust/mpfree-plughost`, shared block version 3): `SandboxNode::set_tuning` keeps the tuning and writes it into the shared block with the next request, as plain numbers (`Tuning::words`, 260 floats, with `Tuning::from_words` to read them back) and a number that counts the changes. The plugin process hands it to the plugin before that block, and a process started again after a crash takes it at once. Nothing waits or allocates on the audio thread. The process's hello says whether the plugin follows tunings (bit 1 of its kind byte, so an older hello reads as following).
- **Tests:** `mpfree-tuning`'s `a_tunings_plain_numbers_make_it_again`; `mpfree-clap/tests/tunings.rs` (the test synth plays C, E, G and B4 within 0.2 cents of just on C, a slide lands on just's E4, a held note moves from 12-TET's E4 to just's at the block after the change, and 12-TET at 440 renders the same samples as no tuning at all); `tests/midi_only.rs` (a synth whose note port takes only MIDI says it can't follow, plays E4 at 12-TET's pitch in quarter tones, and its notes and note-off arrive as MIDI); the CLAP `no_alloc` test changes tunings every 50 blocks; `mpfree-plughost`'s `a_sandboxed_synth_plays_its_tuning_as_in_process` (sample for sample, held notes moving back to 12-TET the same, and a killed process restarted in the tuning it had) and the hello's new flag round-tripping; the app's `a_plugin_that_takes_only_midi_says_once_it_plays_12_tet`.
