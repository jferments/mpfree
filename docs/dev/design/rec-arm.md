# REC records what is armed

**Status:** sequencer roadmap S2, first to third parts (`rust/mpfree-app`, 2026-11-17); the one-take pad take (sampler R5.7) on 18 Nov. Jay's sequencer decision 1 (16 Nov 2026, "REC records what is armed"): REC, OVERDUB and PLAY act on one armed target from every page, and the transport bar always names it. Design: the sequencer review (mpfree-claude `sequencer/rethink/README.md`, "REC, OVERDUB and PLAY", mockups F to H). Not run on the MPC One yet: the `rec-arm` device check goes into the next combined round, and the `transport` check now opens the Loop view first.

Until now the page decided what REC recorded: the looper on Play's All and Loop views (the Loop view went in the looper redesign's step 5) and on the Looper tab, a clip on Beats and Arrange. The home view showed the loop strip and the pattern together and REC went to the loop, so in the 16 Nov round the pattern stayed silent; nothing on screen said which.

## The rule

REC records the last recordable thing you opened or armed, and the transport bar names it.

- **The notes:** the armed track (the one the pads play), into its clip: the one the pattern shows, or a new clip in its first empty slot, as the time engine's Record does (`ready_to_record`).
- **A loop:** the looper's picked loop; or, with an empty Loop cell armed on the Clips grid, a new loop for that cell ([loop-tracks.md](loop-tracks.md) part 4).

One thing is armed at a time, unless **SHIFT + Arm** adds the other (`RecArm::both`, `RecArm::add`): then REC records the notes into the clip and the loop in one pass, as the MPC arms several tracks with SHIFT and Live with Ctrl. A new or opened project arms the notes alone. The arm isn't saved and isn't an undo step. SHIFT + REC is still Capture, whatever is armed.

## What moves the arm

1. **A tap on an Arm pill** (Play's loop row's, the pattern's) or a row of the chip's target list arms what it names, even when pinned (`Mover::Tap`, `FromUi::ArmTap`); so does **the loop's own Record or Overdub** (a ring tapped on Play's loop row or the Looper tab), letting go of the notes' ready.
2. Opening the **Sequencer** tab (its Steps, Beats' pages, which were Play's Beats view until the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign) arms the notes; the **Looper** tab arms the loop; **Play leaves the arm alone**, since it shows the loop row and the pattern both (`Mover::Page`, `Player::arm_for_page`, once each time the page is shown, so an arm moved on it since stays).
3. **Picking a track** (`Mover::Track`, `TimeAction::Arm(Some(_))`) arms its notes. **A tap on an empty Loop cell** on the Clips grid arms the loop, as a tap on its pill does, and that cell (`TimeAction::LoopCell`, `desk::arm_loop_cell`); the notes armed alone let go of the cell.
4. Nothing else: Play's All view, Mix, Synth, Pads, Sample, the Song view, the menus and Prefs leave it where it is.
5. **Pinned** (the target list's Pin the arm, `RecArm::pinned`, `FromUi::RecPin`), only a tap moves it: pages and track picks leave it. Pin isn't saved either.
6. **Nothing moves it while a take records** (the notes recording or counting in, or a loop recording its first pass or overdubbing); a tap then says "Finish the take first".
7. **SHIFT + a tap** on a pill or a row adds that target to the armed one (`FromUi::ArmAdd`), sharing a ready: the loop added to the notes ready waits for PLAY too, the notes added to an armed loop get ready. With both armed, SHIFT + a tap lets go of the one tapped and a plain tap keeps only the one tapped (the other lets go of its ready or its wait); a page or a track picked finds its target armed already and changes nothing. A SHIFT + tap on the only target says how to add one. Refused while a take records, like a tap.
8. **A ready goes with the arm:** the notes ready moving to the loop arm the loop (Record, or Overdub for an added take); an armed loop moving to the notes lets go of the loop and leaves the notes ready.

## The presses

With the notes armed (`RecArm::press`):

| Press | Stopped | Playing | Recording or counting in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| REC | Ready (again: not ready); PLAY then records, after the count-in | Punch in, replacing | Punch out; the music plays on |
| OVERDUB | Ready to add (again: not ready) | Punch in, adding | Switch between replacing and adding |
| PLAY / PLAY START while ready | Records (PLAY START from bar 1) | | |
| STOP while ready | Not ready (and STOP's own job) | | |

Ready is the app's own (`App::rec`); the transport only hears Record and ToggleOverdub when the take starts, so REC held and PLAY pressed records at once, the MPC's way. The transport's Replace / Overdub mode is set to the take's as it starts.

With a loop armed, REC is the looper's Record and OVERDUB its Overdub, as the Looper tab's were: REC stopped arms the loop and PLAY starts its first pass; the looper's armed loop is its ready. With a Loop cell armed (`desk::loop_take`), Record goes into an empty loop (Resample on a Resample track), or says why with 8 loops in use or the memory full, and Overdub layers the loop that Loop track plays; a first pass or an overdub under way takes them as before.

With both armed (`RecArm::press_both`), each does what it does alone, in step. Stopped, REC readies the notes and arms the loop (REC again or STOP lets go of both); PLAY starts the notes' take and, with it, the loop's first pass, after the count-in (the looper waits for the transport to start, not for the count-in). Playing with nothing recording, REC punches the notes in and the loop starts on the next bar line (or overdubs, if it has a take). While either records, REC goes only to what records: the notes punch out and the loop's first pass closes on the bar line (an overdub punches out). OVERDUB does the same with the notes' added take and the looper's Overdub.

## A pad take

Sampler R5.7 (the sampler's R5 plan, mpfree-claude `sampler/r5/plan.md`; Jay's card of 18 Nov 2026 08:52Z, one take only). **An empty kit pad held + REC** arms the Sample tab's recorder onto that pad for one take, over the arm (`RecArm::pad`, a `PadTake`): the target and both are never touched, so when the take ends REC records what it did before. Plain REC never stays pointed at a pad. The rule for musicians: SHIFT + REC means the past (Capture, the looper's last bars), a pad + REC records from now.

- **The gesture** (`App::held_kit_pads`, `Effect::PadRec`): REC without SHIFT while kit pads are held, and only while the pads play the kit (not the synth's layouts, STEP SEQ, TRACK MUTE, 16 Levels or the loop keys). The audio loop knows which pads have a sound (`sampler::Player::has_sound`), so it picks the first held pad with none on the armed track's kit (`desk::pad_rec`) and gives `Effect::PadArm`, or plain REC when every held pad has a sound or the track plays the synth. The kit is the sampler's kit slot.
- **Arming** (`RecArm::arm_pad`): refused while a take counts in or records, the notes' or a loop's or another pad take's ("Finish the take first"). A notes ready lets go and doesn't come back (rule 8), and a loop waiting for PLAY lets go too (`StopWaiting`): one take at a time. The same pad + REC again calls it off; another empty pad moves it there.
- **It holds like a pin** (rules 2, 3 and 5): pages and track picks leave it, and the user's pin is untouched. A tap on an Arm pill or a row of the target list before it records calls it off ("Pad A5 take called off") and arms what was tapped (rule 1); while it records the tap is refused. SHIFT + a tap is refused ("A pad take records alone", rule 7). While it is armed no Arm pill is red (`RecArm::armed` is false for both targets).
- **The presses** (`RecArm::press` with a pad take): REC or STOP end it (`PadDo::End`): what recorded lands on the pad, and nothing recorded calls it off. STOP while it records leaves the music playing (the app doesn't stop the transport, as for Sample Recall); while it only waits, STOP also stops as usual. OVERDUB says it doesn't add to a pad take. PLAY does its own job. UNDO while it records throws it away (`PadDo::Throw`), on every page, the Sample tab's too.
- **The go** (`RecArm::rolled`, `desk::pad_rolled`, every period after the transport runs): once the transport plays (PLAY, PLAY START, Arrange's Play, another device's Start; not during a count-in), the recorder gets `PadDo::Start`, once; armed while the music plays, at once. The recorder applies its own Starts from there (Now: at once; Next bar: the next bar line); with Starts on On input it waits for the threshold from the moment it is armed, music or not. A count-in before a pad take started by PLAY waits for a count-in on PLAY in the transport.
- **The wire**: the desk sends `gui::ToUi::PadTake(PadDo)` (`Arm { kit, pad, id }`, `ArmHere { kit, pad, id }` for the Wave page's REC, `Start`, `End`, `Throw`) to the Sample tab (`SamplePage::pad_take`); the screen polls `SamplePage::take_pad_now` each frame and answers `gui::FromUi::PadTake(PadNow)` (`Recording { id }`, `Ended { id }`, a word at a time in order), which the desk gives `RecArm::pad_now`. `id` is the take's number (`PadTake::id`, counted up from `RecArm::taken` for each take armed; one-page step 2b, the sequencer thread's review of 18 Nov), and the word is matched on it, not on the pad: the Wave page's take may stay on the page, so its pad no longer says where it lands. Word of another take than the one armed changes nothing: a take moved to another pad ends after the arm already holds the new one, and must not end it, nor may a held pad's late word for A6 end the row's take named by A6. `Ended` comes when the take landed, was called off, ended by itself (silence, a length) or couldn't run (the line input off, a take or a Flatten already running, a pad that plays an instrument); the recorder says why in the log.
- **The Wave page's REC** (the one Sample page's step 2a; the sequencer thread's line of 18 Nov, so the MPC's REC ends what the screen started): a tap on the recorder row's REC is a pad take onto the pad the row names (`FromUi::RecHere`, `RecArm::rec_here`, `PadTake::here`) that goes at once instead of waiting for PLAY: the recorder gets `PadDo::ArmHere` and `PadDo::Start` together and applies its own Starts (Next bar still waits for the music's next bar line, so stopped it waits for PLAY). The pad is the page's: the take lands where the Rec settings' Onto says when it stops (since step 2b, Stay here keeps it on the Sample page, the default; This pad replaces the pad's sound; a held pad's take must be empty). From there it is a pad take like a held pad's: the REC chip names it (the name the take will get, "● REC → Take 4 · Line in", or the pad it lands on; the arm's own lines say "Sample page"), REC there or on the MPC or STOP ends it and keeps what recorded with the music playing on, UNDO throws it away, nothing moves the arm until it ends, and REC then records what it did before. The row's REC again before it records calls it off; a held pad's take still waiting for PLAY moves to the row's pad, and a held empty pad + REC while the row's take waits moves the take to that pad (even the row's own pad: the gesture is a held pad's). While another take counts in or records it is refused, and the desk sends why (`ToUi::RecHereNo`, "Finish the take first: Drums records"), which the row says on its second line for 4 s and the hint line too. Test: `rec_arm::tests::the_wave_pages_rec_is_a_pad_take_that_goes_at_once`; the screen's side in `gui::tests::the_wave_pages_recorder_row_records_a_take_with_the_rec_settings`, through a `RecArm` as the desk drives it.
- **How it shows**: the REC chip reads "REC → Pad A5 · Line in" (the recorder's Source, `SamplePage::source_name`) while it waits and "● REC → Pad A5 · Line in" while it records, and the header chip shows "REC → Pad A5" or "● REC → Pad A5" on pages without a transport bar; REC blinks. A tap on the chip says what the take does.

## UNDO while recording

While a take records, UNDO throws it away (`RecArm::throw`, from the desk's `Effect::Undo`): the notes punch out (`TimeAction::ThrowTake`) and, once the punch out lands and the take's last changes have come (`TimeControl::period`, `throw_pass`), their take is undone and forgotten, so REDO can't bring it back; the log says "undo: take 3 thrown away". During the count-in it stops. A loop recording its first pass or overdubbing gets the looper's Undo. Otherwise UNDO is the page's, as before.

## How it shows

- **The REC chip** on Play's transport card, between the tempo and the bar dots (the dots narrowed to 8 px a 16th for it): "REC → Drums · Clip 1" in grey with a red dot while armed ("REC · PINNED" while pinned; "Drums + Loop 1" with both armed, as far along as the one further on); "READY · PLAY starts → Loop 1" outlined in red while ready ("READY TO ADD" for OVERDUB); solid red "● REC" or "● OVERDUB" while recording. Arrange's transport bar shows the same as a chip at its right end ("REC → Loop 1"), and its Rec, Play and Stop are REC, PLAY and STOP. A tap on either chip says what REC records and what arms another target.
- **Play's loop row** (the looper redesign's step 5) took the loop strip's place, and its empty text with it: an empty loop's card says what it records from, and the row's Arm pill says whether REC records it. Play shows the loop and the pattern both, so it arms neither (Play's Loop view, which armed the loop, went in the same step).
- **The Arm pills** at the left of the loop row's and the pattern's heading (`play_widgets::ArmPill`, `paint_arm`): grey "Arm" on what REC doesn't record, red "Armed", "Ready" or "Rec" on what it does (both red with both armed, each with its own state); the armed panel gets a red edge, a dashed red outline while ready and a solid one while recording (mockups F and G).
- **The target list** (mockup H, `rec_list.rs`, page `Play · rec`): a tap on Play's or the Sequencer's chip opens it in place of the view; rows for the notes and the loop, Rec song, Pin the arm, Close, and what moves the arm and the chip's three looks. The chip again, Close or another tab picked closes it. In Perform the Rec song switch is **Rec song with REC** ([make-perform.md](make-perform.md)): on while the take will bring Rec song, a tap takes it off for one take (`gui::rec_song_switch`).
- **The header chip** while REC is ready or recording, on pages without a transport bar: "READY → Track 1", "● REC → Loop 1"; a tap says what REC records.
- **The hint line** while the notes are ready: "PLAY: record Drums · Clip 1 · PLAY START: from bar 1 · STOP: not ready".
- **The lights:** the notes ready blink REC (OVERDUB for an added take) with PLAY, as an armed loop does.

## Where it lives

- `rec_arm.rs`: `Target`, `Take`, `Mover`, `RecArm` (`press`, `move_to`, `add`, `armed`, `throw`, `ready_lights`, and for a pad take `arm_pad`, `rec_here`, `rolled`, `pad_now`), `PadTake`, `PadDo`, `PadNow`, `Now` (what a press finds) and `Do` (what it does, in order).
- `rec_list.rs`: the chip's target list (`RecList`, `ListRow`).
- `time/control.rs`: `TimeAction::ThrowTake`, `throw_pass`.
- `app.rs`: REC and OVERDUB (without SHIFT) on every page give `Effect::Rec`; PLAY, PLAY START and STOP do too while the notes are ready; `transport_lights` blinks for ready.
- `desk.rs`: Arrange's bar Rec (`TimeAction::Transport(Record)`) is REC, and its Play and Stop act as PLAY and STOP while the notes are ready. `Effect::Rec` and `FromUi::Arm` find the transport and the loops (`rec_now`, `LoopHost::rec_now`), run the arm and carry out what it gives as the app's time and looper effects; the screen hears `ToUi::Rec`.
- `gui.rs`: `arm_for_page`, `arm_tap` (SHIFT held: `FromUi::ArmAdd`), `arm_pill`, `rec_chip`, `rec_chip_of` and `refresh_rec_chip` (the pills too), `toggle_rec_list` and `refresh_rec_list`, `rec_ready_hint`, the header chip in `refresh_chips`; `play_widgets::RecChip` and `paint_arm` paint them.

## Later in S2

Pin on in Perform (S4), a blinking chip, the pass number on the chip while recording ("● REC · pass 2"), a target that can't record (a full looper, no free clip slot) dimmed in the list with the reason, and audio tracks. With more than two targets, `RecArm::both` becomes a set. The sampler's recorder is a one-take pad take rather than a target (above). In Perform a pad take is to start on the launch line for a set number of bars and loop warped (the sampler's R5.3). While a pad take is armed the loop strip's empty text still says what REC records once it ends ("REC records the notes now"); it could name the pad.
