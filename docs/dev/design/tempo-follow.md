# Audio following the tempo

**Status:** phase 5a of the audio tracks rethink, built 2026-11-25 (the mapping, Follow tempo, Repitch, saving, the one history, the read's measures and bench rows), with no screen yet: phase 5c draws it. Phase 5b.1 built the scheduler copies are made on; 5b.2 plays Stretch from copies made ahead at a steady tempo (through a ramp, and while its copy isn't made, it plays as Repitch); 5b.3 keeps all derived audio within one budget, and moves the sampler's Warps onto the scheduler and into the budget; 5b.4 keeps a region on Keep pitch in pitch through a tempo change nobody planned, playing on from its audio and rejoining the bars on a bar line, and makes a launch wait for its copy (moving to a later line; at its second move it starts on Repitch on Play on time and is cancelled on Keep pitch). Phase 5c.1 draws the audio clip card's Tempo and Until ready rows and its foot (boards 1 and 2, "The clip card" below). Workstation only; it has not run on the MPC One.

Until phase 5 a region of an audio track, and an audio clip's region, played its recording at the rate it was recorded at, whatever the tempo ([audio-tracks.md, "Time"](audio-tracks.md#time)). A region recorded at 120 BPM and played at 90 stayed on its bar's start and ran on past its bars. Phase 5 lets a region follow the song's tempo instead, so it stays on its beats. The plan is [phase 5's](https://claude.ai/artifact/4hTk7JTozq8jiWPMzTvhrd) (mpfree-claude `notes/audio-tracks/07-phase-5-plan-clips-follow-the-tempo.md`), and its build steps note 16.

## The setting

Every region, of the song or of a clip, has three new fields (`Region` in `mpfree-audiotracks`, `ClipRegion` in `mpfree-seq` and in the track player):

- **`follow`** (`FollowTempo`): **Off** (the default, as before), **Repitch** (read faster or slower with the tempo: on its beats, its pitch moving with the speed) or **Stretch** (on its beats at its own pitch, from a copy stretched to the tempo and made ahead: see "Stretch's copies" below. While it has none it plays as Repitch).
- **`keep_pitch`**: on Stretch, whether to keep the pitch while its copy isn't ready (Keep pitch) or to play on time meanwhile (Play on time). A launch whose copy is still late after two lines starts on Repitch on Play on time and is cancelled on Keep pitch ("Launches that wait for their copy" below). Through a tempo change nobody planned, Keep pitch plays on in pitch and rejoins the bars on a bar line ("Keep pitch through a tempo change"); a song region with no copy when it is first heard plays on time either way, as it has no launch to wait.
- **`bpm`**: the tempo its audio was made at. A song region carries it resolved: its take's (`TakeInfo::bpm`) unless set by hand, or 0 for none known. A clip region says 0 for its recording's and a tempo only when it has one of its own, so a clip from before phase 5, followed later, plays at its recording's tempo; the track player (`takes_for`), Song → scenes (`SongSound::tempos`) and the cues that wait for their audio (the time engine's `rec_tempos`, which the audio tracks hand over when they change) resolve the 0.

A take whose tempo changed while it recorded has no one tempo (`TakeInfo::varied`, saved as `tempo=varies`). Its regions get tempo 0, and a region with tempo 0 plays Off whatever it says.

An audio clip has no setting of its own: each region has its own, as a song region does, so Song → scenes and To a cell carry the song's exactly. The clip card's Tempo and Until ready rows (5c.1) set every region (`AudioClip::set_follow`) and show "mixed" when they differ (`AudioClip::follow`).

## The clip card

Phase 5c.1 (boards 1 and 2, approved as drawn 25 Nov 11:27Z; layout spec 4j). In `mpfree-time-ui`'s `view.rs`, with the facts the app puts in each audio cell's `AudioCellInfo`:

- **Tempo** (`AUDIO_TEMPO`) steps Off, Repitch, Stretch (`AudioSet::Follow`, every region, one take a turn); "mixed" when the regions differ (`AudioCellInfo::mixed`). Off to Repitch or Stretch is refused while no region's made-at tempo is known. **Until ready** (`AUDIO_UNTIL`) steps on time and keep pitch on Stretch (`keep_pitch`); otherwise it is "—", a faint row (`OptionRow::faint`: no arrows, a step says why). Leaving Stretch drops keep pitch.
- **The made-at tempos** (`AudioCellInfo::tempos`, filled by `TrackHost::fill_cells`): each region's, its own else its recording's (`TakeInfo::bpm`, 0 for a take whose tempo varied). The card reads them as one, several or none known (`made_at`).
- **Where its copy stands** (`AudioCellInfo::copy`, a `CopyState`): the app asks the host for each clip with a region on Stretch, at the tempo now (`TrackHost::clip_copy_state`, `AudioHost::clip_copy_state`, over the keys `clip_copy_keys` works out): Ready when every copy is held, Making with how far the least made has got, Waiting (wanted, or being read), Unmade (no room, refused or failed: asked again when what is wanted changes), None when it needs none. How far a copy has got comes from the scheduler's worker without a lock: each copy under way has a slot in a small table (`copying::Progress`, one for each of the 8 at most), and `CopyWork::step` writes its tag (the low 32 bits of its key's id) and progress there after every step; the host reads a slot only under its own tag. The app's change fingerprint takes the copies held and under way and each one's progress in percent, so the card's line moves as it fills.
- **The foot** (`card_note`, an `OptionNote` with a tone for each line, a progress line and `picks`): the made-at tempo over what Tempo does now, or the copy being made, as layout spec 4j lists. A tap on it gives `Select(AUDIO_MADE_AT)` (the row past Name, `OptionList::picks`), and the wheel's steps then go to `AUDIO_MADE_AT`: 0.1 BPM a step, 1 with SHIFT, from the one made-at tempo (the tempo now when none is known), as `AudioSet::MadeAt`, which sets every region's (0 where its recording was made at it) and the clip's own `bpm`, joining a turn into one take. A comp of several tempos refuses it (`several_why`). The hint line then says so (`made_at_hint`, through `why_not` on the pick).
- **From its length** (`from_length`): the clip's length in quarters × 60 over its sound's length (`AudioCellInfo::secs`), to 0.1 BPM, as `AudioSet::MadeAt`; refused for a once clip, a comp of several tempos, a tempo MPFree doesn't play, or one it has already.
- **Fit to tempo went** (decision 3): its button, `TimeAction::FitClip` and the app's `ClipFit` and `fit_ended`. The host's take fit stays for phase 6's Commit stretch.
- **Off's words** (the cell's "made at 120" and its hint) are only for a clip on Off: one following the tempo keeps to the bars.

## The cells' words

Phase 5c.2 (board 3, approved as drawn 25 Nov 11:27Z; layout spec 4f). Each word comes from a state the engines already keep; `audio_cell` and `audio_word` in `mpfree-time-ui`'s `view.rs` pick one, and `audio_cell_hint` says it in full.

- **"copy 40 %"** is `AudioCellInfo::copy` (`copy_making`: Making, or Waiting as 0 %), on a stopped or cued clip, or one drifting on Keep pitch while its new copy is made. The cell draws a 3 px line in the accent along its foot (`GridCell::copy`) and the word in the accent (`CellAlert::Info`).
- **"waits copy"** is a cue the wait engine moved on for its copies alone: `AudioWaits::copy_late` (the late waits whose audio is held and copies aren't), as `TimeState::copy_late`. Its hint says what comes at its line: on time, its copy or Repitch; keep pitch, its copy or cancelled.
- **"not ready"** after a Keep pitch launch whose copy was late: `cancel_wait` records whether the wait was on its copies alone (`Wait::copies_only`), the fourth field of `TimeState::not_ready`, for the hint.
- **"repitch now"** and **"rejoins 9.1"** come from the track player, as it plays (`AudioTracks::on_stretch(track, clip)`, filled into `AudioCellInfo::stretch_now` by `TrackHost::fill_cells`). Each region's latch now carries its lane (`owner`: the track's song, or one of its voices) and whether it plays its recording for want of its copy (`repitch`: a copy wanted, by key, and not held when it was decided; kept up to date as the tempo moves). `Copies::on_stretch` looks at the latches of the clip's voices heard in their latest run, and gives one on Repitch for want, one drifting, and the soonest bar line a drifting one rejoins on. A latch decided on Repitch keeps it to the end of its pass, so "repitch now" stays until the pass after its copy came. Nothing allocates; the host's change fingerprint takes each playing clip's answer.
- **"too fast"**: a clip following the tempo (any region, when they differ) with a made-at tempo the tempo now is more than `MAX_SPEED` (4) times, stopped or playing.
- Playing, a word stays while it isn't as made (`GridCell::word_plays`); in Make it takes the wave's place, in Perform the foot's.
- **Tests:** `mpfree-time-ui`'s `an_audio_cell_on_stretch_says_where_its_copy_stands` (each word and hint from its state); `mpfree-audiotracks`' `a_clips_voice_says_when_it_plays_repitch_for_want_of_its_copy_to_the_end_of_its_pass` and `a_clip_drifting_on_keep_pitch_says_so_and_the_bar_it_rejoins_on_once_its_copy_is_here`; the app's `an_audio_clip_playing_repitch_for_want_of_its_copy_says_so_until_its_copy_plays` (through the time engine and the track host) and `on_keep_pitch_a_cue_whose_copy_is_late_is_cancelled_after_two_moves` ("waits copy", then "not ready"); the sample screen `arrange-clips-audio-copies`.

## The mapping

`Region::frames_to` stays what it was: the frames on the song from the region's start, counted along the tempo map. Fades, joins, cut counts (`into`, `left`) and a region's length on the song are measured in those.

A new position, the plan's `x(t)`, says which frame of the recording plays at song tick `t` (`Region::src_pos`): its `phase` plus

- Off: the frames along the tempo map from its start, as before;
- following the tempo: `(t − start) × 60 × rate / (bpm × PPQ)`, the ticks at its own tempo (`Region::per_tick`), whatever the song's tempo.

It is summed in f64 from the ticks every period, never added up a period at a time, so a region plays the same frames from any period size, after a locate and through a ramp. Every place that worked out a recording's frame now asks for it: the track player's read, a cut's `src` and `phase` (`cut`; Song → scenes' `SongSound::cut`), joins and seams (a region runs on into the next only on the same mapping), a clip voice's ring, the streaming heads and read ahead (in the recording's frames at the speed the song gives it now), the Song view's waves and a cell's small wave, To a cell's `last_frame` and the cues waiting for their audio.

## Repitch

A run (a stretch of a period with one tempo; the track player already cut runs at tempo changes) of a region following the tempo at a speed other than 1 reads through the streaming cursor's band-limited read, `Read::Sinc8`, on frames the track gathers itself (`Reader::add_pitched`): the frames under the run with the read's neighbours either side (`mpfree_stream::cursor::reach_of`), from the pool, the take's file or a part, then `mpfree_stream::cursor::read_gathered` on that slice. The cursor's `Cursor::play` calls the same function with its own fields, bit for bit as before.

- **One read at every speed.** `Sinc8` meets every row of the [pitched reads](pitched-reads.md) standard, so a region never changes read as the tempo moves and needs no fade between two, and a song region stays stateless. The pads' cheaper two-read scheme would need a 256-frame crossfade when the read changes, and so state a song region hasn't got; it is the fallback if the MPC's bench rows (5a.2) show `Sinc8` costs too much.
- **At speed 1** (the song at the region's own tempo) it takes Off's path, bit for bit, from the same frames (`at_its_own_tempo_a_region_following_the_tempo_plays_as_off_frame_for_frame`).
- **Range.** Past `MAX_SPEED`, four times (the cursor's), a run is silent and counted in `Stats::too_fast`, for the screens to say in 5c.
- **The real-time rules hold.** The gathered frames and the read's output have room made at start (`gathered`, `pitched`), and the kernels are built before the sound starts (`prepare_reads`); `no_alloc.rs` covers regions following the tempo through a tempo change.
- **Measured** through the whole track player as one of the [pitched reads](pitched-reads.md) (fxlab's `Reader::Tracks`: a take made at 100 BPM, the song at the speed's tempo): every row is the streaming cursor's band-limited read's.
- **Cost.** 8 tracks of comped takes repitched cost about 0.60 M instructions a 128-frame block at 0.9x and 0.78 M at 1.3x on the workstation, all of the track player, against 37 k played as recorded (the bench rows `Audio tracks: 8 playing comped takes, repitched to 0.9x` and `1.3x`). Their time on the MPC is on the next device round's list; if 8 don't fit beside a full project there, the pads' two-read scheme above is the fallback.

## Saving

- **The audio tracks' part:** a region line ends with `follow=repitch` or `follow=stretch`, `keep=pitch` and `bpm=` when its tempo isn't its take's, each only when not the default; a take line has `tempo=varies` when its tempo changed as it recorded.
- **The clips part:** a clip's `region` line ends with the same words, after where between frames it starts, `bpm=` only when it has a tempo of its own.
- **The gate.** A project with any of them says it needs `audio-tempo` ([project-safety.md](project-safety.md)), so a build from before phase 5 refuses it rather than play the audio at its recorded rate and drop the words at its next save. A project with none is written as before and needs nothing new.

## One history

- A song region's Follow tempo and tempo are region edits (`Command::SetFollow`, `Command::SetTempo`; the app's `AudioAction::Follow` and `AudioAction::Tempo`), each a step UNDO takes back, as Gain and Fades are.
- A clip's Follow tempo, and one of its region's tempos, go through the clip card's edit (`AudioSet::Follow`, `AudioSet::Tempo`): one take on the arrangement's history each.

## Making copies ahead: one scheduler

Stretch plays a copy of its recording made at the tempo (phase 5b). A copy is cheap to keep and slow to make, so copies are made before they are needed, and `mpfree_stretch::sched` (5b.1) is where: one worker for every stretch MPFree makes ahead, the tracks' copies and the sampler's Warps (5b.3), and in time the looper's tempo copies, so they never compete for the cores. The plan's "Preparing ahead: one scheduler" says why; mpfree-claude note 18 has the order of the 5b pull requests.

- **Steps.** A job is `sched::Work`, done a step at a time (`Work::step(frames)`; a stretch is `mpfree_stretch::Job::run`). A step asks for about 10 ms of work (`Config::step`), its frames set from how long the last steps took, within 256 and 8192 frames (`Config::max_frames`; the sampler's Warp promises a copy stops within 8192 frames of being told to). After every step the most urgent job goes next, so a 64-bar backing track's stretch holds up a launch's 2-bar copy by one step at most.
- **The order:** the plan's four priorities (`Priority::Urgent`, a launch waiting; `Protect`, what something playing needs next; `Prepared`, what is set up to come; `Speculative`, the rest), then the nearest deadline (`Job::deadline`; a job with none after those with one), then the first asked. `Sched::reorder` moves a job when its use changes (a cell queued after it was asked for).
- **Keys.** A job has a `Key`: what it makes, for whom. A newer job with the key of one waiting or part done takes its place; one in its step stops at the step's end. The old one is told (`Work::cancelled`) and never finishes. `Sched::cancel` and `Sched::retain` stop jobs the same way. A client files each result under what made it as well, so one that comes after its clip changed is thrown away. The tracks' copies carry their host's number in the id's low 16 bits (`copy_key`, each host's worker its own), so two hosts making the same copy on the process's scheduler never take each other's place, and Clear copies stops only its own; before 5c.2 two such hosts (two tests side by side) replaced each other's job without end and neither copy came (`two_hosts_making_the_same_copy_on_one_scheduler_each_get_it`).
- **Memory.** A job starts only if derived audio is within the one budget for its priority (`Budget::check`, below), and with a promise of the memory it says it holds (`Job::needs`, `Config::promise`), kept until it ends; otherwise it is refused, counted, and told why (`Work::refused`). The app's promise is the shared memory check's (`memory::reserve`, through `audiotracks::stretch_config`), in the same account as every load's.
- **Where it runs.** The worker (`mpfree-stretch`) starts with the first job and runs `Config::on_start` first on its own thread; the app (5b.2, `main.rs`) gives it the sampler's sound lane's, `mpfree_lanes::os::idle_job` (off the sound's cores, at idle priority). If it can't start, the jobs wait and `Counts::spawn_failed` says so. `Sched::shared` is the process's one scheduler, configured at the program's start (`sched::configure`); a test makes its own, or a manual one it steps itself (`Sched::manual`, `Sched::step`).
- **Counts** (`Counts`): done, replaced, cancelled, refused, couldn't start, steps, for the Dev > Debug lines.

The shape is the sampler's job service's (`mpfree-sampler`'s `jobs.rs`: keys, the promise, `on_start`, one a process), with steps and priorities added, so moving Warps onto it (5b.3) changed how its jobs are run, not what they are:

- **The Warps' copies** (`warp::Worker`) go on the scheduler of the job service the worker is on (`Jobs::stretch`): the process's service has the process's scheduler, and a test's service a scheduler of its own, started as its sound lane's workers are and promising memory as it does. The worker files each copy under the sampler's own key (its region and tune, whatever the tempo and pins), so a copy asked for at a newer tempo still takes the place of the one waiting or running, which comes back `Done::Cancelled` at once and stops at its step's end (`tempo_turns_keep_one_stretch_a_region_and_only_the_last_is_made`, unchanged).
- **Their priority:** `Protect` for the pad the Sample page shows (a hit picks it) and the take it has open, which wait on their copies after a tempo turn; `Prepared` for the rest (`Worker::send_at`). One asked for again more urgently while it waits, or whose pad the page comes to show (`Worker::raise`), is raised, not made twice, and `Jobs::promote` on a copy's key raises it on the scheduler too.
- **Refusals** (the budget's or the promise's) come back as `Done::Refused` with the reason, and the pad repitches. One for want of room in the budget (its reason starts with `budget::NO_ROOM`) is asked for again once all derived audio uses less than it did then, or every 5 s (`views::RETRY`), and said once on the log and the hint line until the tempo turns ("Not stretched for now, the pad repitches"); one the memory check refused waits for the next tempo, as before; `Jobs::counts`, `waiting`, `busy` and `reserved` count the scheduler's jobs with the service's, and `Jobs::hold` holds both.

### One budget for derived audio

Every copy MPFree makes from a recording it already has can be made again, so all of them share one budget, `mpfree_stretch::budget` (5b.3): the tracks' tempo copies, the sampler's Warps, and in time the looper's tempo copies (5d) and reverse copies (phase 6). Each cache keeps its own code and copies and lets go of its own; the total is one number, `Budget::total`, 200 MiB to start with (`DEFAULT_TOTAL`, the sampler's Warps budget before, now for all of them together, not each).

- **What a cache uses** is what it holds (`Budget::set_held`) plus what it has asked for and not had back (`Budget::set_making`); it says each whenever it changes, from whichever thread knows it. The tracks' host says both every period. The Warps say what they hold as a copy comes in or goes and as the Sample page takes in what was made (`Warps::tidy`), and their worker says what it has under way as it asks and as each copy comes back.
- **Room goes by priority** (`Budget::limit`): what is needed now (Urgent, Protect) may fill the whole budget, what is set up to come (Prepared) three quarters, the rest (Speculative) half, so copies made just in case never leave a launch without room.
- **Two checks.** A cache asks before it asks for a copy (`Budget::fits`), letting go of its own copies nothing wants if it must; the scheduler checks again as each job starts (`Budget::check`), so two caches asking at once can't pass it between them, and a job that doesn't fit then is refused with why. A copy that found no room is asked for again once less is used.
- **Which budget.** The process's scheduler checks the process's budget (`Budget::shared`); a test's scheduler has its own, and a host making copies on it (`AudioHost::copies_on`) uses that one. The Warps count in their worker's (`Warps::join(worker.budget())`, as the Sample page first asks for a copy); until then, in one of their own.
- **What changed for the Warps.** Before, they had 200 MB of their own. Now a copy for a pad not shown (Prepared) is refused once all derived audio passes three quarters of the budget, 150 MiB, even with nothing else using it, so a kit with many warped pads starts repitching some of them there; the pad shown (Protect) may fill the whole 200 MiB.
- **The Warps' room** (`Warps::room`) is the less of their own cap (`Warps::set_budget`, `DEFAULT_BUDGET`, 200 MiB) and what the budget leaves them beside the other caches and their own copies on their way. Past it, the least recently used copies no kit wants at the tempo go, never one a kit wants, nor one a voice or an engine view still plays (its handle is shared): that one stays, counted, until the view lets it go. A copy taken out while something still plays it (replaced, or the project cleared) is counted as held until its last handle goes.
- **Not yet safe.** The number is checked on the MPC, as a named read-only check in a device round with Jay's go, before it is treated as safe (mpfree-claude `notes/device-rounds/next-round.md`). System status's Memory shows each cache's share in 5c.

## Stretch's copies

A region on Stretch plays a copy of its recording stretched to the tempo (5b.2). The track player's side is `mpfree-audiotracks`' `copies.rs`; the host's, which asks for them and has them made, is `copying.rs`.

- **Filed by what made it** (`CopyKey`): the recording and its frames then, the region's stretch of it and where between frames it starts, the frames it plays for, its made-at tempo and the tempo, the rate, its ends, the stretch's settings and `mpfree_stretch::VERSION`. A region plays a copy only while the key it works out now is the copy's, so a copy made before an edit, for another tempo or by an older build is never played (`a_copy_made_before_an_edit_or_for_another_tempo_is_never_played`). Its frames are worked out from ticks, never along the map, so the host and the player always work out the same key.
- **Held apart from the takes.** A copy never takes a recording's room in the pool: it is audio the scheduler made, held in a table of `MAX_COPIES` (64) in the track player and handed back to the host to drop off the audio thread. The copies share the one budget for derived audio (above) within the limit for each copy's priority. A copy that doesn't fit lets go of copies nothing wants, the one heard longest ago first, never one playing; if it still doesn't fit it isn't made and its region plays Repitch until room comes back.
- **Played at speed 1.** At a steady tempo a copy is the region's frames at that tempo, so it plays as an Off region plays its take: from `Copy::lead` plus the frames from the region's start, with the region's gain, fades and joins. Back at its own tempo a region plays its recording, frame for frame, with no copy.
- **Ends.** A copy has its recording's own audio either side of the region, two stretch windows' worth (`Ends::Edges`), so its fades, crossfades and a ring past its end sound as they would; a clip's last region reaches to the clip's `last_frame` for its ring. A looping clip's one region whose recording ends where it does is made wrapped round with its seam crossfaded (`Ends::Wrapped`, as the looper's prints are), so it goes round with no click.
- **Latched.** What a region plays is decided when it is first heard (each pass of a clip, each time a song region plays): its copy if it is held, else Repitch (Play on time). The choice holds to its end, so a copy that comes mid-pass waits for the next pass, and the pitch never moves mid-pass; a tempo change that leaves its copy behind plays Repitch to its end, then the new copy (on Play on time; Keep pitch plays on, below). A locate, a jump or the transport stopping starts the choices again. Where a copy and a recording, or two copies, meet, they meet with the equal-power fade (`Join::Fade`).
- **Keep pitch through a tempo change** (5b.4). A region on Keep pitch latched to a copy, or to its recording at its own tempo, doesn't switch to Repitch when the tempo moves away from it: it plays on from that audio at speed 1, in pitch, from where it was (`Drift`: the frame of the audio and the output frame at the change, worked out from the ticks played at the old tempo), drifting from the bars, counted in `Stats::drifted`. The copy it plays on from counts as playing, so it is never let go meanwhile. Once the copy for the tempo now is held (or the tempo comes back to its own, for its recording), it rejoins on the first bar line from there, by the run's meter, if the region still plays there: from that audio where the bars put it, crossfaded with equal gain over `FADE` frames (the same audio a little apart, as `Join::Ramp`), and holds it from there. A tempo turned again before the bar line waits for the copy for the newer tempo; Keep pitch turned off, or its audio gone, plays Repitch to its end. The host asks for a drifting region's copy first, at `Protect` (`AudioTracks::drift_wants`), so a clip's copy the app listed at `Prepared` is raised.
- **What is wanted** (`CopySide`, on the audio thread with the host, allocating nothing). Once the tempo map has held for half a second (a tempo being turned asks for nothing), every 32 periods and at the next one after a command or a copy coming back: the copies the regions drifting on Keep pitch wait for, then every song region on Stretch over one steady tempo, from the playhead to two bars on first (`Priority::Protect`), then the next eight bars (`Prepared`), then the rest (`Speculative`); and the audio clips with a region on Stretch the app lists (`AudioHost::clip_copies`, from the pads and the song's own clips each time the project changes, `Prepared`), at the tempo at the playhead. A copy under way that is no longer wanted is cancelled on the scheduler; one that comes back no longer wanted, or for tracks since emptied, is dropped.
- **Made.** The source frames are read from memory into a buffer the host's worker makes, at `READ_SPEED` (64) frames a frame played, one copy at a time; the host's worker hands them to the scheduler, keyed by the copy and at its priority, and the scheduler's worker stretches them a step at a time (`CopyJob`) and sends the copy back. The host's own worker never stretches: it writes recordings to their files.
- **Counts** (`AudioHost::copy_counts`): made, stale (dropped, never played), no room, refused (the scheduler's memory check), failed, let go, and too many wanted (`MAX_WANTED`, 256); `Stats::copied`, `Stats::for_now` and `Stats::drifted` count the frames played from copies, on Repitch for want of one, and drifting on Keep pitch. `AudioHost::copy_memory` is what they hold. **Clear copies** (`AudioHost::clear_copies`, System status's Memory in 5c) lets go of every copy nothing plays: no recording is touched, it is no undo step, and each is made again when next wanted.
- **Where two meet straight.** A region's frames from its start are rounded to the nearest frame with halves up either side of its start, and float noise within a millionth of a frame of a half counted as one (`nearest`, used by `Region::frames_to`), so a looping clip's wrapped copy ending one pass and starting the next agree on the frame between them. With `round`, a tempo change on a tick whose frame falls on a half left a frame of silence at every pass's end (found by 5b.4's tests; `a_looping_copy_goes_round_with_no_frame_missing_after_a_tempo_change`).
- **Timing.** A copy puts each hit where the stretch puts it: its hit finder finds an onset just before the peak, and the attack plays at its own speed from there, so a hit lands about 30 × (stretch − 1) frames early at 22.05 kHz (10 frames, 0.45 ms, at 120 → 90). That is the stretch's own, the same for the sampler's Warps; the click test allows it, and the follow-up is with the sampler thread (TODO.md).
- **Not copied in 5b:** a take on its file (streaming stays off on the MPC for now) plays Repitch, and so does a region over a ramp or a step (5d).

## Launches that wait for their copy

Jay's decision 2 (23 Nov): a launch whose copy is still late after two launch lines starts on Repitch on Play on time, and is cancelled on Keep pitch (5b.4). It rides on the cues that wait for their audio ([audio-tracks.md, "Cues wait for their audio"](audio-tracks.md#cues-wait-for-their-audio-phase-2)), in `mpfree-app`'s `time/audio_wait.rs`.

- **What waits.** With copy waits on (`set_copy_waits`, which the app turns on for the live engine at start and for each project's engine; never the offline copy), the cues decided together wait when any launches an audio clip with a region on Stretch whose made-at tempo is known (its own, else its recording's) and isn't the tempo at its line (one steady tempo in 5b), streaming on or not. With streaming off its audio needs nothing.
- **What it needs.** `copy_needs()` lists a `CopyNeed` for each such region: the cue's key, its track, slot and region, and the tempo at its line, under the same `needs_rev` as the audio. A copy said ready stays listed until its cue lands or goes.
- **The app's half.** Whenever `needs_rev` rises the app hands the copy needs to the tracks (`TrackHost::hold_copies`): each launched clip goes to the host at `Urgent`, at the tempo at its line (`AudioHost::launch_copies`, asked for before anything else at the next steady period, with `ClipWant::bpm`), and the keys each cue needs are kept. Every look, `TrackHost::copy_answers` tells the engine `copies_ready(key, ok)` when whether they are all held changes. A region whose copy has no key (its take not here, or past the speeds a copy is made at) needs nothing.
- **Ready, or not.** A wait goes into the view once its audio is held (or it needs none) and its copies are ready, and lands on its line. A line played without its copies moves it to the next, as for audio ("copy not ready: Vocal: Chorus moves to bar 3"). Waiting on its copies alone, at its second move it goes in on Play on time and lands on that line, from its copy if it came meanwhile, else on Repitch (the latch decides as the region is first heard: "... starts on Repitch at bar 4"); on Keep pitch (any region needing a copy) it is cancelled after its second move, as audio is ("... cancelled after 2 moves"), and its cell says not ready. At Now, Play on time goes in after two bars where Keep pitch is cancelled. Waiting on its audio, it moves and is cancelled as before, whatever its copies.
- **Counted** beside the audio waits (`AudioWaitCounts::copy_kept`, `copy_ready`, `copy_moved`, `copy_repitch`, `copy_cancelled`), on the status line once a cue has waited for its copies.
- **Tests** (`time.rs`, `audio_waits`): a cue on Stretch waits for its copies and lands on its line once ready, with each region's need listed; on Play on time one whose copy is late moves once and starts on Repitch at its second move, landing on that line; on Keep pitch it moves twice and is cancelled, never launching; at Now Play on time goes after two bars and Keep pitch is cancelled; with streaming on it waits for its audio and its copies under one key; a clip at its own tempo needs no copy, and copy waits turned off let one waiting go. The app's `a_launch_waiting_for_its_copy_gets_it_made_at_once_and_is_told_when_ready`: the host makes it at once at the tempo at its line though the clip was never listed ahead, and the engine is told once, when it is ready.
