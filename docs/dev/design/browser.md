**Status:** built (crates `mpfree-library` and `mpfree-browser-ui`). In the pad player since 12 Nov 2026 (first as the Pads tab's Browse view, a picker that stayed open, until the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign of 18 Nov), and since 13 Nov as the full browser over every page (BROWSE, the top bar's folder icon, the Pads tab's Browse for), with whole projects: SHIFT + BROWSE, Save, Save as, Open and New project (section "Projects in the pad player"). The design, its research and the open decisions are in the plan doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/DQbPuih2LPA4pUkQUx3QyW. ## What it does

BROWSE opens the browser over the current page, under the top bar, as Prefs and the mixer do. It is not a tab. BROWSE again closes it. SHIFT + BROWSE saves the project from any page.

- **Places** on the left: Projects, Samples, Kits, Instruments, Patterns, Recordings; Plugins; Recent and Favourites; Factory (the kits made from the owner's own backup, read only); Trash. **Plugins** (MPC plugins from the Open MPC catalog and zips on the stick) is not a library folder: the browser draws its list and Selected column from a `PluginsScreen` the app gives it (`Browser::set_plugins`) and hands every touch, dial turn, push, + and - back as `BrowseAction::Plugins`; see [Installing MPC plugins](mpc-plugin-store.md). Pickers never offer it.
- **The list** in the middle: folders (plain folders, projects and kits) first, then files, in natural order (`Kick 2` before `Kick 10`). Back, the path, New folder and Paste at the top (Save kit too in Kits). Each row that points at something has a **star** at its right end (`Input::Star`): a tap makes it a favourite or not (Jay, 16 Nov: no Favourite button). One tap on a plain folder opens it; anything else is picked by the first tap and opened or loaded by the second, as the dial's push does (no Open button). The filter chips at the bottom (All, Audio, Projects, Kits), then **Search** and the order chip (**A to Z** or **Newest**: folders first either way, then the last changed first). A line under them says what just happened, or why something failed.
- **Search** opens the keyboard. The text is looked for, in any case, in every name in the library except the Trash, and not inside projects. Results show where each item is, with the same buttons as Recent (no Rename or Cut), and the filters still apply. Back returns to the folder. At most 200 results; the search stops after 4000 folders so it costs a bounded time on the worker.
- **The selected item** on the right: for a sound, its length, rate, bits and channels and a quick waveform (WAV, AIFF and uncompressed AIFF-C read at each column's place; FLAC decodes the one frame at each column's place, found by guessing from the bytes, so a 3-minute FLAC reads about a third of the file: `mpfree_flac::overview`); for a project, its tempo and when it was saved. Then its buttons: Load, Load to pad, Audition, Rename, Copy, Cut, Delete (a sound's Load reads Load on A5 and its Load to pad Next pad hit while the app gives the pad picked: `Browser::set_pad_target`) (Earlier saves for a project; Put back in the Trash). There is no Open or Favourite button (Jay, 16 Nov: clutter): a tap or the push opens, the row's star stars. **The pad map** (the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign's board 8): under a sound, out of a picker, with a kit on the pads (`Browser::set_pad_map`, a `PadMap` of the kit's name, each pad's sound and the pad picked, given before each step), the facts go on one line, the waveform shrinks, the buttons go three across, and a bank of the kit's pads fills the middle (`Screen::pad_map`, `MapShown`), pad 13 top left as on the MPC, the pad picked outlined; the arrows turn the bank (`Input::MapBank`, round from H to A), and a pad picked in another bank brings that bank. A finger on a pad is a pad: `Input::MapPad { down }` on the press and the lift (`BrowserView` keeps the finger), `BrowseAction::PadPress { pad, down }` for the kit's pad; the app picks it (`SamplePage::hit`, so Load on follows) and plays it while held (`FromUi::Audition`), logging `browser: pad A9 picked (Break 92)`. Only the full width has the room (not under `NARROW_BELOW`).
- **Audition** is on by default: moving the selection to a sound plays it once through the preview path. A list arriving never plays anything by itself.
- **Delete** asks first, with the name, and moves the item to the Trash. Empty Trash asks again.
- **Rename and New folder** use an on-screen keyboard. Names may have spaces and capitals. They may not have `/ \ : * ? " < > |`, a leading dot or space, or a trailing dot or space, so they also fit a FAT stick.
- **Saving** needs no typing: the first save names the project for the time (`Project 2026-11-11 21.04`), and Rename or Save as changes it.

| Control while the browser is open | Does |
|---|---|
| Data dial | Moves the selection |
| SHIFT + dial | Moves through the places |
| Dial push | Opens a folder or project, loads anything else (a kit whole), answers yes to a question |
| `-` / `+` | Up a folder / into the selected folder or kit |
| PLAY | Hears the selected sound |
| ERASE | Delete (asks first) |
| Q-Links 1 to 4 | Scroll, filter, preview level, place |

## Pickers

A picker is the same browser narrowed (`Browser::open_picker(Pick)`), in the same place under the top bar. It offers only Load (and opening by tap or push), Audition, the stars and Cancel; nothing is renamed, moved or deleted there. When it closes, the full browser is back where it was.

| Picker | Opened from | Shows | Does |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Pick::Preset { engine }` | the Synth tab's title (`Browser::open_preset_picker`) | Factory (the engine's own presets, as the Synth tab lists them, no files), Yours (`Instruments/<engine>/`), and that engine's presets in Recent and Favourites | Each turn of the dial sends `TryFactoryPreset` or `TryPreset` so the preset can be played on the pads; Load sends `LoadFactoryPreset` or `LoadPreset`; Cancel sends `RevertPreset`; Save preset sends `SavePresetRequested`, answered with `Browser::save_preset` (below); New track sends `NewTrackWithPreset` (after `RevertPreset` if one was tried) |
| `Pick::Sample` | the Sample page or a pad | Samples, Recordings, and sounds in Recent and Favourites | Auditions as you move; Load sends `LoadSample` for the pad it was opened for |

Every picker ends with `PickerClosed { loaded }`. BROWSE while a picker is open should call `cancel_picker`. The hint line is `PICKER_HINT`. Screens 12 to 14 in the previews.

**The preset picker in the app** (layout spec 4g, Jay's pick A of 18 Nov; `mpfree-app/src/presets.rs`). A tap on the Synth tab's title (an engine's, not a plugin's) gives `SynthAction::OpenPresets`; the screen keeps what the tab names (`presets::Picker::ask`: the engine's folder, its presets' names, `PresetNow` with "edited") and sends `FromUi::PresetPicker(true)`, which the app turns into the browser view (`App::open_browser`). Showing the browser opens the picker (`Picker::opened`). Another view showing cancels it (BROWSE is Cancel), and `PickerClosed` sends `PresetPicker(false)` (`App::close_browser`, back to the Synth tab). The browser's actions go to the sound loop as `FromUi::Preset(presets::Ask)`: `TryFactory`, `Try`/`Load` with the file's text (read on its own thread, `Picker::read`, at most `MAX_PRESET_TEXT`), `Revert`, `Text` (Save preset: answered with `ToUi::PresetText`, the sound as preset text and the engine's folder) and `Saved`. `TrackPresets::act` does them on the selected track's own synth (a new copy from the builder per try, the newest kept while it is busy, the sound before the first try kept to put back) or on the synth on the pads (`Synth::try_preset` and the rest). A save ends the try: Cancel then keeps the saved sound. **New track** (instrument redesign step 4c) gives `NewTrackWithPreset`: the screen asks for a new track (`Player::add_track_for_sound`) and, once it is armed and a file is read, sends `FromUi::NewTrackSound`, which `TrackPresets::give` turns into the new track's own synth playing the preset ([instruments.md](instruments.md#4c-as-built-new-instrument-track)). The Synth tab's title names a preset of the musician's by its file's name (`SynthState::own`). Preview screen `browser-presets`.

## Where files go (rule zero)

Everything is written to `library/` inside the data folder the app is given with `--data`, which is the test stick's MPFREE-DATA partition (`/run/mpfree-data`). The eMMC and the SD card are never written. The storage spec's "Proposed" section adds `library/` beside the store's `settings/` and `files/` and grows the partition. That needs Jay's go.

```text
library/
  Projects/<name>/        project.txt, parts/<owner>.txt, samples/<hash>.<ext>, versions/<id>/
  Samples/  Kits/  Patterns/  Recordings/
  Instruments/<engine>/<name>.preset   user presets (Jay, 2026-11-11 22:47Z)
  .trash/<id>/<name>      with origin.txt (where it was, when)
  .work/<owner>/          an engine's files for work not saved yet (Library::work_dir)
```

- A location is a place plus checked names (`Loc`), never a typed path. Every folder on the way must be a real folder; links are never followed for reads or writes.
- Whole or nothing: a new or copied file is written to `NAME.part`, synced and renamed; a copied folder is built under a hidden name and renamed. `Library::write_new_unsynced` is the same without the sync, for thousands of files synced once at the end (the creator stick's first start copies Akai's samples that way: two flushes of the stick per file had it crawling). For the same setup, `new_folder_unsynced` makes a folder without the sync, `move_file_unsynced` moves a file into a folder under a given name, and `link_file_unsynced` gives a file a second name in another folder (a hard link, so a sound in two kit folders is stored once). The last two refuse a name that is taken, a folder or link as the source, and the Trash. The library never changes a file in place, so a change through one name never shows through the other; deleting one name leaves the other.
- A project save writes its new sounds to hidden `.incoming-<pid>-<n>.part` files and its parts to `.parts-new/` without a sync each, makes them durable together (one `sync` in the pad player, set with `mpfree_library::set_flush`; a sync of each file elsewhere), and only then renames the sounds in. A save of 16 sounds and 12 parts flushes the disk at most twice, where it flushed once per file (64 s for one save on the MPC, round 4). Hidden sounds a power cut left are removed at the next save of that project.
- Every file operation runs on the `mpfree-library` worker thread, never on the UI or audio thread.

## Kit files

Jay, 16 Nov: load whole kits at once, as on the MPC, and keep kits as kits rather than loose sounds. A kit is one folder (`mpfree-library/src/kit.rs`):

```text
<Kit name>/
  kit.txt        the sampler's part for one kit: "mpfree-sampler 1", kits = 1,
                 every pad and setting (mpfree_sampler::save), sounds named by file
  Kick.wav ...   its sounds, beside it
```

- Any folder holding `kit.txt` is `Kind::Kit`, wherever it is (Kits, Samples ...), listed with the folders. The folder's name is the kit's name, so Rename renames it. Search doesn't look inside kits (as projects).
- **Load** (tap again, push, Load) sends `BrowseAction::LoadKit { loc, path }` with the folder. The button names the armed track, whose kit it replaces, **Load on Drums** (board 8; the app gives the name before each step with `Browser::set_kit_target`, none while the pads play an instrument or no track is armed, and the button then reads Load); the app (`SamplePage::open_kit`) reads `kit.txt` and its sounds on a thread (each only if memory allows, `memory::room_in`), and puts the kit in place of the kit the pads play, named for the folder, as an edit (the work is unsaved). `+` goes into the kit to show its sounds.
- **New track with this kit** (`Button::NewTrack`, beside Load for a kit; the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign's step 1, board 8) sends `BrowseAction::NewTrackWithKit { loc, path }`. The app (`Player::add_track_for_kit`) asks the time engine for a track as the Pads tab's + Kit track does (`TimeAction::AddTrack`, which arms it), refused and said with no empty kit left or while another new track is on its way. Once the new track is there and armed, `Player::kit_track_armed` loads the folder into the first empty kit (`SamplePage::open_kit_into`) and gives that kit to the track (`FromUi::Kit`), so the kit the pads played is untouched. A track that never comes is given up after 3 s, and the log says the kit wasn't loaded (at 128 tracks it is refused at once). One UNDO takes back the track and its kit (a kit's load is on no undo history, so the kit goes with the track): `TrackKits::follow`, run each period after the time engine in main.rs and the smoke session, sets the entry of a track UNDO took away aside (its place plays kit 1 again, so a track added there later never plays the kit the one before it had), and while it is aside the kit is out of the list of kits, no New kit's or load's spare and no SHIFT + wheel stop (`TrackKits::set_aside`; gui.rs `spare_kit` and `kit_rows`, main.rs `kit_turned`). A REDO (the time engine's count of them, `Time::redos`) gives the track its kit back. Once REDO can't bring the track back (`can_redo` false: a new take cleared the undone ones), the entries aside are dropped and the kit is listed again, played by no track, so nothing loaded is lost. The same holds for + Kit track and the Sample tab's Chop to new kit.
- **Save kit** (Kits place, full browser and the Pads tab's picker) sends `SaveKitRequested`; the app answers with `Browser::save_kit(name, text, sounds)` from `SamplePage::kit_file` (the kit the pads play; sounds named after themselves, `Kick.wav`, `Kick 2.wav`, by `sample_page::kit_file_of`). The worker's `Job::SaveKit` runs `Library::save_kit`: the folder is built under a hidden `.kit-<id>-<name>` beside where it goes, each file made new (`create_new`, `O_NOFOLLOW`) and synced, then renamed in (`Name 2` if taken). Never in Projects or the Trash.
- **MPFree's own kits** (`mpfree-app/src/bundled.rs`): once per library, the factory kits whose names are the Drums synth's (`MPFree <preset>`, from kits.bin) or MPFree's free kits' (`mpfree_factory::freekits::kit_names`, the public-domain kits a release stick carries after its own) are written as kit folders in `Kits/MPFree/` on a background thread. `bundled = mpfree-kits 2` in the browser's lists (`Browser::set_bundled`) records it, so a deleted kit stays deleted; a later tag adds only what is missing (tag 2, 2026-11-16, added the free kits).
- **Akai's kits** (`mpfree-app/src/setup.rs`, the USB creator's first start): each drum kit's folder in `Samples/Akai factory/` already holds its sounds, so the setup writes only its `kit.txt` (`setup::kit_text`: pads 1-16 and their sounds, every other setting a new pad's), through `Library::write_new_unsynced` and one sync ([USB creator](usb-creator.md)).

## Big folders

A folder of thousands of sounds (Akai's factory content, 4,338 in one folder on Jay's round of 16 Nov, listed after minutes of "Reading…") must list at once.

- `Library::list` reads each entry's type from the folder itself (`DirEntry::file_type`, no look at each file). Over `QUICK_LIST` (300) entries it leaves each file's size and time unknown (`Entry::sized` false); the browser shows the names at once and asks for `Job::Sizes`, which fills them in.
- The worker's jobs can go in a **lane** (`Worker::send_in`): a job is skipped (`Outcome::Skipped`) when a newer one in the same lane was sent before it started. Listings and sizes go in `Lane::List`, a sound's picture and a project's facts in `Lane::Detail`, so moving quickly through folders or down a long list runs only the newest.
- A listing or sizes job that takes more than 1.5 s says so in the log (`BrowseAction::Note`: `browser: listed Samples / ... (N items) in S s`), so a slow stick shows in the next round's report.

## Instrument presets

`Instruments/` holds one folder per engine, named as the engine shows it (`Engine::name`: Poly, FM, Keys, Strings ...), with that engine's user presets as `<name>.preset` text files (`Sound::to_text`, with its effects and macros). The app turns a sound into preset text and back and does no file work of its own: `Library::save_preset` (`mpfree-library/src/preset.rs`, the worker's `Job::SavePreset`) makes the engine's folder if needed and writes whole or nothing; a name already there comes back as `PresetSave::Taken`, and the browser asks before saving again with `replace`, which moves the old file to the Trash first. Load on a preset sends `LoadPreset { loc, path, engine }`; in the full browser too, it goes on the Synth tab's sound if it is that engine's (else the screen says why).

A `.mid`, `.midi` or `.smf` file is `Kind::Midi` ("MIDI file"), wherever it is (Patterns, an export's folder in Recordings ...): Load sends `LoadMidi { loc, path }`, and the app reads it and puts its tracks on the armed track and the ones after it as clips ([MIDI file import](midi-io.md#midi-file-import)).

## Projects

A project is one folder, complete in itself, readable on any computer.

- `project.txt`: `mpfree-project 1`, then `name`, `tempo`, `created`, `saved`, `app`. Lines it does not know are kept. The pad player adds `format = 2` (what its parts mean together; no line is format 1, from before instruments had ids: [instruments.md](instruments.md#2a-as-built)) and `swing`.
- `parts/<owner>.txt`: one text file per engine. An engine implements `mpfree_library::Part` (`owner`, `save`, `load`). The browser never reads a part. A part no engine claims is kept when the project is saved again (`Snapshot::keep_unknown`).
- `samples/`: every sound the project uses, copied in and named by a 128-bit hash of its bytes and its length, so the same sound is stored once and a project never misses a sample because a drive is gone. Two different sounds that hash the same are told apart by comparing bytes.
- `versions/<id>/`: the last 10 saves before this one, each with its `project.txt` and `parts/`. **Earlier saves** on a project lists them, newest first, by when they were saved. Open on one gives its parts to the app in place of the project's (it asks first over unsaved work) and marks the work unsaved; the next save makes it the newest, and the save it replaces becomes an earlier save, so nothing is lost. An earlier save's row only opens: Delete, Rename and the rest do nothing there.
- A save writes the new parts to `.parts-new/`, syncs them, moves the old `parts/` into `versions/`, renames the new parts into place, then replaces `project.txt` through a `.part` file. Opening a project finishes or undoes a save that was cut off.
- The Projects place's list has **New project**, **Save as** and **Save** at the top (pickers never show them). Save asks the app for its parts (`BrowseAction::SaveRequested`); Save as asks for a name first; New project asks first over unsaved work (`Confirm::NewProject`), forgets the project and sends `BrowseAction::NewProject`. The app says when the work changes (`Browser::set_unsaved`), so Open and New ask before throwing it away. **Export** sits after Save: it asks the app for the song's facts (`BrowseAction::ExportAsked`) and opens the Export panel over the browser (`Browser::open_export`, `mpfree_browser_ui::export`); the export itself is [export.md](export.md#the-apps-part).
- **Work folders.** `Library::work_dir(owner)` is `library/.work/<owner>/` (made when missing, never shown: it is not a place): where an engine keeps the files of work not saved yet, such as an audio track's takes, on the library's own drive. A save copies them in with `NewSample::file(name, path)` (read without following a link, written as any other sample), so the project keeps its own copy. `Library::clear_work(owner)` removes the files in it (not links or folders), for New project or an open over unsaved work.

### The parts

The pad player's parts, one file each in `parts/`. Each is text: a first line naming its format and version, then lines a reader skips when it doesn't know them, so an older build opens a newer project as far as it can. A part a build can't read at all (a newer version, or a damaged file) stops the open before anything changes, and so does a newer project format or a `needs` line naming something the build hasn't got ([Project safety](project-safety.md#opening-is-all-or-nothing-01-as-built)). Code: `mpfree-app/src/project.rs`, `mpfree-seq/src/save.rs`.

| Owner | Engine | Format | What it holds |
|---|---|---|---|

Then the scenes (names, mutes, follow, stored values), the song's own clips (`songclip`, clips only the song plays, with their ids) and the song (placements, each naming its clip by id as `clip <id>`, markers, morphs, song lanes; an `mpfree-clips 1` part's `slot` and `own` placements open as the clip in that row and as a song clip: [arrange.md](arrange.md#the-model-mpfree-seq)). `Project::check` runs on what was read. |
| `mixer` | the mixer (`mpfree-fx`) | `mpfree-mixer 1` | `padmutes`, `padsolos` (the kit's pads soloed, only when any is), a `lent <strip> <kit> <pad>` line for each pad channel lent to a pad (`lent ps16 1 20`: kit 2's B5, numbered from 0), `track1 <strip>` (where track 1 plays: `t0`, Drums, or `t4`, its old own channel; a project saved before sampler R3 part 4b has none: see [sampler-architecture.md](sampler-architecture.md#kits-on-tracks-the-pad-player)), each strip's controls (`strip t0 1=-6 2=0.25 ...`), and each insert in order: `insert <strip> <uid> <on> <node id> [key=<strip>] [extras]`, its `param` lines and its `state` (base64, from `Node::save_state`). An older build skips the `sound` lines and the effects' and macros' params, so the track plays the plain engine; a design this build can't make (an effect it hasn't got) plays the plain engine too, said. Written only while a track has one; no part (New, or an older project) is every track on the kit. |
| `track-kits` | what each track plays (`sampler::TrackKits`) | `mpfree-track-kits 2` (version 1, from before instruments had ids, said `synth` for the shared synth and is still read) | A `track <n> kit <k>` line for each track that plays another kit than kit 1, and `track <n> instrument <id>` for one that plays an instrument of the project's list (the shared synth is instrument 1, or another track's own instrument, written beside the track's kit line: [instruments.md](instruments.md#4a-as-built)), numbered from 1. A track whose instrument isn't listed plays its kit, said. Written only once a track plays another kit than kit 1; no part is every track on kit 1 ([sampler-architecture.md](sampler-architecture.md#kits-on-tracks-the-pad-player)). A part with a line that doesn't read stops the open. |
| `instrument-list` | the project's instruments (`instrument_list.rs`) | `mpfree-instrument-list 1` | `next <id>` (the id the next instrument gets, so none is given twice), then `instrument <id> <kind>` for each: `instrument 1 shared-synth`, and `instrument <id> own track <n>` for each track's own instrument (its sound stays in `instruments`, under its track). Each port's input and thru are the rig's, kept for every project, and an open leaves them (`MidiSetup::set_project`). No part (New, or an older project) is the defaults (`Player::open_midi`). |
| `looper` | the looper (`looping.rs`) | `mpfree-looper 1` | Each loop with its settings and its layers' mixes (`mpfree_looper::save`); each layer's audio goes in `samples/` as a WAV named for its content (`loop-<128-bit id>.wav`), written only when missing. |
| `audiotracks` | the audio tracks (`audiotracks.rs`, `mpfree-audiotracks`) | `mpfree-audiotracks 1` | The rate, each track kept (`track=N input= monitor= level= pan= muted= solo=`) with its regions (`region start= end= take= src= gain= fade_in= fade_out=`), and each take's place (`take=N track= group= pass= bpm= anchor= frames= channels= file=`), as `mpfree_audiotracks::save` writes them. Each take's audio goes in `samples/` as a 32-bit float WAV (mono or stereo, as recorded) named for its content (`take-<128-bit id>.wav`, the channels counted in), written only when missing. No part (an older project) is no audio tracks: they are emptied, as for New project. |
| `arp` | the arps (`app/arp/part.rs`) | `mpfree-arp 1` | A `track N` block for the armed track and each track whose arp isn't the default (a track left out opens with the default; a project can have 128 tracks), `key = value` lines: the panel's `mode`, what the arp `plays` while its track isn't armed (what a through-arp track's clips hear), `rate` (also Note Repeat's), every `mpfree_arp::Settings` field (order, octaves, gate, swing, velocity, accent, snap, chord, run, turns, strum, retrigger, repeats, ramp, start), `scale` by name and `root`, `swing-from`, and the pattern as it plays (`pattern`, `Pattern::to_text`) with the library pattern it came from (`pattern-name`). Latch isn't kept. No part (New, or an older project) is every arp at its default. |
| `perform` | the Perform set (`perform_pads.rs`) | `mpfree-perform 1` | A `page <letter> = <name>` line for each named page, then a `pad <page><n> = <action>` line for each pad with an action (pages A-H, pads 1-16, numbers 1-based as the screen shows them): `loop-key <loop>`, `loop-mute <loop>`, `throw <slug> hold=held\|latch\|bar` with `start=now\|beat` and `end=cut` when not the default (the next 1/16, ringing out; `start=1/16` and `end=ring` read too) and `on=t<n>` (a track's mixer strip: 0 Drums, 1 Synth, 2 Loop, 3 Audio, 4 on each song track's own channel) or `on=bus<n>` (a submix) when it doesn't play on the master (`on=master` reads too) (the slug from `perform_pads::THROW_SLUGS`, a name a renamed throw keeps), `mute <track>`, `scene <row>`, `macro <n> value=<0-100>` (no value: 100). The part holds the whole set: a page or pad it doesn't name is empty. A line that doesn't read is skipped and said in the log. No part (New, or an older project) is a new project's set: the loop keys on page A, Play's throws on B. |

The tempo and the transport's swing are in `project.txt` (`tempo`, and `swing = <grid ticks> <amount>` among its extra lines).

Effects are made by id through `project::make_node(id, extras, plugins)`: a built-in one through `mpfree_fx_ui::make`, a plugin (`clap:...`) through the rack, in its own process. A track's instrument is made the same way (`project::instruments_load`: an engine at its defaults through `synth::engine_node`, then its `param` lines). A node keeps more than its parameters with `Node::save_state` / `load_state` and names what it needs to be made again (a plugin's file) with `save_extras`. A plugin's saved state is the rack's last snapshot (taken when it starts and every few seconds, `SandboxNode::saved_state`), so a save never waits on its process; its parameters are saved apart and applied after the state, so a knob turned since the snapshot is still kept. An effect or instrument this build can't make (a plugin whose file isn't in `files/plugins/` is named by its file) is left out of the open with a line in the log; the rest of the chain opens, and the track plays the kit.

### Projects in the pad player

- **Save** (SHIFT + BROWSE on any page, or Save in the browser). The audio loop first has the looper read its loops as they play (`LoopHost::save`: a few periods, more while a loop records, with no glitch) and the audio tracks read their takes (`AudioHost::save`: the worker makes room, then the takes are copied a piece a period; it waits while a track records), then copies its engines' parts between periods (`project::Collected`: the tempo and swing, a copy of the clips, the mixer's and mod engine's text, the synth's, the tracks' instruments, the loops and the audio tracks with their audio) and sends them to the screen thread (`ToUi::ProjectParts`), which adds its own (the kits with their sounds, the I/O setup) and gives the lot to the browser's worker (`Browser::save_with`). The worker writes it whole or not at all. While an open is still landing, a save is refused with a line in the log. The parts wait on the audio thread until the screen has room for them (the loops' and takes' audio is never freed there). An open arriving while a save is under way stops the save (`LoopHost::cancel_save`, `AudioHost::cancel_save`; what was read goes to their workers to drop). The screen names each take by hashing its audio (`project::audio_part`, as the loops' layers are) and the library's worker writes each take's WAV a piece at a time (`mpfree_audiotracks::save::write_wav`). The browser is stepped while its worker is busy, so a save made from another page lands and is said.
- **Open** (a project's row, Open or an earlier save). First the whole project is checked and every part read (`project::check`, then `project::opening`); one that can't be is refused before anything changes, and the browser goes back to the project that was open (`Player::open_refused`, `Browser::open_refused`: [Project safety](project-safety.md)). A clips, mixer, modulation or synth part the project hasn't got is New project's (`project::completed`), a missing kits part is the factory kits, and a missing looper or audio tracks part is none. The screen thread reads the parts and builds what is costly there (`project::opening`: the time engine compiled from the clips, the mixer's effects and the tracks' instruments made and loaded, a plugin's process started and given its state), opens the kits itself (`SamplePage::open_part`) and the I/O setup, then sends one box to the audio loop (`FromUi::OpenProject`). The loop stops the transport and every sound, sets the tempo, swaps the time engine in, gives the synth its sounds and options, puts each track's instrument in (a track the project doesn't name is emptied and plays the kit; the old instruments ring out), and opens the mixer over the next periods (`MixHost::open` / `tend_open`: every insert out, the new ones in order, then the strips' controls, the pad mutes and the mod engine), using the mixer's queued edits so no period allocates. The engines it replaced go back to the screen thread in the same box to be freed (`ToUi::ProjectBack`). The looper's layers are read on a thread of their own (`project::loops_open`) and follow (`FromUi::OpenLoops`); meanwhile every loop fades out as Clear does (`LoopHost::clear_all`), and the project's loops go in once all are empty (`LoopHost::tend_open`). The audio tracks are the same: their takes are read on a thread (`project::audio_open`; a take whose file is missing or isn't its sound is left out with the regions playing it, and said) and follow (`FromUi::OpenAudio`); meanwhile every recording ends, and once nothing records the tracks are emptied, every take forgotten (`TrackHost::clear`, `AudioTracks::clear`); then the part goes in (`TrackHost::open`, `AudioHost::load`: the takes copy in over the next periods), and the screen side's copy of each track's settings is read back from the tracks.
- **New project.** Once the start-up effects are in, the audio loop sends its parts as they are at start, once (`ToUi::ProjectDefaults`). New project opens a project made from them with an empty song of 8 tracks and straight swing (`project::new_snapshot`), the loops and the audio tracks are cleared, every arp goes back to its default (no `arp` part), the I/O setup's song settings go back to theirs (the rig's stay), every track plays the kit (no `instruments` part), the Synth tab's options are the defaults, and the kits go back to the factory kits loaded at start (`SamplePage::reset_kits`).
- **At start** (Prefs, `Settings::open_last`, kept as `start-with = last | new`; Last project is the default since the fourth creator round, 16 Nov, when a restart lost the pads the owner had set. The older key `at-start` was written as `new` by every save while New project was the default, so only an old `last` is still read). The browser keeps the project last saved or opened with Recent and Favourites (`project = Projects/<name>` in `browser/lists.txt`; `Browser::last_project`; a project moved to the Trash is forgotten). With Last project picked, the screen opens it once, when the start's parts have come (`Player::open_at_start` in its tick, through `Browser::open_last`, which leaves the Pads tab's picker open); if the library can't open it, `BrowseAction::LastNotOpened` says why in the log and the new project stays.
- **Unsaved work.** The screen marks the work unsaved when it sends an edit (`Player::saw_action`: anything but pads played, pages, kits picked, the Level, the transport but its swing, previews, the UNDO light and list, and the browser's own; a kit's view sent to the audio thread counts only when the Sample page's edit count moved, so an open's kits still loading after the settle time on a slow machine, or a new tempo's stretched copies, are not edits) or an engine reports one (clips, steps, synth, chain and macros, and `ToUi::Edited` when the app sees a Synth tab option or an arp change made with the controls: `App::take_project_edit`, which compares the arps by a hash a few times a second without allocating), except in the moments after an open or New lands (that is the open's own news). It errs on the side of asking: a clip launched counts too, since the time engine reports launches with its edits.
- **The project's and the system's** (Jay, 13 Nov). The project keeps what makes the song sound as it does: the parts above, the tempo and swing, the Synth tab's options and the arps. Prefs keep what belongs to the player, the MPC and the gear around it, the same in every project, which New leaves alone: the Prefs rows, the found buttons, the metronome, the Looper's level and measured latency, touch tuning, the MIDI rig (port inputs, names, delays) and CV tuning, Recent and Favourites, the pattern library. The Level and the line input never come from a project. A template for New project comes later.
- **Autosave** (Jay's pick, 14 Nov: one Autosave project). `Player::autosave_tick` counts `gui::AUTOSAVE_S` (120 s) while the work is unsaved and has been edited since autosave last asked (`autosave_dirty`, set by `mark_unsaved`), and not while a project opens or before At start was looked at. Then it asks the audio loop for its parts as a save does (`FromUi::SaveProject`), marked as autosave's (`autosaving`); `Player::save_project` hands them to `Browser::autosave_with`, which names the snapshot `Autosave of <project>` and sends `Job::Autosave`. The worker marks `project.txt` (`autosave = yes`, `Meta::mark_autosave`) and saves into `Library::autosave_project` (`Projects/Autosave`, or `Autosave 2` ... when the name is a project of the musician's, unmarked) keeping no earlier saves. `Outcome::Autosaved` changes nothing else: the current project, the unsaved mark and the status line stay, and the app logs `project: autosaved into Autosave` to the terminal only. A save the musician asks for meanwhile wins (`Player::ask_save` clears the autosave's mark, so the parts serve the save); parts asked for that never come (an open stopped the save) are given up after the same time and asked for again. A project whose `project.txt` is marked opens as unsaved work (`Browser::open_landed` keeps the mark; the status says when it was autosaved), with no project, so SHIFT + BROWSE saves it as a new one.
- **Setting the last run's work aside.** The app's only way out is the power switch (or a crash), so autosave's project can hold the last run's unsaved work. Before anything autosaves, `Player::open_at_start` calls `Browser::keep_newer_autosave`: when autosave's `saved` is newer than the last project's (`browser/lists.txt`'s `project`, 0 if none), `Library::keep_autosave` renames it `Unsaved <date> <time>` (when it was written; ` 2` ... if taken), so this run's autosave starts a new one. It stays marked, so it opens as unsaved work. The hint line and the Events log say where it went (`Unsaved work from the last run is in Projects > ...`, or the safe start's line after a crash, `crash::screen_line`). If the rename fails, autosave is off for the run, with a line in the log, rather than write over it. Known limits: autosave's `samples/` keeps every sound it was ever given until the project is set aside (sounds no part names are never removed, as in any project); a project set aside stays until the musician deletes it.
- **Audio tracks and the work folder.** The audio tracks keep their takes in RAM today (streaming waits for the stick speed test), so their part's WAVs are written from memory and `Library::work_dir("audiotracks")` is not used yet. When takes stream from the stick, they will live in that folder while unsaved, a save will copy them in with `NewSample::file`, and New project will call `clear_work("audiotracks")`.

browser.save(Snapshot::from_parts(meta, &[&transport, ...]), now_utc);

The sampler's part comes with the sounds it names, which the save writes into the project's `samples/` first where they aren't there yet (`Library::save_project_with`; a sound that can't be written leaves the last save standing):

```rust
let (text, sounds) = sample_page.save_part();                   // SamplePage in mpfree-app
snap.parts.push(("sampler".into(), text));
let samples = sounds
    .into_iter()
    .map(|(file, s)| NewSample::new(file, Arc::new(move |w: &mut dyn Write| mpfree_sampler::wav::write(&s, w))))
    .collect();
browser.save_with(snap, samples, now_utc);
// BrowseAction::ProjectOpened { loc, snap }:
if let Some(text) = snap.part("sampler") {
    let lib = Library::at(data_dir)?;
    sample_page.open_part(text.to_string(), Box::new(move |f| {
        lib.sample_path(&loc, f).and_then(std::fs::read).map_err(|e| e.to_string())
    }));
}
// BrowseAction::LoadSample { path, .. } => sample_page.load_file(path, false),
// BrowseAction::LoadToPad { path, .. } => sample_page.load_file(path, true),
```

`BrowseAction`s for the app: `LoadSample` / `LoadToPad` / `LoadKit` / `LoadFactoryKit` (to the sampler thread's loader), `NewTrackWithKit` (a new track, then the kit into its empty kit), `NewTrackWithPreset` (a new track, then a synth of its own playing the preset), `SaveKitRequested` (answer with `Browser::save_kit`), `Note` (a line for the log), `LoadPreset` (with the engine's folder name), `TryPreset` / `TryFactoryPreset` / `LoadFactoryPreset` / `RevertPreset` / `SavePresetRequested` / `PresetSaved` / `PickerClosed` (pickers; the preset picker's in `mpfree-app/src/presets.rs`), `Audition` / `StopAudition` (the preview path), `ProjectOpened` (give each engine its part), `ProjectSaved`, `SaveRequested` (Save as got a name: call `save`), `SaveLists` (write it to the store as `files/browser/lists.txt`), `ExportAsked` / `ExportStart` / `ExportCancel` (the Export panel: [export.md](export.md#the-apps-part)).

`mpfree-library` and `mpfree-browser-ui` are linked into the pad player, so `tools/mpc-one/tests/test_app_mpc.py` reads them (`LINKED_SRC`) and lists the library's write sites, all in `lib.rs`, `project.rs` and `kit.rs` and all under the library folder, line by line (`LIBRARY_NEEDLES`, `LIBRARY_OPENS`). A change to one of them changes that list in the same pull request.

**In the pad player.** With `--data`, the app sends the screen thread the folder and the saved lists (`ToUi::Library`); the screen opens `Library::at` (which makes `library/` and its place folders when missing) and a `Browser` on the library's worker thread. `mpfree_browser_ui::step` runs each tick while the browser shows and after each touch. Before each step the app gives it the pad picked (`Browser::set_pad_target` from `SamplePage::load_target`, none while the synth has the pads), so a sound's Load reads **Load on A5** and follows a pad hit (`Screen::load_on`); Load to pad reads **Next pad hit** on screen (`view.rs`, `detail_label`; `Button::label` keeps the plain names for the status lines). Its actions: `LoadSample` and `LoadToPad` go to `SamplePage::load_file`; `Audition` reads the file on a thread (`sample_page::read_in_thread`) and hands the sound to the audio thread in a box (`FromUi::Preview`, `mpfree-app/src/preview.rs`), which mixes it into the main out at the preview level and gives the box back to be freed on the screen thread (`ToUi::PreviewBack`); `StopAudition` fades it out over 256 frames (`FromUi::PreviewStop`); `SaveLists` goes to the store (`files/browser/lists.txt`). Presets go to the preset picker's code (`presets.rs`, above). The factory kits are logged as not wired yet. Without `--data`, the browser opens empty and the log says so.
