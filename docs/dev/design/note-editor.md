# The note editor: piano roll, list, tracker and Timing Correct

**Status:** built and tested on the workstation, 2026-11-12; the tracker lens and the Euclidean fill came later the same day. The piano roll plan (approved 22 Nov 2026 05:10Z) rebuilds the roll for touch: phase 0 made its edits safe and fast, its step 1A.1 the zoom, the scrolling, the overview, Snap Auto and the Edit options drawer, 1B.1 the Draw button with the step feel and a finger told apart as a tap, a hold or a drag before anything goes, 1B.2 picking: a box, a held note with taps, handles, the outline and readout while dragging, and the near miss, 1B.3 the Select menu with its filters and the Add to pick switch, 1B.4 COPY + a tap copying notes and pasting them into any clip with their pressure and locks, 1C.1 the Q-Link note inspector, – / + and the wheel's cursor, 2A.1 the lane under the roll, 2B.1 a scale for each clip with Fold, 2B.2 chords: drawing them, their names, Strum, Invert and Spread, 2B.3 the tools: Strum, Invert, Spread, Chop, Legato and Humanize previewed on the roll until Done or Cancel, 3A.1 the Pressure lane, which draws the picked notes' pressure, 3A.2 lock lanes, which draw the Steps' locks along the notes, and marks on notes, 1C.2 pads that pick and write at the cursor, a chord pad's chord as one trigger, and notes heard as their own player, 4.1 other clips faintly behind, lined up where they sound, and 4.2 drum clips with a named row for each pad and PAD BANK. After the plan's 20 steps, 1B.5 brought ERASE + a drag, which erases every note it crosses, 1A.2 the clip's end handle on the ruler, and 1C.3 a finger on a note with the wheel turning its velocity, which finishes the plan's phase 1. Nothing here has run on the MPC.

The note editor shows one clip's notes through one of three lenses: a piano roll, a list, or a tracker. Notes can be picked, moved, drawn, erased, nudged, transposed and timing-corrected, and a step's chance, trig condition and ratchet set; the Euclidean fill writes a rhythm into one sound's row. It is MPFree's take on the MPC's Grid View, List Edit and Timing Correct, with a tracker's view of the steps and the Digitakt's Euclidean mode. The model is in `rust/mpfree-seq/src/notes.rs`, the screen in `rust/mpfree-time-ui/src/editor.rs`, and the pad player's side in `TimeHost::edit` (`rust/mpfree-app/src/time.rs`). The step grid on the Play page ([steps.md](steps.md)) is the other way to edit a clip; both make the same kind of edit.

## The model (`mpfree_seq::notes`)

Nothing here changes a clip. Every function returns a `steps::Edit` (the notes to take out and the notes to put in), which the control thread applies as one undoable take, as the step editor's edits are. A **selection** is a list of indexes into `clip.notes` as it is now. The edit names the notes themselves, so it applies and undoes whatever order the notes are kept in.

- **Timing Correct** (`Tc`, `timing_correct`), on all the notes, the selected ones, or a range of ticks and notes (`Which`):
  - **Start** moves each note's start toward the nearest line of the division (its length stays). **End** moves its end (its start stays). **Length** makes its length a whole number of divisions. **Legato** holds each note until the next one starts.
  - **Division**: the 12 entries of `DIVISIONS`, from 1 bar to 1/64, with triplets.
  - **Swing** (50 to 75 %) moves every second line later: 50 is straight, about 66 a triplet feel.
  - **Window** (0 to 100 %) corrects only notes that close to a line (100: every note). **Strength** (0 to 100 %) is how far of the way they move. **Shift** then moves every chosen note by that many ticks.
  - A start that comes to the clip's end wraps to its start, as the clip goes round.
  - `Tc::correct` gives one tick's corrected place, for recording with Timing Correct on (not wired yet).
- **Edits of a selection**: `nudge` (by a tick, or to the snap's lines: absolute, relative or no snap, `Snap`), `shift` (a drag: ticks and notes), `edit_start`, `edit_end`, `transpose`, `velocity` (by steps), `set_velocity`, `chance` (by percent, kept within 1 to 100), `condition` (each note's trig condition stepped along `Cond::all`, see [steps.md](steps.md#trig-conditions-cond)), `ratchet` (kept within 1 to 8), `delete`, `add`, `duplicate` (after the selection, on the snap), `paste`, `split` (into equal parts), `humanize` (seeded, so the same seed gives the same result), and `pitch_quantize` (to a scale mask).
- **The clip's scale** (plan 2B.1): `Clip::scale` (`Option<mpfree_scale::Scale>`, a clip setting beside `step`, saved as a `scale` line after it: the root's name then the steps up and, if it differs, `/` and the steps down, as the Synth tab writes the owner's scales; `scale_ok` checks a root under 12 and masks inside 12 bits with the root on, and `ModelError::BadScale` refuses the rest). **Only a clip of notes takes one** (`Clip::takes_scale`, `holds` Notes): a clip of pad hits has pad numbers, not pitches, so a scale step would move a hit onto another pad; `check` and `set_settings` refuse a scale on one (`BadScale`), the writer leaves it out, the reader reads one as Off, and the editor refuses Scale and Root there ("a clip of pads has no scale"). **The line is read only as it is written** (`save.rs`'s `scale_steps`): semitones from 0, rising, at most 11, each once, and after one `/` the notes down the same way; anything else under a root it names (`1 4 7`, `03`, `b3`, a `/` with a side empty) refuses the part, as a value out of range does, while a root it doesn't name (a flat, a newer build's word such as a project key's) reads as Off, as an unknown step size reads as 1/16, since a scale changes nothing that plays. An independent review of the line (23 Nov 2026) asked for these three rules, which the first build lacked. Moves go by the notes going up only (`lit`): `scale_move(note, steps, k)` walks from the scale note at or under a note going up (at or over it going down), so a note off the scale counts the scale note beside it as its first step; `scale_steps(from, to, k)` is the steps a drag from `from` to `to` makes (to the scale note nearest `to`, the one nearer `from` on a tie); `shift_in_scale` moves a selection by ticks and scale steps, refused whole if a note would pass 0 or 127, so a chord keeps its shape in the scale; `fit_to_scale` is `pitch_quantize` onto it.
- **Chords** (plan 2B.2): `add_all` puts in a drawn chord whole (or nothing). `chords_of(clip, sel)` groups the picks into chords: taken by start, a note joins a chord while it starts before the chord's first note ends (so a strummed chord stays one and the next chord, starting as it ends, is the next), each lowest first. `strum_of` is a chord's gap (lowest start to highest over the steps, below 0 strummed down); `strum(clip, sel, by, down)` (2B.3) moves each chord's notes `by` ticks further apart (closer below 0, down to together) and sets each note's start to its place times the gap after the chord's first start, lowest first or with `down` highest first (`None` keeps each chord's way), its end staying, refused whole when a note would lose its length; strummed back to 0 a chord is as it was, and a chord asked for no change is left as it is, even or not. `invert` takes the lowest note up an octave a time (below 0 the highest down) and `spread` the note over the lowest up (below 0 the highest down while it stays over the lowest), chords of two (three for spread) notes or more, refused whole past 0 or 127. The chord's notes and names come from `mpfree_scale::chords`: `chord(root, shape, scale, minor)` (Triad and Seventh up the scale's notes as the Chord pads build theirs, or major or minor with none; Sus2, Sus4, Power, Octave fixed) and `ChordName::of(notes)` (the Chord pads' `chord_quality` plus sus2, sus4 and 5, the lowest note tried as the root first, the bass after a slash).
- **Whole-clip edits**: `double_length` (copies the notes, locks and FX moves), `half_speed` and `double_speed` (refused for a clip with FX moves, which can't be stretched yet; they stretch each note's pad pressure with it, [playback.md](playback.md#pad-pressure-goes-on-its-note)).
- **Locks go with their notes.** A lock on a note's own sound and tick moves when the note moves and goes when it is deleted, and a duplicate copies it. Locks on a whole step (no sound) stay on their line, as the step editor keeps them.

- **Tools** (plan 2B.3): `chop(clip, sel, pieces, reset)` cuts each note into 2 to `MAX_PIECES` (16) equal pieces, the last taking what is left over; the first keeps the note's own pressure run (what falls past its end does not play) and each later one gets a new run (`Edit::touch`, at the end of the clip's pressure): the value held at its cut as a point at 0 (when no point falls on the cut), then the points inside it moved to its start, the last piece taking every point after its cut. Trigger groups (#827) as `split` does: each piece of a trigger chopped whole is a trigger on its tick (`groups::settle_moved` for the first pieces, which keep their ticks, `settle_copied_beside` for the rest), so a chord that rolls as one still does at each piece; the later pieces of a note chopped without the rest of its trigger decide alone, its first piece staying in it. With `reset` no piece has pressure. Refused whole for a note shorter than its pieces, too many notes or no room for the pressure. `split` stays as it was (pressure on the first part only). `legato(clip, sel, fill)` moves each note's end `fill` % (0 to 100) of the way to the next start of a picked note after its own (100 is Timing Correct's Legato; a note past that start comes back toward it), rounded half away from 0; notes starting together go to the same start, the last ones stay. Humanize is the model's `humanize` with its seed.
- **Drawn pressure** (plan 3A.1): `draw_touch(clip, sel, points, end)` takes a stroke as (clip tick, value) points in time order, each held to the next and the last to `end`, and gives each picked note it crosses a new run (`Edit::touch`): the note's own points before the stroke, the drawn ones inside it (from the value over its start when the stroke began before it), the value the note had at `end` as a point there, then its own points after. A value equal to the one before is left out (`push_point`), a run left all 0 is none, and a note whose pressure plays the same is left alone. The old run stays in the clip, since a clip's pressure only grows, so UNDO puts the note back with it; once the clip's pressure is past half its room the control frees what nothing names (`Takes::reclaim_touch`, the step sequencer's B4b), now for an editor edit that added runs (a chop, a stroke) as well as for recording, and the editor's picks stay through it. None (refused) when the clip has no room for the new runs.

## The screen (`mpfree_time_ui::editor`)

The editor is the **Sequencer tab's Notes view** (the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign, Jay 18 Nov 00:30Z; layout spec 4m). **Notes** on the Sequencer's transport switch, or **Edit ›** at the top of Arrange's clip grid, opens it on the clip the Sequencer's Steps show (the armed track's launched clip, or its first clip), and the app shows the Sequencer (`App::set_notes_open`, which `time_action` calls with `TimeHost::editing` after every time action). The notes take the page's width under Arrange's transport bar, the Q-Link column at the right (`EDIT_LAYOUT`). The Edit options and the takes are a **drawer** (`EDIT_DRAWER_LAYOUT`: a 250 px column, the Edit options over the takes, the notes narrower beside it; `TimePage::edit_drawer`, the plan's call 2): the **Edit** button opens and closes it, and the MPC's TC, the data wheel, - / + and the wheel's push open it (`TimePage::open_drawer`; with it closed a turn or a - / + only opens it, a push opens it and enters the option). With the drawer open each edit shows as a take, as before. The page shows it only while the Sequencer does (`TimePage::edit_hidden` is set on every other tab, so Arrange keeps its grid while the editor stays open), and **‹ Steps** or STEP SEQ closes it.

- **`Editor`** is the state: the clip's track and slot, the selection, the lens (`Lens`: Grid, List or Tracker), the tool (`Tool`: Draw on or off), the snap (with `Editor::auto`, the division Snap Auto picked), the Timing Correct settings, the option picked, where the roll, list or tracker is scrolled to, the **sound** being worked on (the last note tapped or drawn; to begin with the clip's lowest sound, else pad 1 or C3), the velocity last used (`Editor::velocity`: a note picked or drawn, the lane; the next drawn note gets it), and the Euclidean pattern (`Euclid`). It lives in `TimeHost` and goes to the screen in `TimeState::edit` (`EditInfo`, with a copy of the clip).
- **`Editor::act`** turns an `EditAction` into a `Done`: the edit, a join key (so the wheel's steps of one option make one take), the log line, and how the selection follows. The app applies the edit through the take history, then calls `Editor::applied`, which finds the notes the selection should hold in the clip as it now is (`find_notes`: the clip's notes sorted by start and pitch once, each matched at most once, so a big selection costs a sort rather than a search of the clip per note).
- **Edits stay correct** (piano roll plan 0A.1, 22 Nov). If the play view can't be built after an editor edit, `TimeControl::edit` takes it back from the clip and the takes (`Takes::withdraw`, as a step edit does), puts the editor back as it was before the touch, builds the old view again and says "the edit was taken back". The control half keeps a copy of the clip as the editor last knew it (`edit_seen`) and a revision (`edit_rev`, sent in `EditInfo::rev`) that moves whenever the clip differs from that copy. When something else changed the clip (a recorded note, UNDO, an edit from Steps, a clip moved), `TimeControl::edit_follow` runs before the screens get the new state (in `take_changed`) and before every editor touch: each pick follows its note by value (`Editor::follow`), and if a picked note is gone every pick is cleared and the legend says "the clip changed: picks cleared" until the next touch. A tap on a note carries the revision of the picture it was made on (`EditAction::Note { index, rev }`); if the clip has moved on since, the tap is refused ("the clip changed under your finger") and the screen gets the clip again, so a tap never lands on another note. Picks stay positions in the clip; there are no lasting note ids. With the editing side on its own thread (`--time-thread`, Arrange 2.6b; [time-threads.md](time-threads.md)) the screen's touches go as asks (`TimeHost::act_then`) and answer later, in order and in the same words; what the screen reads lags until the copy comes back, so a tap made on the picture from before an edit's answer carries the older revision and is refused, as it is together.
- **Speed** (plan 0C.1). An editor edit goes into the playing view in place when it fits (`TimeControl::in_place`, as a step edit does: up to 64 changes, no length, pressure or moves), else it compiles once; the status line counts both (`Costs::notes_in_place`, `notes_compiled`, timed in `TimeControl::edit`). A stroke along the lane is shown under the finger and sent once, as it lifts (`NoteGrid`'s `Stroke`, `EditAction::Lane`): one edit and one take a stroke, where it was an edit and a compile at every move. The screens get the clip as one shared copy (`EditInfo::clip`, an `Arc`): the control half sends the copy the editor last knew (`edit_seen`) while the clip is still that, so publishing copies no notes, and `TimePage::update` builds the roll and the Edit options again only when the copy, the revision or the editor changed (`same_edit`, kept in `edit_built`); an update that only moved the transport sets the roll's playhead (`note_playhead`) and touches nothing else, so the playing transport no longer rebuilds 8,192 notes and redraws the options at every step. The bench measures the roll and the edits on a full clip of 8,192 notes (plan 0C.3; [profiling.md](profiling.md#mpfree-bench)): the roll built and drawn (with and without pad pressure), a frame while it plays, scrolled, zoomed and switched between two clips, and a velocity change with its UNDO and a transpose of every note until they play. On a clip that full an editor edit compiles: a velocity change is a note out and one in, twice the clip's notes of work in place, past `IN_PLACE_WORK`. First desktop means: a roll frame 1.5 to 2.6 ms (zoomed out to the whole clip the dearest), a velocity change 0.28 ms until it plays, and a transpose of every note about a second, nearly all in `Takes::edit` taking the notes out one at a time (the step sequencer's code; see profiling.md).
- **Answers and what the roll keeps** (plan 0C.2). The copy of the clip the screen reads carries the answer to the editor's last touch (`EditInfo::answer`, `EditAnswer`): its number (the editor's touches counted from the time engine's start, so a new number is a new answer), the revision it was carried out on, and what became of it (`EditOutcome`): `Taken` with the new revision and where the notes it made landed (the picks after it, from `Editor::applied`, with no search), `Refused` with why (an old picture, an edit taken back, a gone clip), or `Unchanged` (a pick, a tool, a scroll, a setting). `TimeControl::edit` follows the clip once and wraps the touch (`edit_touch`) to fill it in; phase 1's previews wait on it. On the screen, `NoteGrid` works out the tracker's rows, the list's order and a note index once for its notes and keeps them (`Kept`, checked against the notes' count and where they are kept, the clip's length, the bar and the step, and dropped when the grid is shown again; `NoteGrid::forget` after changing notes in place). The index (`NoteIndex`) puts each note in every bar it sounds in, cut at the clip's end, and `near` gives the notes in the bars a span of ticks touches, once each, in drawing order; the roll asks it only when part of the clip is in view (phase 1's zoom), and takes every note while the whole clip shows or a note is dragged. Drawing, touching and the control list work the roll's places out once (`note_rect_in`), not once a note.
- **The view** (plan 1A.1, `editor/zoom.rs`): `RollView` is where the roll looks: the first tick shown (`start`), pixels a tick (`ppt`), how far the rows are scrolled (`lift`), the row height (`row_h`, 24 to 96 px) and whether it follows the playhead. It is the screen's alone: `TimePage::views` keeps one per clip (track and slot), so going to Steps, another clip or another tab and back finds the roll as it was, and nothing about it goes to the time engine. `x_of` rounds a tick to the nearest pixel and `tick_at` gives the first tick at a pixel; with `ppt` at most 2/3 (a 1/64 is 40 px, `MAX_PPT`) each pixel holds at least one tick, so the two turn into each other exactly, and `cell_at` gives the tick and row under a touch. `clamp` keeps the view inside the clip (time from the whole clip to `MAX_PPT`; rows within the keys). It opens (`RollView::open`, once the roll has a size) with 40 px rows, as tall as fits the clip's notes up to 64 px (`ROW_FILL`), from the lowest note when they don't fit; the whole clip when a 1/16 would still be 24 px wide, else two bars from the first note's bar. `zoom_time` and `zoom_rows` zoom round a tick or row kept under a pixel, `fit` shows a span of ticks and notes (Fit: the picked notes, or all), and `follow_to` pages to the playhead.
- **Snap Auto** (plan 1A.1): the snap's two first settings, **Auto** and **Auto T** (`SNAP_AUTO`, `SNAP_AUTO_T`, Auto by default), follow the zoom. `zoom::auto_division` picks the finest division whose steps are at least 28 px (`AUTO_MIN_PX`, about 3 mm on the MPC's screen) and goes finer only once the finer one is 35 px (`AUTO_FINER_PX`), so a small zoom doesn't flip it, from the straight or the triplet ladder. The screen works it out and asks the editor for it (`EditAction::AutoSnap`) only when the division changes and no finger is down (`TimePage::asks`, sent from `gui.tick`); the editor keeps it in `Editor::auto`, so the time engine never learns the view. The snap button names it ("Auto · 1/16"). With Auto a drag moves by whole snaps and keeps each note's offset from its line (`Snap::Rel`); with a fixed snap the dragged note lands on a line. Timing Correct puts notes on the grid.
- **The piano roll** (`NoteGrid`): a key column, a ruler with bar numbers, the notes as bars (the selected ones lighter), the playhead, and a lane under it (below). A note played with pad pressure shows it as a lighter contour along its bottom (the note's colour mixed half with the text colour): from each value's tick to the next one's, or to the note's end, as high as the value (127 fills the bar). Values past the note's end, which never play, aren't drawn. Notes are drawn as they will play (plan 0A.2): an off note (`NoteBox::on` false, a step tapped off) is only an outline in the track colour, with no fill and no pressure, and a faint bar in the lane; a ratchet's hits are drawn apart over the note's length, each shaded by its own velocity (`NoteGrid::hit_rects`, `mpfree_seq::ramped`, a 1 px gap between hits 5 px or wider, hits past the clip's end left out), with a thinner bar in the Velocity lane for each later hit; a tie has a short level stub just before its start and a slide a short rising slant (`paint_join`), kept off the key column. The colours are the theme's and the track's only. `note_grid` gives the grid only the runs its notes name (`NoteGrid::touch`, each `NoteBox::touch` a run of it), not the runs the clip keeps for notes taken out. A clip of pad hits shows a row for each of the kit's pads instead of keys (see Drum clips below). The roll draws only the rows, lines and notes in view, clipped to the key column, the roll and the ruler; the ruler numbers every bar, or every 2nd, 4th ... when bars get narrow, and marks beats once they are 48 px wide, and a line marks the clip's end. SHIFT + wheel scrolls the roll's rows (`TimePage::scroll`, screen-side) or the list.
- **The list**: one row per note in time order (#, position as bar.beat.tick, note name, velocity, length, chance), the selected rows lit.
- **The tracker**: one row per step, empty steps included, so the clip reads top to bottom as a tracker's pattern does (`track_rows`, `TrackRow`). The steps are the snap's (`Editor::grid`, `step_grid`); with the snap off or finer than 1/32, which a step grid can't hold, they are 1/16s, and the legend says so ("tracker · 1/16 steps (snap Off)"). A step's notes follow on rows of their own, lowest sound first; a note belongs to the step whose line is nearest, as on the step grid (`Grid::place`). The columns: the step as bar.beat.step (the step only when steps are shorter than a beat), the note or pad, velocity, length, nudge in ticks from the line, chance, condition, ratchet and the number of locks (the row's own on its step, and the whole step's on its first row; an empty step shows its trigless locks). A setting at its default shows as a faint dot, so what is set stands out. A ratchet with a ramp shows it ("×4 -40"); an off note's row is faint as an empty step's, and the note's name says "off", and "tie" or "slide" for a joined note (`NoteGrid::note_name`, used by the list too, whose off rows are faint). Rows are 40 px, as the list's (plan call 7: a fingertip's size), and the whole row's width picks it; the step on a beat is shaded, a bar starts with a line, and the playing step has a green mark. SHIFT + wheel scrolls it, and its hint line says so (`edit_hint`: the roll, the list and the tracker each have their own). Opening it scrolls to the first picked note.
- **Touches on the tracker**: a note's row picks it (ERASE held + a tap takes it out); an empty step's row gives `Cell { row: PEN, col: tick }` (`EditAction::Step`), which with Draw puts the editor's sound there, a step long, and with Select lets the notes go.
- **Touches** (plan 1B.1): a finger on a note is a tap, a hold or a drag before anything goes (the hand's `Press`). It is a drag once it moves 24 px (`TAP_SLOP`, about 3 mm), a hold once it has been down 0.4 s (`HOLD`, timed by `NoteGrid::tick` from the app's tick through `TimePage::tick`), and a tap if it lifts first. A tap sends `Toggle(index)` on the lift (`EditAction::Tap`): with **Draw on** a note no longer than the clip's step (`Clip::step`, a 1/16 unless changed on Steps) comes out, only the note tapped and never every note of its step and pitch (the plan's call 3), and a longer one is picked; with **Draw off** the note is picked alone. A hold sends `Select(index)` (`EditAction::Note`: picked, keeping the others when it was picked already) and its lift sends nothing. A drag moves the note by ticks and notes, snapped, and sends `Marker { index, frame }` on the lift (`EditAction::Drop { index, rev, .. }`): the picked notes move with it when it is one of them, else it moves alone and is picked after; while it moves, the notes that move are drawn where they would land (`NoteGrid::moves`). A tap on empty space acts when its finger lifts (the hand's `Wait`), so a pinch that starts there does nothing: with Draw on a note one snap long goes in at the last velocity (`EditAction::Empty`), with Draw off the picks go, and a second tap within 0.4 s and 24 px puts a note in without turning Draw on (`Span`, `EditAction::Draw`). With Draw on a finger moved 24 px on empty space draws (the hand's `Pen`): from the cell it went down on to the end of the cell under the finger, at least a snap, never past the clip's end, drawn as it goes and sent as `Span { index: note, from, to }` on the lift. A drawn note stops at the clip's end (the plan's choice). **ERASE held** + a tap on a note (the roll, the list or the tracker) takes it out (`EditAction::Erase`, in the app's `erase_tap`); there is no Erase tool now. On the roll (plan 1B.5) the app sets `NoteGrid::erase` as each finger goes down, as it sets `fine` for SHIFT, and with it a finger anywhere on the roll, Draw on or off, is the hand's `Sweep`: no hold, move, handle, box or pen. Lifted within 24 px it is the tap, `Toggle` of the note on top under it (or beside it with Draw off), the faint note's `Tab` if that is all there is, else nothing, so ERASE + a tap on empty roll no longer puts a note in. Moved further it sweeps: every note in view whose rectangle a point of its path crosses joins `NoteGrid::swept` in the order met (`sweep_along`, a point every 3 px, `SWEEP_STEP`, so one fast move misses none; the notes under the one on top too, as an eraser rubs out what it passes over), drawn filled and outlined in `t.bad` with a readout ("Erase 3 notes"), and the lift sends `Values { index: ERASE_SWEEP, set: (note, 0) each }` (`EditAction::Sweep { notes, rev }`): one edit, `notes::delete` of each note once, one take, the other picks kept. A new picture finds the swept notes again (`NoteGrid::show`); a second finger makes it a pinch and nothing is sent. The sweep is decided as the finger goes down, so ERASE let go before the lift still erases what is marked. While a finger is on a note or drags one, a new picture of the clip finds its note again by value (`NoteGrid::show`, `refind`), or lets the finger go if the note is gone, so a lift never acts on another note; every action naming a note carries the picture's revision and the app refuses it on an old one (`EditAction::rev`).
- **Picking** (plan 1B.2). With Draw off a finger moved 24 px on empty roll draws a box (the hand's `Box`); on the lift it sends `Span` with its index `BOX` plus its lowest row times 256 plus its highest (`box_of`) and its ticks, and the editor picks every note sounding in it in place of the picks (`EditAction::Pick`, found by value, so it needs no revision). A finger holding a note it picked (a hold, the hand's `Holding`) lets other fingers tap notes: each sends `Cell { row: ADD, col: index }` as it lifts (`EditAction::Add`: the note joins the picks, or leaves them), a tapping finger that moves 24 px sends nothing, a finger elsewhere does nothing, and the holding finger's lift sends nothing; a second finger on empty roll while the hold has not yet fired still pinches. Picked notes in view show **handles** while there are at most 64 of them (`MAX_HANDLES`; none while notes move): 28 px wide outside each end (`HANDLE_W`), or hanging under a note narrower than a 1/16 at the zoom (`HANDLE_H` tall, meeting at its middle). A note under the finger wins over a handle. A handle drag moves the start or the end by whole snaps with Snap Auto, onto a line with a fixed snap, draws the notes as `notes::edit_start` and `edit_end` will leave them (`NoteGrid::dragged`), and sends `Stretch { index, to: ticks }` on the lift, plus `START_HANDLE` for a start (`EditAction::Resize`: the picks when the note is one, else the note alone, picked after). While notes move or a handle drags, an outline marks where they were and a readout above the finger, kept on the roll, says the moved note's position and row, or the start and length a handle leaves (`paint_readout`, its text `ink_on` its fill). With Draw off a touch up to 24 px beside a note in its row gets it (`NEAR`, `note_near`, the plan's near miss); with Draw on that place stays empty, so a tap next to a short step still draws. The Select menu (plan 1B.3) brings the Add to pick switch.
- **The Select menu** (plan 1B.3). The Select button opens a menu over the roll, the roll's own (`NoteGrid::menu`, nothing sent): two columns of 40 px rows, 210 px wide, above the button (`menu_rects`), `SELECT_ITEMS` in order: All, None, Invert, Add to pick and Fit to picked, then the filters (from `SELECT_FILTERS`) Same pitch, Pitch range, Time range, Quiet notes, Loud notes, With a condition, With repeats, Overlapping and Off the scale. A tap on an item sends `Tab(MENU + k)` (`EditAction::Choose(k)`) and closes the menu, except Add to pick, which keeps it open, and Fit to picked, which fits the view as the overview's Fit does and sends `View`; a touch anywhere else, the Select button too, closes it and does nothing more. While it is open only the buttons and the items are controls. `Editor::choose` changes only the picks: All, None and Invert replace them; the filters pick from the whole clip, Same pitch at the picks' pitches (the sound worked on with none), Pitch range from the lowest pick to the highest, Time range the notes starting from the first pick's start to the last one's end (both say "pick notes first" with none), Quiet and Loud either side of halfway between the clip's softest and loudest velocity, With a condition anything not Always, With repeats a ratchet over 1, Overlapping a note sounding over another of its pitch (one sweep by pitch and start), Off the scale the notes the roll marks ("the clip has no scale" without one). **Add to pick** (`Editor::add`, a switch; it and the Select button lit while on) makes the filters, a box and a tap add to the picks (`picks_with`: in order, once each); a tap still toggles, and with Draw on a short note still comes out. Picking by Off-scale notes waits for the clip's scale (plan 2B.1).
- **Copy and paste** (plan 1B.4). **COPY held** + a tap on a note (the roll, the list or the tracker) sends `EditAction::Copy` (the app's `copy_tap`, as ERASE's `erase_tap`): the editor picks the picks when the note is one, else the note alone (`Editor::copying`), and the control half keeps `notes::copy` of them (`TimeControl::notes_copy`, a `NoteCopy`: the notes apart from the clip, their pressure and the locks they own, the track it came from by its lasting id with its MIDI effects as they were), so the copy outlives an edit or the clip. COPY held + a tap on empty roll, or on an empty line of the tracker (`PEN`), sends `EditAction::Paste { tick }`: `TimeControl::paste_notes` puts the copy into the editor's clip with its first note at the tap's cell (`notes::carry`, pitches kept, starts wrapping round the clip) as one take, the pasted notes picked after (`Editor::pasted`). Their pressure comes as new runs at the end of the clip's pressure, never naming another clip's. A lock comes when the track has its setting (`notes::same_setting`; the step sequencer thread reviewed the rules, 22 Nov): on the same track always; on another, a lock on its strip, sequencer settings or arp (fixed id tables), on a MIDI effect of the same kind under the same id, and on the instrument when that track plays the same kind of instrument the lock was made for, its control named the same (the step sequencer's F1: `NoteCopy::inst` is the copied clip's `made_for`, else what its track played; `notes::TrackSettings`); a lock on a kit pad, an insert or MIDI out does not, since another track may play another kit and the time engine doesn't know where its MIDI out goes. An instrument lock for another instrument than the track plays now stays out even on its own track, and one goes only into a clip whose own instrument locks are for the same instrument (`Clip::inst_refuses`), since a clip's are all made for one; the time engine's `tag_clips` tags the clip after the paste. Instrument locks left out say why on the paste's line when it is known: "1 on the instrument (made for Poly, Lead plays FM)", or "(Verse's are made for FM)". A pad's own lock stays out of a clip of notes, where it would not play. Pad hits go only into a clip of pad hits and notes into a clip of notes (`Copied::holds`, `CarryError::Holds`), so a kit's pad 3 never lands as MIDI note 3. A run of pressure several notes share (a chord's) is copied once. Trigger groups (the step sequencer's chords that roll as one, `mpfree_seq::groups`): `notes::copy` takes the notes by `groups::copy_out`, so a group copied whole keeps its number and a part of one decides alone; `notes::carry` settles the pasted notes with `groups::settle_new` (not joining), so a group lands as a trigger of its own, with a free number where its tick has its number already, and a tick with every number taken refuses the paste (`CarryError::Groups`). The ones left out are counted and named in the paste's line, said in place of the hint as COPY + a clip's is (`main.rs`'s `time_action`): "1 lock left out, as Bass may not have its setting: 1 on the instrument". A copied lock takes the place of the lock in its place. A clip with no room for the notes, locks or pressure refuses the whole paste and says which; short of pressure room, the room no take still needs is taken back first (`Takes::reclaim_touch`, the step sequencer's B4b) and the carry is made again, once. The hint line while COPY is held says what a tap copies or pastes (`view::notes_copy_hint`, from `TimeState::notes_copied`).
- **The note inspector** (plan 1C.1; the plan's call 2). While notes are picked and the editor shows, the Q-Link column is the note inspector (`gui.rs`'s `note_inspector`, after the chain editor's macro banks, an audio region's knobs, step pads held and the arp, before Play's knobs): a 44 px header (`QLinkColumn::header`, `HEADER_H`, its text `ink_on` its fill) in the theme's `accent2` reading "Note edit" over the count ("3 notes"), then four cards in the same colour (`view::note_cards`, `KNOBS`): **Position** (bar.beat.tick for one place, bar.beat of the first and last for several), **Length**, **Semitones** and **Velocity**, each its value or range ("80–104"). A turn sends `EditAction::Knob { k, ticks, fine, turn }` (`fine` is SHIFT): Q1 `notes::nudge` a snap a detent (`Editor::move_snap`: whole snaps keeping each note's offset with Snap Auto, onto lines with a fixed snap, `FREE_NUDGE` ticks with SHIFT or no snap), Q2 `edit_end` by the snap (SHIFT: 4 ticks), Q3 `shift` by semitones (refused whole past 0 or 127, so a chord keeps its shape), or `shift_in_scale` by scale steps while the clip has a scale and SHIFT is up (its card then reads Scale steps, `knob_name`), Q4 `velocity_kept` (every note by the same, the loudest stopping at 127 and the softest at 1) or with SHIFT `velocity_spread` (the gap between the softest and the loudest an eighth wider or narrower a detent, at least 1 at each end, the notes between keeping their places; narrowing stops before the ends meet). **A turn is one take**: its join key is `TURN_KEY` with the turn's number in the top 16 bits, whatever the knob, so `Takes::edit` joins every detent of it. The screen counts turns (`NoteTurn`): the first knob touched starts one (`ToUi::QLinkTouch`, the MPC One's touch sensing), the last let go ends it; a screen touch (`Player::touch`), a pad (`ToUi::PadDown`), a button with rows (`Player::rows`), Q-LINK, or a detent more than 3 s after the last (`TURN_GAP`, for a lost release or a controller with no touch) starts a new one. Q-LINK's macro banks go over the inspector (`chain_page.drives`), as on Play. With no notes picked the Q-Links are Play's.
- **– / +, the wheel and the cursor** (plan 1C.1). On the Notes view the app sends – / + as `RowKey::Arrow { delta, shift }`, since only the screen knows whether the drawer is open (`TimePage::drawer_closed`). With it closed: – / + send `EditAction::Nudge` (as the Nudge row, its join key, so a run of presses is one take; with nothing picked they open the drawer as before), SHIFT + – / + zoom the roll in time half as much again a step about the cursor when it shows, else the roll's middle (`TimePage::zoom_time`, `NoteGrid::zoom_step`, screen-side; Snap Auto follows), and the wheel (`RowKey::Pick`) sends `EditAction::Cursor(d)`: the editor's cursor (`Editor::cursor`) moves a snap a detent, onto the snap's line first, round the clip; on the tracker `Editor::first` keeps its line third from the top. The roll draws it as a 2 px line in `accent2` with a mark on the ruler, under the playhead (`NoteGrid::cursor`; none on the list), and the page scrolls just far enough to keep it an eighth of the roll in from the edge when it moves (`RollView::show_tick`, `TimePage::cursor_seen`; not when a clip first shows). The tracker outlines the cursor's step. On the list the wheel opens the drawer, as before; the push opens it everywhere. The cursor is where the pads write (1C.2, below). With the drawer open, Arrow is `Step` and everything is as before. The hint line with notes picked and the drawer closed says what the Q-Links and – / + do (`EDIT_PICKED_HINT`).
- **Hold and turn** (plan 1C.3; Jay's 17 Nov hold a control and turn the wheel). `NoteGrid` is a `Widget` with a value under the finger: its `held` names the note a press would take, as a `held-note` target (`HELD_NOTE`: an id no target of the roll has, since the `Ui` follows a held target by id and index and a turn's edit moves the note in the clip's order, so the grabbed outline stays on the note held) (`note_under`, or with Draw off `note_near`; not a handle on empty roll), on the piano roll only, never with ERASE held, a tool open, a menu over the roll or another finger already doing something, so a finger holding a note keeps the wheel while others tap notes into the picks. The `Ui` keeps the hold (a tap target, so it lets go if the finger moves 12 px first) and the app sends the wheel and – / + to it from every view (`App::wheel_to_held`, `ToUi::Held`), the push doing nothing. `held_step` turns the hand's `Press` into `Holding` (no drag, no 0.4 s pick, nothing on the lift, and nothing for `show` to lose when the note's velocity changes its place) and sends `Values { index: HELD_TURN (+ HELD_FIRST, + HELD_FINE), set: [(note, detents), (hold, 0)] }`: the first detent of a hold (`NoteGrid::turning`, cleared by a first finger down) names the note where it is now, or `HELD_PICKS` when the hold had picked it and others were tapped in, and marks it picked on the grid as the editor will (with Add to pick, added); later detents name only the hold, numbered by `NoteGrid::holds`. The page makes it `EditAction::Held`, whose `rev()` is the picture only on the first detent, so detents sent faster than the screen catches up are never refused. `Editor::act` picks the note on the first (`picks_with`; the picks kept when it is one of them), remembers the hold (`Editor::held_turn`), turns the picks as Q4 does (`velocity_kept`, SHIFT `velocity_spread`) and joins every detent of the hold into one take (`HELD_KEY` with the hold's number); a later detent of a hold it never saw start does nothing. The hint line says what turns, from the grid's picture as each edit comes back (`NoteGrid::held_velocity`, `Player::retell_held_note`): "Velocity 96 · the wheel turns it while you hold", or "3 notes' velocity 80 to 104". `EDIT_PICKED_HINT` names it beside the cursor.
- **Pads write** (plan 1C.2). The **Pads write** chip (`NoteGrid::pads_write`, 104 px, `PADS_WRITE` tab) sits left of Follow on the overview row, since the head band has no room for it with the drawer open; on the list and the tracker it is at the heading's right end. It is lit (`t.bad`, as REC) while `Editor::pads_write` is on, which is off for every clip the editor opens. A tap sends `EditAction::PadsWrite`. The pads reach the editor from the session loop (`main.rs`'s `editor_pad`, after the pad's own handling, so the pad still plays as always) as `EditAction::Pad { key, hit, notes, velocity, down }` while the Notes view shows an open editor: `hit` is the note the pad played (a kit pad's number on a clip of pad hits) and `notes` the notes its pad map plays (`rows.notes`, a chord pad's chord, from the instrument redesign). With Pads write on, a pad down writes at `Editor::cursor` (`Editor::pad_write`): its hit on a clip of hits, else its notes, each a snap long (`Editor::step`, cut at the clip's end) at the pad's velocity, through `notes::write`, which takes the place of a note already on that line and refuses an edit over `MAX_NOTES`. On a clip of notes a pad's notes are its trigger (trigger groups, `mpfree_seq::groups`): they come with `groups::pad_group(hit)`, as the pad's recorded notes do, so a chord pad's chord plays whole or not at all, and `notes::write` settles them with `settle_new` joining, so a note written again on its step joins what is left of the pad's trigger there and takes its chance, condition and on. Each pad held is its own trigger, as when recorded. A clip of pad hits has none. Pads held together are one chord: the editor counts the pads held (`pads_held`) and numbers each chord (`pad_chord`), and every write of one chord has the join key `PAD_KEY` with that number, so `Takes` makes it one take and one UNDO. When the last held pad lets go, the cursor steps a snap (`Editor::pad_up`), if the chord wrote anything. The written notes are picked, not heard again: the pad itself sounds. With Pads write off, a pad on a clip of hits picks every note of that pad (`"Pad 3's 8 notes picked"`); on a clip of notes it only plays. While the transport records, a pad down writes nothing here (the take has the pads); its lift still passes, so a chord always ends. `TimeControl::pads_write` publishes the switch (the apart mode's `Light::pads_write`). ERASE held + a pad erases its notes, as before.
- **Hearing notes** (plan 1C.2, `mpfree-app/src/hearing.rs`). A note picked (a tap, a hold, a tracker row), drawn or moved (a drag, the Position and Semitones knobs, Nudge, their Edit options rows) is heard once: `Editor::hear` puts the picked notes that start first (at most `HEARD_MAX`, 8) in `Editor::heard` with a new number, and `Done::hear` hears what an edit added. A note dragged across rows is heard on each row it passes (`NoteGrid::dragging`). The screen's side is `hearing::Asker` (`gui.rs`'s `hear_tick`, every tick): it watches the heard number and the drag, thins a run of asks to one every 70 ms (`GAP`), and sends `FromUi::Hear(Ask)`: the track, whether the clip holds pad hits, and each note's pitch or pad, velocity and length in seconds at the clip's tempo, at most 1 s (`LONGEST`). An empty ask lets everything go; it goes when the Notes view goes, when the clip changes and when a drag ends or is called off. The **Sound** chip (`Hearing`: On, Stopped only, Off; the screen's own, kept for every clip by `TimePage::edit_hearing`) says whether to ask: Stopped only asks only while the transport is stopped. The sound loop's side is `hearing::Hearing` (`main.rs`, `HearOn` as its `Sound`): each ask lets the last go, starts its notes, and lets each go when its time is up, at most 32 keys at once; it also lets everything go when the transport starts or stops. A track playing an instrument hears them as the instruments' own player (`Instruments::AUDITION`, a row of its own beside the holders), so a heard note never cuts the same note a clip holds, and its note-off only ever ends its own; a track on the synth hears them by number, counted with the synth's other holders; a kit track hits the kit's pad, as the Steps' audition does; a MIDI out track hears nothing. Nothing heard reaches the recorder: it goes straight to what plays it.

COPY held + a faint note pastes at the tapped tick (`NoteGrid::ghost_cell`), ERASE held + one says it's another clip's.
- **Drum clips** (plan 4.2). `editor::pads(clip)` is `Clip::holds == Holds::Hits` alone (the instrument redesign's step 5c says which clips play a kit, so nothing is guessed from the notes): such a clip's roll has a row for each of the kit's 128 pads (`row_map`, Fold as on the keys), named `pad_place` ("A1" to "H16", the bank's letter and the pad's number in it, as the Sample page names pads) and the kit's name for the pad (`NoteGrid::pad_names`, "A1 Kick"), left-aligned in a 112 px key column (`PAD_KEYS_W`, 64 on the keys), with a line under each bank's first pad as under each C. - **The lane** (plan 2A.1): under the roll, one lane at a time, drawn with the shared lane widget (`crate::lane::Lane`, the step sequencer's D3; it only draws values and turns a finger's path into values, and knows nothing of notes). `NoteGrid::lane_of` makes its bars for a field (`mpfree_seq::notes::Field`: Velocity 1 to 127, Chance 1 to 100 %, Repeats 1 to `MAX_RATCHET`, Ramp ±`MAX_RAMP`): one bar for each place in view where notes start, starts closer than 3 px sharing one (`PLACE_PX`, a strum's), as wide as the room to the next place (6 to 28 px). A bar sets the notes there that are picked when any are, else all of them, so of notes that start together one picked is set alone; its value is their highest (on the ramp the farthest from 0), outlined when picked, faint when every note is off (or, on the ramp, none repeats), with its value over it (`lane_label`: "96", "50%", "3↑" for repeats that ramp up). A finger down on it starts a `Stroke` (the lane as it was and the notes each bar sets); its moves preview, its lift sends `Values { index, set }` once (`FIELDS[index]`, each note with its new value), and a second finger calls it off with nothing sent. - **The Pressure lane** (plan 3A.1): `LaneKind::Pressure`, the lane's fourth. `NoteGrid::pressure_lane` makes a column every 4 px across the roll (`PRESSURE_COL`) wherever a picked note sounds, as high as the highest of the picked notes' pressures at the column's first tick (0 where a note has none; faint where none of them has any), with each column's ticks (`Stroke::cols`); the lane draws them as a line over a light fill (`LaneStyle::Line`, whose lift returns every column the stroke set, changed or not, since the stroke redraws the line). - **Lock lanes** (plan 3A.2): `LaneKind::Lock(addr)`, after Pressure, one for each of `NoteGrid::lock_settings` (`LockSetting`: the setting's address, name, own value, the values a stroke can set, lowest first, with their words, and whether it is centred), in the chip's order (`lane_kinds`). - **The clip's scale and Fold** (plan 2B.1; the plan's call 10). ### Euclidean fill

The last four Edit options (from `EUCLID_ROW`) write a Euclidean rhythm into one sound's row ([steps.md](steps.md#euclidean-fill-euclid-grideuclid)):

- **Euclid** is the sound it writes: the editor's sound (the last note tapped). Turning it steps the pad or note and picks that sound's notes, so the roll shows which; it writes nothing.
- **Hits**, **Steps** (shown as "16 × 1/16": the steps and their size, the tracker's) and **Rotate** set the pattern. Each turn writes it at once (`Grid::euclid`), as the Nudge and Transpose rows act at once, and picks the sound's notes after (`Done::select_sound`). The three rows share one join key, so a run of turns is one take and one UNDO puts the row back as it was.
- New hits play as loud as the sound's first note (100 with none) and are a step long. Hits that land on notes already there keep them.
- **Selection after an edit**: Duplicate and Draw pick the new notes; a move, nudge, transpose, velocity or length change keeps the moved notes picked; Timing Correct leaves the selection as it was (none after correcting them all).

## Not yet

- A project key, which the plan has as the Scale menu's second choice: there is none to offer yet.
- Hearing a tool's preview: the preview is shown, not played (notes picked, drawn, moved and written are heard, 1C.2). Flam, Arpeggiate and groove come later, as the plan says; Invert's top note down is a turn to the left rather than SHIFT.
- Buttons for split, pitch quantize, double length, half and double speed: the model has them.
- Timing Correct while recording (`Tc::correct`), and a range (`Which::Range`) picked on screen.
- Showing pad pressure in the list and the tracker (the roll draws it, and the Pressure lane draws and edits it).
- The lane's gestures (a stroke, SHIFT for the ramp, the top edge and the fold), drawing pressure, drawing and erasing locks and the tap on a note's marks are built but wait for the MPC usability round before they are called finished, as the plan's gate says.
- Curved moves (FX moves) in the roll: they move with their notes but are not shown. A lock lane for a setting the clip doesn't lock yet is added on the Steps (+ Lane), not in the roll; a lane whose setting no knob turns is left out of the roll. The tracker counts a step's locks but doesn't name them yet (the parameter names live with the track's instrument, not in `mpfree-seq`).
- Other clips behind on the list and the tracker, a Show list kept for each clip after the editor closes, and faint clips that follow a clip's own moves (the arranger's song placements, a clip's own start offset): 4.1 places them by launch and speed only.
- A tracker cursor that the wheel moves cell by cell with data entry from the pads, and the tracker following the playhead.
- A second Euclidean pattern per sound (the Digitakt's two pulse generators) and Euclidean fills of several sounds at once.
- MIDI out locks pasted into another track sending to the same port and channel. Until then they are left out and said.
- Hold and turn on the list and the tracker (the roll only), and SHIFT + the wheel's push on a held note putting its velocity back to a default.
