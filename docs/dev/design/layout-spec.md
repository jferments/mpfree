# MPFree pad player: layout spec

**Status:** APPROVED by Jay on 2026-11-11 at 08:15Z ("Approve, Synth before Looper"). Every thread works to this. Any change to it needs Jay's approval again.
**Owner:** the app thread ("Build app rounds and own the layout"; it was the "Hello MPC" thread until Jay renamed the app mpfree, 22:36Z). Send layout proposals there.
**Shared layout means:** the top bar, the tab set and its order, the Play page's structure, the right-hand column, the pad grid, the hint line, Prefs, and the global buttons (MENU, SHIFT + MENU, STOP, SHIFT + STOP, the Console's chord, NOTE REPEAT, the mode buttons).

## 1. The screen

- **Size:** 1280 × 800, landscape.
- **Every page, top to bottom:**
  - the top bar, 60 px;
  - the time engine's transport bar, on pages that use it;
  - the page's own content;
  - the hint line, 26 px, which says what the buttons do on that page.
- **Full-screen pages** (Loading, Touch lab, Calibrate touch) have no top bar. Any page change or MENU closes them.
- **Touch targets** (Jay after live test 3, 13 Nov): anything tapped answers over its whole visible button, and packed rows (pattern lanes, list rows) answer over the row's full pitch with no dead gap between them. A mute is at least the width of the name it belongs to, never a bare 32 px icon. Where a control can't be that big, the wheel and – / + reach it.
- **Typing a number** (Jay after live test 3: "Warp tempo (and other numeric fields with large range) should have ability to enter a number, instead of only being able to use dial"): a field that can be typed has a dotted line under its value, and a tap on the value opens the number pad over the page, under the top bar (keys 0 to 9, the point and the minus where the field takes them, Delete, Cancel and a two-row Enter, every key at least 64 px). It shows the range and the value now; Enter out of range keeps the pad open and says the range in red; Cancel or a change of page leaves the field as it was. The wheel and – / + step the number, and the wheel's push is Enter. Fields so far: the Sample tab's Warp tempo (Wave and Warp pages) and the tempo (hold Play's BPM). A typed value is one undo step.
- **Typing a name** (the sampler review's R1: "No names. There is no way to type one"; Jay, 17 Nov 15:20Z, on the `sample-name` screen: "Your text input box looks good. It would be nice to be able to input more symbols though"): a name that can be typed has a dotted line under it, and a tap on it opens the text keyboard over the page, under the top bar, as the number pad does (the `Type a name` page, `mpfree_ui::textpad`). Its head says what is named, the name now and how long a name may be (32 characters; 24 for a port), with a switch at its right when there is more than one name to type (Pad and Sound, 168 × 64 px each, the one showing outlined). Under it the name, large, with the cursor; then five rows of ten places: the digits; Q to P; A to L and Delete; Shift, Z to M, the hyphen and the apostrophe; Cancel (two places), Clear, #+=, Space (three), the point and Enter (two, in the accent). #+= turns the letter keys into symbols (! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) ~ ` = + [ ] { } ; : " < > ? / |), outlined, until it is pressed again (it reads ABC then); a new keyboard starts with letters. Every key is at least 64 px. The name starts as it is, with the cursor at its end, so Delete takes off its last letter and Clear the lot. Shift is for one letter (on by itself in an empty name, outlined while on) and shows the digits as ! @ # $ % & ( ) _ + and the point as a comma; the keys show what they type. A key that can't type (the name is full, nothing before the cursor) is drawn as a button that can't be used. The wheel and – / + move the cursor, and the wheel's push is Enter. Enter with a name left empty keeps the keyboard open and says so in red; Cancel or a change of page changes nothing. Names so far: the Sample tab's pad and its sound (a tap on the pad bar's title, on Wave, Warp, Chop, Play and Grain; the names typed are one undo step), and a USB MIDI device (Name on its port page in the I/O view, 24 characters at most so it fits Routing's columns; the 5-pin jacks keep the panel's MIDI In and MIDI Out; a rig setting, as the rest of the port page, so not on UNDO: typing its own name takes the given one off).
- **Button labels** (Jay after live test 3: "light gray on a medium gray button is no bueno"): a label on a `raised` fill is `text`, or `text_dim` while the button can't be used (`Theme::button_ink`); `text_faint` is not for labels. `Theme::check` holds both inks at 4.5:1 on `raised` in every theme, and the built-in themes are tested against it.

## 2. The top bar

- **Tabs** start at the left edge. There is no MPFree wordmark: that belongs to the startup and loading screens only (Jay, 08:02Z).
- **Status chips** sit on the right: CPU %, RAM % (green under 70 %, amber under 85 %, red above), session, level, tempo, and the arp chip. There is **no kit chip** (the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign, step 4, Jay's approval 18 Nov 00:30Z): what the pads play is on Play's transport (section 4), and the list of kits opens from there and from the Pads tab's **Kit** (section 4e). It was the kit's name and bank, "Classic House · Bank A", from live test 3 until then.
  - Chips never push tabs off the bar, and tabs never push chips off.
  - **The list of kits** (Jay's card on the UI audit thread, 13 Nov 14:57Z), from what the pads play on Play's transport or the Pads tab's Kit, opens over the page: every kit, then the synth (as the wheel steps them), then **New kit** while one of the empty user kits is left, which puts it on the armed track (sampler R3 part 3, Jay's decision 1, 16 Nov 2026; a user kit is listed once a track plays it or it has sounds), beside a card with **Back**. A tap on a kit loads it and closes the list; Back or any tab closes it and keeps the kit. The hint line says so while it is open. **A tap on the Full level or Half level chip** turns that level off, as FULL LEVEL again would (the same card).
  - The CPU, RAM, session and time-left chips only show: a tap says on the hint line what the chip shows.
  - **The "Pads: steps" chip** (sequencer decision 2, Jay's card "Pads become steps, as designed", 16 Nov 2026 20:00Z; mockup I, `step-pads.png` in the sequencer design notes): while STEP SEQ has the pads it reads "Pads: steps · Kick" (the sound's name, then "· Follow" while Follow is on), warn, where the 16 Levels chip goes; while STEP SEQ is held it reads "Pads: pick a sound". A tap gives the pads back, as STEP SEQ again would.
- **The MAKE / PERFORM chip** (sequencer decisions 4 and 5, Jay 16 Nov 2026: Make and Perform as designed; the sequencer design's "Make and Perform" section, mockup K): at the left of the status chips, it reads MAKE, or PERFORM with the second accent colour's dot. A tap switches, as SHIFT + MAIN does, and the hint line says what changed. The project keeps it (a new project is in Make), and switching never stops or changes anything playing. What Perform switches is listed with MAIN (section 6), TRACK MUTE (section 6) and REC's arm. **Rec song with REC** (S4's fourth part): in Perform the take REC records (the notes, their count-in, or a loop's pass) turns Rec song on for its length, unless it is on already; the REC chip adds "+ Rec song" ("REC → Loops C + Rec song"), and the target list's switch reads **Rec song with REC** and takes it off for the next take, or for the take recording (it is back for the take after). **Perform's guard** (S4's fourth part): on-screen buttons that delete or clear (the Song view's Delete and an audio region's Erase, the note editor's Delete, the Looper tab's Clear, a layer's × and ½, Play's loop strip's Clear, the step inspector's Clear, the song list's Remove) need a half-second hold in Perform: a red fill crosses the button from the left, and when it is across the button acts; let go early, or slide off, and nothing happens. The hint line says "Hold to delete". In Make they take one tap, and UNDO brings them back; ERASE + a tap and the wheel's push are as before. Perform's grid and NEXT: section 4f.
- **Width:** up to 6 tabs are drawn at full width (112 px). More tabs share that room, each as wide as its name (about 11 px a letter and 22 px more, at least 72 px), all narrowed alike to fit: the 8 music tabs run from 68 px (Play, Pads, Mix) to 116 px (Sequencer), and with the Dev tab from 61 to 104 px (the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign, 18 Nov; before it the 7 tabs were 96 px each).
- **Active tab:** the page's own tab is highlighted; the Mix tab stays lit while an effect chain is open. Prefs, menus, the macros panel and the guide highlight none.
- **No mixer icon:** the Mix tab took the small sliders icon's job (Jay's card, 14 Nov 07:15Z; before that the icon opened the mixer, Jay 09:17Z).
- **The folder icon:** a small folder at the bar's right edge, after the chips, in the I/O icon's place (the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign, approved by Jay 18 Nov 00:30Z; doc https://claude.ai/artifact/KHBG7mNbDFU4uZCFqNp9dW). A tap opens the browser over the page, as BROWSE does (section 3, Not tabs), and the icon is lit while it shows; the icon again, BROWSE or a tab closes it.
- **The I/O view** is the Mix tab's: the project's routing (audio ins and outs, MIDI ports, clock and sync, CV), saved with the project. The Mix tab's I/O chip opens it, and a tap on the tempo chip opens it at Sync. (Jay's picks on the MIDI thread's cards, 12 Nov 08:38Z and 08:39Z; MIDI and sync are project settings, not Prefs, 08:26Z. The FX thread owns the mixer and its frame; the MIDI thread builds the MIDI, sync and CV sections.)

- **Not tabs:**
  - **Prefs.** SHIFT + MENU opens it.
  - **Each page's options.** MENU opens them.
  - **The Arp panel.** NOTE REPEAT + MENU opens it; it belongs to Play.
  - **The browser.** BROWSE, the folder icon (section 2) or the Pads tab's Browse for (section 4e) opens it over the current page, under the top bar, as the mixer does; BROWSE again, the folder icon or a tab closes it (controls decision 13; Jay after live test 3, 13 Nov 11:46Z; built by the project thread). **Load on and Next pad hit** (the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign, Jay 18 Nov 00:30Z): for a sound, Load names the pad it goes on, **Load on A5** (the pad picked: a pad hit picks another, and the button follows), and Load to pad reads **Next pad hit** (the sound goes on the next pad hit). A kit's Load is still Load, in place of the kit the pads play. Its Projects place has New project, Save as, Save and Export. **Export** (Jay's pick on the missing-features thread's card, 14 Nov 01:39Z: "Yes, in BROWSE") opens the Export panel over the browser: Mixdown, Stems and MIDI file to tick, Stems by (Track, the default, or Channel, with how many times the range plays; Jay's pick "Yes, a Stems by row" on the missing-features thread's card, 14 Nov 15:20Z), the range (Arrangement, or a number of bars with - / +), the File row (24-bit, 16-bit or 32-bit float, and 44.1 or 48 kHz; it starts as Prefs > Export file and Export rate have it and changes the format for that export only, Jay's pick "Both" on the missing-features thread's card, 14 Nov 07:51Z), how it will play and the folder, then Start; while it runs, the time done and Cancel; when it is over, what it made and Show in BROWSE. While the panel shows, the wheel sets the bars, its push is Start (Close once it is over) and - closes it. BROWSE still closes the browser, and an export keeps running behind it. **Rows and kits** (Jay, 16 Nov 05:36Z, PR #410): each row has a star at its right end that toggles Favourites, and there are no Open or Favourite buttons; one tap opens a folder, and on anything else the first tap picks it and the second opens or loads it, as the wheel's push does. A kit is a folder holding `kit.txt` and its sounds; it loads whole, + goes inside it, and Kits has Save kit and New folder at the top. **Plugins** (Jay, 15 Nov 23:36Z, card on the Open MPC plugins thread: "Yes, build it as drawn"; mockups in /mnt/project-files/mpc-plugins/browser/) is a place in the Library column after Recordings. Its list has Catalog | Installed | On the stick at the top: Catalog is the Open MPC catalog's plugins that run on the MPC One, fetched over Ethernet; On the stick is the release zips copied to the stick's `files/plugins/`; each row is the plugin's name, its maker and its state (Installed, Update, Zip on the stick) or its kind. The chips under it are All, Instruments and Effects, with Search and a sort. The Selected column shows the plugin's own screen (Pictures steps through its tabs and the catalog's picture), its maker, kind, version and size, licence, the catalog's CPU figure and summary, and Install (or Install from zip, or Update), Pictures, Favourite and Remove (or Delete zip).
  - **The pattern workspace.**
  - **The button guide.**
  - **System info, Touch lab and Calibrate touch.** These open from Prefs.
- **Adding, removing, renaming or reordering a tab** is a layout change, so it needs Jay's approval. The "Steps, Clips, Looper, Arrange, Synth, Mix" bar in the synth previews is a design mock, not this list.
- **The order is Play, Pads, Sequencer, Arrange, Synth, Looper, Sample, Mix, then Dev.** Jay chose it at 08:15Z, renamed Clips to Arrange at 12:55Z, put Pads after Play (12 Nov 15:46Z), Mix last (14 Nov 07:15Z) and the Sequencer after Pads (the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign, 18 Nov 00:30Z; its last step moves the tabs again, design doc https://claude.ai/artifact/KHBG7mNbDFU4uZCFqNp9dW).

## 3a. The Mix tab

Approved by Jay on 14 Nov at 07:15Z ("Approve as drawn" on the Mix tab mockups: https://claude.ai/artifact/9w1vtAhfGcKmWj81JZdCUG). It answers his ask 7 after live test 3: a place for the mixer and effects, and a clear way to add an effect to a pad, a track or an audio channel.

- **The tab** holds the mixer and the effect chains. **TRACK MIX** opens it on Track Mix and **SHIFT + TRACK MIX** on Pad Mix, both on Faders; pressed on the tab (the mixer or a chain), either goes back to the page you came from. A tap on the tab opens the mixer on the view and the look it showed last.
- **Three looks of the same strips**, switched by a **Faders | Effects | EQ** toggle (246 px) left of the page arrows, where the mixer's CPU chip was (the cost, while profiling, moves to the master's name row, and on Effects and EQ to the title line). The strip views (Tracks, Pads, Inputs, Submixes) and the arrows work in all three; the I/O view is its own. When the view chips don't fit beside the toggle at their 124 px least (the tab beside the Q-Link column), they drop their counts and take 110, 90, 96 and 120 px (EQ look approved by Jay, 15 Nov 21:56Z, "Approve as drawn" on https://claude.ai/artifact/TXEcz7FkcTHqMdDNTFPHVp).
- **Faders** is the mixer as section 4b's strips have it.
- **Effects** puts one panel over the strips and the master: a title line ("TRACKS 1-8 · EACH CHAIN IN ORDER, LEFT TO RIGHT"), the slot numbers, then a 52 px row a strip (a page is the rows that fit, 8 on the MPC), and the master's row pinned at the bottom under a rule. A row is the strip's name over its colour stripe, with which strip it is and how many of its 8 slots are used, then its 8 slots: each effect (its name, a dot: green on, blue keyed with "SC from ...", grey bypassed with "Off"), **+ FX** in the first free slot, the rest outlined. A strip has 8 insert slots.
- **On Effects:** a tap on + FX opens the effect picker for that strip; the effect lands in that slot and opens in the chain editor. A tap on an effect picks it (outlined; the title line names it) and the Q-Link column turns its first four knobs, as on the chain editor; a tap on the picked effect opens it in the chain editor. A drag along the row moves the effect (the slot it would land in is outlined). A tap on a strip's name selects the strip and gives the Q-Links back to it, as does Faders.
- **EQ** puts the same panel over the strips: a title line ("TRACKS 1-8 · EACH STRIP'S EQ · TAP A CURVE TO SHAPE IT", and on the right what is picked, "BASS · EQ · Band 2 picked"), the frequencies over the curves (20 Hz, 100, 1k, 10k, 20 kHz) and "Where it sits", then a 52 px row a strip and the master's row pinned under a rule. A row is the strip's name cell as on Effects, a box with the strip's first parametric EQ drawn as its response curve (±18 dB, the strip's colour, a dot a band in the band's colour, grey while bypassed; the Channel strip's EQ stage doesn't count), and two lines: its slot and how many bands ("Slot 2 · 3 bands"), and its cuts ("Low cut 35 Hz, 12 dB", "Cuts off", or "Bypassed"). A strip without one shows **+ EQ** in a dashed box with where it goes ("it goes in slot 3, the first free slot"; "the chain is full" and no button when it is).
- **On EQ:** a tap on + EQ adds a parametric EQ at the end of the strip's chain, in place. A tap on a curve picks that EQ (its box outlined) and the band under the finger (its dot ringed); the Q-Link column turns the band's Freq, Gain and Q and the EQ's Output, as on the EQ's page; a tap on what is picked opens the EQ in the chain editor. A dot dragged past 12 px moves its band, sideways its frequency and up and down its gain (a cut: only its frequency); a drag is one UNDO step. A tap on a strip's name selects the strip and gives the Q-Links back to it.
- **The EQ's page in the chain editor:** in place of sections, a graph over 63 % of the page (20 Hz to 20 kHz, ±18 dB, the curve in white over each band's shape in its colour, a dot a band: 1 to 8, LC and HC for the cuts) and the band panel under it: a chip a band in use and + Band, the picked band's type (Low cut, Low shelf, Bell, Notch, High shelf, High cut), its Freq, Gain (a cut: Slope) and Q, ON, Solo band and Remove. A dot dragged past 12 px moves its band; the wheel or a second finger sets its Q; two taps on the graph add a bell there; while a dot is held a bubble reads its values. Solo band plays the picked band alone and stops when the page closes (named Listen until the owner's rename, 16 Nov). The Q-Links turn the picked band's Freq, Gain and Q and the Output.
- **The chain editor's head:** a 44 px **‹** at the top left goes back to where the editor opened (the Mix tab as it was, or the Pads tab), then the **strip button**: the strip's colour, its name and which strip it is ("Pad 3"). A tap on it opens the **strip list** in the editor's place: group buttons (Tracks, Pads, Inputs, Returns, Submixes, Master), < > to page a long group, and the group's strips; a tap on one opens its chain (on its first effect, or with the picker up when it has none), and Back keeps the strip.
- **The effect picker's head** says where the effect goes: "ADD TO KEYS · IT GOES IN SLOT 4 OF 8" (the strip and the slot at the end of its chain), "PICK A PRESET OR DEFAULTS · IT GOES IN SLOT 4 OF KEYS" on the presets, and "KEYS IS FULL" when there is no room; holding a tile for presets is on the hint line.
- **The Pads tab's Effects row:** the pad's settings end with **Effects**, a button (no arrows) reading "+ FX" or the effects on the pad's mixer channel ("Compressor, EQ", or "Compressor +2" when they don't fit). A tap, or the wheel's push on the row, opens the pad's chain in the Mix tab (the picker up on an empty chain), and ‹ comes back to the Pads tab. Pads past the 16 with a channel show "—".

## 4. The Play page

Built in round 8 (PR #106) to Jay's approved mockups F to I (10:05Z), with real data and working controls (13:56Z on; side-by-side sheets in /mnt/project-files/hello-mpc/round8-play/compare/). The old big pads are gone, and since 12 Nov the mini pads too: Jay saw no benefit in them on the home screen (08:11Z) and picked "the pattern fills the room" (15:45Z, mockups in /mnt/project-files/hello-mpc/pads-mockups/). The pads live on the Pads tab (section 4e).

- **The frame, in every view:**
  - the transport bar: the state, the position, the tempo, the REC chip, **what the pads play** and the view switch (All, Loop) on its right; on the Sequencer tab, whose Steps share this frame, the bar and step dots stand where what the pads play is, and the switch reads Steps | Notes (section 4m). **What the pads play** (the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign, step 4; board 1's "PADS PLAY"): "PADS PLAY" over a dot in the track's colour and "Drums · Boom Bap · A", the track Play's rows show, its kit as the list of kits names it and the bank on the pads (the PAD BANK lights can't show E to H), or the synth's line ("Keys · Poly · C3 to D#4"); 132 to 224 px, as wide as its text. Too long, the track goes first, then the kit's name is cut, but the bank stays. A tap opens the list of kits (section 2). It took the kit chip's place, and the dots' (the mockup had no view switch): the position and Play's rows say where the bar is. One tap changes the view, and the screen keeps it while other tabs show. **The REC chip** (sequencer decision 1, mockups F and G) names what REC records: "REC → Drums · Clip 1" in grey with a red dot while armed, "READY · PLAY starts → Loop 1" outlined in red while ready, solid red "● REC" or "● OVERDUB" while recording; "REC · PINNED" while pinned, and "Track 1 + Loop 1" with both armed (SHIFT + Arm); Arrange's transport bar shows it as a chip at its right end. A tap on it opens **the target list** (mockup H) in place of the view: a row for the notes and one for the loop, the armed one red with its pill (a tap arms it, pinned or not), the **Rec song** and **Pin the arm** switches, Close, and on the right what moves the arm and the chip's three looks; the chip again, Close or a view picked closes it. While REC is ready or recording, the pages without a transport bar show a short chip in the header ("READY → Track 1", "● REC → Loop 1"). The step dots are 8 px a 16th to make room for it;
  - the view under it;
  - at the right edge, from under the top bar to the hint line: the master bar (66 px: meter with peak hold, level handle, level, LIM when the limiter works) and the Q-Link column (128 px: four cards, knob 1 at the top, each with the knob's number and its target's name centred over the ring with the value in it). Where the target lives (the page, the track, the pad) is not drawn, so the column is narrow and every page beside it wider (Jay, 17 Nov 15:20Z, on the Sample tab: "you could save some horizontal space (and thus make the actual sample waveform display wider) by editing the Q-link display so that it doesn't say "wave" in the top right and then centering "Q1/Start", "Q2/End", etc over the top of the knob's value display"; on every page, by his pick at 15:22Z; it was 168 px). A drag on a card turns that knob, and a finger on a knob lights its card.
- **The views:**
  - All (home): the loop strip (150 px), **Play's rows** across the whole width, then the throws beside the XY pad (280 px). **Play's rows** (the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign's board 1; Jay 18 Nov 00:21Z: Play is a simple step sequencer of the armed track's pads, as on a Digitakt): the pads of the bank on the pads, one row each in pad order (A1 to A16), empty pads keeping a dashed "empty" row with no speaker, as many rows as fit (six with the loop strip) and a rail at the right; a swipe up or down moves them through the 16 (a finger that moves 12 px swipes, so a tap on a step or a name acts when the finger lifts). A row: the sound's colour and name, the pad's name ("A1") and the speaker (the whole name is the mute, as before), then the 16 steps of the page. A tap on a step turns it on or off. **The head**, after the Arm pill: the track (a dot in its colour, its name over "Track 1 · armed") with ‹ and › at its ends, each half arming the track before or after round the ends (Loop tracks have no pads, so it skips them; with no other it says so); the banks **A** to **H**, the one on the pads lit (a tap is PAD BANK's, and PAD BANK moves the rows; a new bank or track starts at its first row); the steps shown, "1–16 of 32", with ‹ and ›; **Follow** (lit while on: the page turns with the playhead; a page picked by hand turns it off); and **Sequencer tab ›**. There is no length's – and + here: the Sequencer's Steps have them. A synth track shows its scale rows under the same head, its banks faint, and a swipe moves the rows' window. The hint line: "Tap a step: on or off · a name: mute · A–H or PAD BANK: the bank · swipe: the bank's 16 pads · ‹ Drums ›: the next track · Loop: the loop view".
  - Beats, now the Sequencer tab's **Steps** (section 4m; MAIN steps only through All and Loop): the pattern (six lanes; up to eight with the strip off), then the Your timing strip (72 px: the average offset, the spread, the velocity and the spread of offsets over the last two bars against the 1/16 grid), then all eight throws across the width. A tap on a lit step picks it for the Q-Links (velocity, length, nudge, chance; Q-LINK steps to its condition, ratchet and nudge in ticks). Play's options have **Timing strip** (On by default, saved); Off gives the pattern the strip's room.
  - **The step inspector** (sequencer decision 2, Jay 16 Nov 2026 20:00Z, "as designed": the Beats mockup A): while a step is picked on Beats, or held on the pads with STEP SEQ, the inspector takes the throws' place (the same 196 px, so nothing else moves), and the throws come back when the step is picked off, cleared or let go. Its head names the step ("Step 7 · Kick", "Step 5 · Kick · held"); at its right, for a step picked, × picks it off and keeps it; before that, Q-LINK's banks: **Step**, **Trig** and **Sound**, the one the Q-Links show filled (Sound in the lock purple). A tap picks one, as Q-LINK does; Sound only while the step is held, since a turn there locks it. Six chips give the step's velocity, length, nudge, chance, condition and ratchet, the ones the bank's Q-Links turn outlined; a step held with no note says its locks play with no note. **LOCKS ON THIS STEP** lists its locks in the lock purple, each with its value and a × that takes it off (one take, so UNDO brings it back), then a dashed note on how to add one. **Copy**: the next step tapped on the same lane takes a copy, locks included (any other tap lets it go). **Clear**: the step comes out, locks too, as one take. Copy and Clear act on one step, so they are dimmed while several are held.

## 4a. The Looper tab

**The Looper tab as an instrument** (Jay, 17 Nov 2026 21:54Z: the Sample tab's principles, not a copy of its panel; design and mockups approved 22:55Z, https://claude.ai/artifact/GjymkKBEUE6mESjyi3oHwV and https://claude.ai/artifact/VHmK33LYRcxB1LKNazwdSd). Every loop is in sight with one touch to record, overdub or mute it; the picked loop is laid out under them; rare settings live in MENU. Built in five steps, each its own pull request; step 1 (the deck, the picked loop and the Q-Link banks) is this layout.

- **The frame:** the deck of loops (240 px) across the top; the picked loop under it (the rest of the height); the Q-Link column (128 px) at the right of both; the hint line under them. Over the deck, the song's clock (a dot a beat, the bar and beat, the tempo) and the loop memory ("1:42 of 5 min"; Prefs > Loop memory, 1 to 20 minutes from the next start).
- **The deck:** 8 identical loop channels side by side, each its name and length, a ring, a level bar beside the ring and a speaker in a box under it. The ring is the loop: its wave runs round it (72 columns), an arc fills as it plays with a tick a bar and a hand at the playhead, and the middle says the bar ("2 of 4"); its colour is its state (the loop's colour playing, red recording, amber overdubbing, grey muted). An empty loop is a dashed circle with + and its source under it (Pads, Line in, Loops); armed, it turns red and says the bar it starts on, or REC on PLAY while stopped. **A tap on a ring is the loop's next step, on the press** (empty: record from the next bar line; waiting: cancel; first pass: end on the next bar line; playing: overdub; overdubbing: end it; muted: play), and picks the loop. A tap on the name picks it without starting anything. **A press and hold of 1 s clears the loop** (the arc drains in red; letting go sooner leaves it). The speaker mutes or unmutes without picking. The level bar (-24 to +6 dB, with the loop's meter) drags, or takes the wheel while a finger rests on it (section 6). One loop records at a time.
- **The picked loop:** its header (colour, name, "Loops A · Verse · 4 bars · 6 layers", the input with its meter, a tap stepping Pads, Line in, Loops; **Send**; **Clear** outlined in red), a ruler of the bars, and **a lane per layer, 60 px, the newest on top**: a tap on the name mutes it, S solos it, × on the picked layer deletes it (on the only layer it clears the loop), a tap on the wave picks it, and a finger resting on a lane gives the wheel its level (SHIFT: pan). Four lanes show; with more, a map down the right marks each layer and outlines the four in view, a label says how many are below, a swipe scrolls them (a finger that moves 8 px before the hold below) and a tap on the map jumps there. They stay where they were left until another layer is picked.
- **The controls the moment needs,** under the lanes. A loop that plays: **Feedback** (Fade old, 50 to 100 % kept a pass; drag or wheel), **Reverse**, **½**, the length, **× 2**, **Undo**, the history as a row of chips (an overdub in its layer's colour, an edit grey, undone steps hollow; a tap opens the undo history in the lanes' place), **Redo**, and **Merge** with its progress. Dragging the ruler sideways nudges a 1/16 note at a time; a grip at each end of the ruler trims by 1/16 notes on release, the part cut off dimmed. Tapped while the loop records, each says why. An empty loop: **Records from** (Pads, Line in, Loops: Loops records the other loops as heard, in place of round 10's Resample button), **Monitor**, **Length** (Free, 1, 2, 4, 8 bars), **Then** (Play or Overdub), and in the lanes' place the input's level over the last few seconds. The line input, the latency, Loop level and Loop memory are in MENU.
- **Send** shows, in the lanes' place, what to do with the whole loop: **Show in Clips** (opens Arrange with the loop's cell in view; Jay's sequencer decision 3), and faint until the looper's bounce is wired, **To a pad**, **To an audio track** and **Save WAV**.
- **Q-Links:** three banks, which Q-LINK steps through and the hint line names: **Loops 1-4** (their levels, the first bank), **Loops 5-8**, and **the picked loop** (Feedback, the picked layer's level and pan, the seam fade).
- **Hardware:** REC records or overdubs the picked loop, OVERDUB overdubs, UNDO undoes (SHIFT: redo). ERASE + a layer's lane deletes it; whole loops wait for Clear with an undo (step 3).
- **A loop on a Loop track** is named by its place (Loop tracks, [loop-tracks.md](loop-tracks.md)): the header's "Loops B · Verse" (the row's scene name, or "row 2"). Play's loop strip puts the same name first in its heading, and the REC chip says it.

On a value (a mixer fader, pan or send, a Q-Link card, the master level, an effect's knob, a slider) it grabs it: the outline thickens in the second accent colour, the wheel turns the value by the same steps as its Q-Link, and the next push lets it go. Values the wheel can't turn yet (the XY pad, waveform markers, the Looper tab's trims, the macros panel's knobs) say "drag it with a finger". On the picked effect in the chain editor the push picks it up (the outline thickens), a turn or – / + moves it a place along the chain per detent with the outline on it, and the next push puts it down (Jay, 14 Nov 12:03Z; section 4b).
  - **– / +** step an outlined value without a push, and move the outline on anything else.
  - **When it starts:** on Play and the Looper tab the first turn shows the outline on the first control; on the mixer, the chain editor and the macros panel the wheel keeps its job there (the selected strip's volume, Q-Link 1) until a tap or a push puts the outline on a control. Changing page, or a tap where there is no control, takes it away. SHIFT + wheel and NOTE REPEAT keep their jobs.
  - **In the effect picker** the wheel turns the tiles, SHIFT + wheel the groups, and a push opens presets and then adds, whether or not the outline is on the chain editor (the FX thread's picker, #315). With the picker shut, the push is the outline's.
  - The pages with rows (Sample, Pads, Synth, Arrange), the menus, the Arp panel, the workspace and the Dev tab keep their own wheel for now.
  - How it is built: the screen keeps the outline, since it knows each widget's controls (`Widget::targets`, from the control audit) and which of them the wheel can turn (`Widget::steps` and `Widget::step`). It tells the app whether it shows one (`FromUi::Outline`), and the app sends the wheel, its push and – / + to it as `Effect::Nav` ([UI architecture](ui-architecture.md#the-wheels-outline)).
- **Hold and turn** (Jay, 17 Nov 18:28Z: "if I am holding my finger on a UI element on the screen, then I want to be able to use the data wheel to control that element"; the proposal approved as written at 18:38Z, https://claude.ai/artifact/93L3BnFbtiRgBWXqAq4LhC). On every page, while a finger rests on a control with a value, the wheel and – / + turn it, by the same step as the push-and-turn (its Q-Link's step); there is no wait. It shows the grabbed outline (5 px, second accent) where the finger went down, and the hint line reads its name and value ("… · the wheel turns it while you hold"). When the finger lifts, the wheel, its push and – / + go back to the page's own job (the outline, the row highlight, the zoom), the outline staying where the finger put it.
  - **What holds:** a drag the widget's wheel turns (`Widget::steps`: faders, pans, sends, Q-Link and macro cards, the master level, effect knobs and sliders, an EQ dot, which turns its Q as before); a whole settings row (`OptionList`, the Sample tab's `SettingList`, not a button row) as its arrows step it; a Synth tab control or section switch, turned as the wheel turns the selected tile; a marker's tab on the Wave page, moved as its Q-Link moves it (a screen pixel a detent, snapped the way it turns). Buttons, tabs, pads, steps, clips and rows that only pick keep the page's wheel. Sequencer steps are the sequencer thread's to give a held job.
  - **Edge cases:** a touch the wheel didn't turn is what it always was; once turned, the lift is a cancel, not a tap (a typed value's number pad now opens on the lift for that reason, and a macro card's half-second long press is ended by the first turn). A value dragged keeps the wheel and both move it; a row dragged more than 12 px is a scroll and lets go. The wheel follows the newest finger still on a control; lifting it gives it back to an older one. SHIFT + wheel while holding is finer where the control has a finer step (the mixer's SHIFT steps, a tenth of an effect knob's step, a thousandth of a slider, a marker's single frame unsnapped); SHIFT pressed before the touch is still MIDI Learn. The push does nothing while held. A list or pad over the page keeps the wheel; a change of page drops every hold. On the Sample and Pads tabs a hold is one undo step (their edit runs end on the lift); the Synth tab and the mixer join turns of one control within 1.5 s, as before.
  - How it is built: `Widget::held` names the control under a finger as it comes down (before the widget sees the touch, which may scroll it) and `Widget::held_step` turns it, `fine` with SHIFT; `mpfree_ui::Ui` keeps one hold per finger and turns the newest. The screen tells the app at once (`FromUi::Held`), and the app sends the wheel and – / + as `Effect::Held` ahead of every page's own wheel job.
- **The transport lights** (Jay, 13 Nov 06:37Z, card "Capture Akai's lights first"): PLAY, REC and OVERDUB light only once a read-only capture under Akai's 3.6.0 shows the message its software sends for them; until then the app sends nothing for them and says so once in the Console. **The capture ran in live test 3 (13 Nov 09:33Z):** PLAY is lit while playing, REC and OVERDUB blink while armed (with PLAY) and while recording, all with values 3 and 0 (controller protocol page, "Akai's transport lights"). Wiring them is the next build. The MPC One's STOP button has **no LED** (Jay, 13 Nov), so the STOP light the app sends shows nothing on this model.
- **BROWSE** (controls decision 13; Jay after live test 3): opens the browser over the page and goes back to it (section 3, "Not tabs"), as the folder icon does. While it shows, the wheel moves through the list, its push opens or loads, - goes up a folder and + into one, PLAY hears the sound picked (it doesn't start the transport), Q-Link 1 scrolls, Q-Link 3 is the preview level and Q-Link 4 the places, SHIFT + wheel moves through the places, and ERASE deletes the item picked (into the Trash, asking first). **SHIFT + BROWSE** saves the project on any page. BROWSE has no measured note yet, so it works once Map controls has found it.
- **Still to come from the control map:** hold MAIN + pad to pick a track; SHIFT + NEXT SEQ (Perform FX); COPY anywhere but STEP SEQ's steps and the Clips grid; TC.

## 7. How changes happen

- **Each thread owns the inside of its own tab or panel.** A change there needs no layout approval.
- **Anything in the shared layout is a proposal.** Send it to the owner. Jay approves it, this file is updated, and then it lands.
- **Previews draw the real bar.** Every crate's preview pictures use the canonical tab list, `mpfree_ui::widgets::APP_TABS` (and `Header::app`), not a list of their own.
