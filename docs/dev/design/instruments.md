## The model

Jay's rule (20 Nov 2026): **instruments make the sound, tracks organize and record performances, and the pads are controllers that play instruments.** A track is a place you record to. The pads, MIDI and scripts play instruments; tracks record what is played and play it back to instruments; every instrument's sound goes to the mixer, recorded or not. So the code names and routes by instrument, never by track, and a track's mixer channel is not an instrument's output.

Before the redesign an instrument belonged to a track: a track played a kit, the shared synth (the Synth kit) or its own instrument (`instruments.rs`), and the pads played the armed track's. The redesign takes that apart in steps, each of which ships on its own, keeps old projects playing as they did, and gives the musician something they couldn't do before:

1. **Try a synth on the pads** (on main): Play on the pads plays the shared synth while the armed track keeps its kit, and REC records it on a track nobody set up ([rec-arm.md](rec-arm.md), `app/pads_focus.rs`).
2. **Instrument identities** (this page, below): instruments get lasting ids in a list in the project. No visible change.
3. **Pad focus** (built, below): the pads follow the selected track or play an instrument chosen for them, which stays whichever track is selected.
4. **Tracks send to instruments** (4a built, below): a track plays any instrument, several share one; each knob has one owner at a time (4b); Duplicate and New instrument track (4c, built); instrument channels in the mixer (4d).
5. **Recording by source** (built, below), with one recording plan behind both the REC chip and REC.
6. **The list of synths and banks** (built, below; Jay took the banks' way in as built, 22 Nov).
7. **MIDI and scripts play instruments** (keys built, below, and routes that name a synth in step 11e; scripts later).
8. **Layers, mutes and stems by instrument** (stems and who plays built, below; layers in step 11).
9. **Audio points** (built, below): an audio track records from inside the mix.
10. **Pad mutes on every kit and bank** (built, below): a pad mute is the track's, on any kit and bank.
11. **Layers, a track's line, routes that name a synth** (built, below): the pieces of steps 7 and 8 that waited for a place on the screen or a way to record.

## Step 2: instrument identities

Anything that must point at an instrument (a track, later a knob's owner, a script or a MIDI port) needs a name for it that stays put when tracks are added, moved or taken away. An **`InstrumentId`** is that name: a number from 1, which is not a track number, a mixer channel, a slot or a place in a list. A project gives each new instrument the next number, keeps that counter in its file, and never gives a number out again once its instrument is gone, so something that named an old instrument can't reach a new one by mistake.

An instrument's **saved definition** (what it is: its id and kind, later its name and sound) is kept apart from its **running engine** (built, loaded and silent, or sounding). The list holds definitions only; engines are built and freed off the audio thread, and the loop swaps them in, as projects open today.

The step goes in three slices, each proven with the shared Poly before the next:

- **2a (built):** the shared synth becomes instrument 1 of the project's list, and tracks on it point at it by that id. It saves, loads and plays exactly as before.
- **2b (built):** the tracks' own instruments join the list, each with its own id.
- **2c (built):** tracks get lasting ids too, so knob owners (step 4) and anything else that must survive moving tracks has something steady to point at.

What 2a and 2b say about older projects opening (format 1, the track kits part's version 1) held until the sampler refactor's step 14 (23 Nov 2026), which made the project format 3 and refuses every older one: [Kits in the list](#kits-in-the-list-sampler-refactor-step-14).

### 2a as built

- **The list** (`instrument_list::InstrumentList`): the instruments, each an `InstrumentId` and a `Kind`, and the next id. In 2a it always holds one: `Kind::SharedSynth` as `InstrumentId::SHARED_SYNTH` (1), the pads' synth, whichever engine the Synth tab plays. Its sound stays in the `synth` part and it sounds on the Synth strip; its running engine is still `synth::Synth`.
- **Its part**, `instrument-list`, saved with every project:

  ```text
  mpfree-instrument-list 1
  next 2
  instrument 1 shared-synth
  ```

  `InstrumentList::parse` refuses a part with a line it can't read, an id listed twice or not below `next`, or without the shared synth as instrument 1.
- **Tracks point at it by id.** The `track-kits` part went to version 2: `track 4 instrument 1` where version 1 said `track 4 synth` (`sampler::TrackKits::text`). `project::track_kits_parse` reads both versions, each with its own word only, into `TrackPick::Kit` or `TrackPick::Instrument`. The opener turns an instrument the list has into the shared synth's entry; a track whose instrument isn't in the list plays kit 1, and the open's log says which and why (`project::tracks_play`).
- **The project's format.** `project.txt` says `format = 2` (`project::FORMAT_KEY`, `FORMAT`), written by every save and by New project. A project with no format line is format 1, saved before instruments had ids: it has no list, and its shared synth is instrument 1, as in a new project. Nothing is said for that, because nothing was lost. A format newer than this build's, a format line that doesn't read, a format 2 project with no list, or a list that doesn't read are each said in the log, and the project opens with the new project's list. The library's own first line (`mpfree-project 1`) is the folder's format and doesn't change.
- **Threads.** The list is read and made on the screen thread with the rest of the open (`project::opening`, `Opening::instrument_list`). The audio loop swaps it in whole between two periods, and its old one goes back in the box to be freed on the screen thread (`main.rs`, mirrored by the test session). A save copies it out as text with the other parts (`Collected::instrument_list`). The loop never makes or frees one.
- **What an older build does with a format 2 project:** it can't read the track kits part's version 2, says so, and plays every track on kit 1; it keeps the list as a part it doesn't know. Projects are not promised to open in older builds.
- **Tests:** `instrument_list::tests` (the new list, the counter kept, broken lists refused); `project::tests::a_project_from_before_instrument_ids_opens_and_saves_as_it_played`, `a_track_whose_instrument_is_not_listed_plays_kit_1_and_says_so`, `the_project_format_and_the_list_are_written_and_a_problem_is_said`; `sampler::tests::each_track_plays_its_own_entry_and_a_kit_lives_on_its_first_track` (the part's text); and through the whole session, `smoke::song_workflows::tests::a_project_from_before_instrument_ids_opens_saves_and_plays_the_same`: a song saved as the build before wrote it, with track 2 on the synth and track 3 on kit 2, opens from BROWSE with those tracks on them, saves as format 2 with track 2 on instrument 1, and after New project opens again with every track on what it played and the same notes.

What plays is unchanged: the track kits a project opens with are the same as before, and nothing that plays reads the list yet.

### 2b as built

- **When an own instrument has an id.** A track's own instrument (`instruments.rs`) gets the next id when it goes into a track that had none, keeps it while the track has one (a new sound or preset loaded in its place is the same instrument, as the slot it plays in is), and loses it for good when it is taken out: a new one gets a new id, never an old one. `Kind::Own { track }` says whose it is.
- **Fixed storage, so the audio loop can follow.** The list keeps an id for each of the 128 tracks (`own`) and the counter; the shared synth is always instrument 1 and needs no entry. Each period the loop calls `InstrumentList::follow(Instruments::held_tracks())`, a bit for each track that holds one (not one only ringing out an old one), and the list gives and drops ids from that alone. It never allocates, so whatever puts an instrument in or takes it out (the Synth tab's Instrument row, the builder landing one, an open) needs no change.
- **Its part** names each one's track, by id:

  ```text
  mpfree-instrument-list 1
  next 4
  instrument 1 shared-synth
  instrument 2 own track 2
  instrument 3 own track 4
  ```

  `InstrumentList::parse` also refuses a track with two, a track out of range, or instrument 1 as an own one. An older (2a) build refuses a list with own lines and says so; the instruments themselves still open there, since their sounds are where they were.
- **Their sounds stay where they were**, in the instruments part, named by track (`track 2 mpfree.fm`): a track has one at most, so the list's track tells which block is which instrument. A track that plays another's (step 4a) is saved in the track kits part by the instrument's id, so the blocks can stay by track.
- **On open** (`project::own_ids`): every track the instruments part gave an instrument keeps the id the list gives it, or gets the next one. In a project with no format line (saved before instruments had ids) that is every one, in track order, and nothing is said. In a newer one, a track's instrument with no id in the list, and an id whose track has no instrument (not in the part, or it couldn't be made), are each said, and the list is put right. A track in the track kits part pointed at another track's own instrument plays it (step 4a, below).
- **Tests:** `instrument_list::tests::a_tracks_own_instrument_keeps_its_id_and_ids_are_never_given_twice`, `the_next_id_is_kept_through_the_text`; `instruments::tests::the_list_of_instruments_follows_the_tracks_own` (with real instruments: a new sound keeps the id, out and in again is a new id); `project::tests::the_tracks_own_instruments_get_ids_that_come_back` (an old project's FM on track 2 and Poly on track 4 become instruments 2 and 3, saved and opened again), `a_list_that_disagrees_with_the_instruments_part_is_put_right_and_said`.

### 2c as built

- **`mpfree_seq::TrackId`** on every `Track` (`Track::id`), and `Project::next_track`, the id the next track gets. `TrackId::NONE` (0) is a track with none yet: one just made with `Track::new`, or read from a project saved before tracks had ids.
- **Given in one place each.** `Project::give_track_id` gives a track going in the next id (or keeps its own if no other track has it), never allocating: `Takes::add_track_as` (ADD TRACK, Rec's new track, a Loop track) and `loops::place` (a Loop track a recording makes). `Project::give_track_ids` gives every track without one, or sharing an earlier track's, the next id in track order: `TimeHost::with_project` calls it, so every track in the time engine has one. The take keeps the track with its id, so UNDO takes it away and REDO brings back the same track; a track added after the UNDO gets the next id, never its.
- **Saved in the clips part:** `nexttrack <n>` before the tracks and `id <n>` after each track's name. An older build skips both lines. A project without them gets 1, 2, 3 ... as it opens, saying nothing; one that has `nexttrack` but a track without an id, or two sharing one, gets new ones for those, said (`project::opening`).
- **Nothing reads them yet.** Tracks can't be moved or deleted from the middle today (UNDO takes away only the last added), so a track's place still names it, and the list of instruments, the track kits and the instruments part keep naming tracks by place. Step 4's knob owners are the first to use the ids (their priority), and anything that lets tracks move will switch those parts to ids as it goes in. `Project::track_with(id)` finds a track by its id.
- **Tests:** `mpfree_seq` `track_id_tests::tracks_get_lasting_ids_that_are_never_given_twice`, `save::track_id_tests::track_ids_and_the_next_id_come_back`; `mpfree_play` `takes::tests::an_added_track_has_a_lasting_id_that_undo_and_redo_keep`; `project::tests::tracks_get_lasting_ids_as_a_project_opens`; the whole-session test above also checks that an old song's tracks get 1, 2, 3 ... and keep them through a save and an open.

## Kits in the list (sampler refactor step 14)

The sampler refactor's phase 3, as revised and approved on 23 Nov 2026, makes kits instruments; its step 14 puts them in this list ([sampler-architecture.md](sampler-architecture.md#kits-in-the-list-of-instruments-step-14)).

- **Every kit place has an id** (`Kind::Kit { kit }`, `InstrumentList::of_kit`, `follow_kits`). The places are fixed while mpfree runs, so the audio loop gives them ids when it makes its list and again after each open; a new project's list gets them then, after any track's own. The part:

  ```text
  mpfree-instrument-list 2
  next 6
  instrument 1 shared-synth
  instrument 2 kit 1
  instrument 3 kit 2
  instrument 4 kit 3
  instrument 5 own track 1
  ```

- **Tracks name what they play by id only**, entry and send alike (`mpfree-track-kits 3`):

  ```text
  mpfree-track-kits 3
  track 2 instrument 3
  track 4 instrument 1
  track 6 instrument 4
  track 6 instrument 5
  ```

  Track 2 plays kit 2, track 4 the shared synth, and track 6 kit 3 under track 1's own instrument, which it sends to (written entry first, then the send, as 4a's two lines were).
- **The project format is 3**, and a project with an older format, or none, is refused before anything changes ([Project safety](project-safety.md)): "it was saved by an older MPFree, which this one doesn't open (project format 2; this one opens 3)". There are no serious projects yet (the owner, 22 Nov 2026), so nothing converts the older parts; the older list and track kits versions are refused too.
- **The Sample page's commands** name a kit by its id here (`ToUi::KitIds`).

## Step 3: pad focus

The design's rule 2: **the pads follow the selected track until you choose for them.** Selecting a track puts its instrument on the pads, as on the MPC ("Follow track"); an instrument chosen for the pads stays there whichever track is selected ("Pads: Poly") until Follow track. Rule 1 says what never moves them: arming or selecting a track, a row of REC's list, REC.

### As built

- **`app::PadFocus`** (`app/pads_focus.rs`): `Track` (a new project's) or `Instrument(InstrumentId)`, in the app's state (`App::pads_focus`), apart from the track and the arm. Step 3 chose only the shared synth (`PadFocus::SYNTH`, instrument 1): the Synth tab's button, while the selected track plays a kit (`App::toggle_pads_synth`). The list of synths of step 6 chooses others (below).
- **What moves it:** Follow track (the Synth tab's button again, or the header's chip, `App::pads_follow_track`); a project opened (its own). Until the sampler refactor's step 15c the arp coming on over a track that plays something else did too (`App::arp_ends_pads_synth`, gone: below). **What doesn't:** `App::set_track` (selecting or arming), a row of REC's list (`desk.rs`, `ArmTrack`), REC (`App::rec_synth_on` keeps it), and since the sampler refactor's step 15b a kit picked for the selected track (it used to make the pads follow the track; now the pads following the track play its new kit, and a choice of their own stays: [sampler-architecture.md](sampler-architecture.md#kits-play-like-any-instrument-step-15)).
- **How the pads play it** (`main.rs`, `preview_on`, mirrored by the test session): once a period, after the kit has followed the armed track, the loop reconciles. While the synth is chosen and the selected track plays something else (a kit, its own instrument), pad hits go to the shared synth on their own, before the arp and the recorder, as step 1's preview did; while the track plays the shared synth itself, they play it the usual way, through the track (the arp, the recorder, the synth's layout). The pads' colours follow, sent once. A pad held across a change lets go where it started.
- **REC** (step 1's rule 3): the desk's `rec_synth` acts only while the armed track plays something else. It points a track nobody set up at the shared synth (`Effect::SynthOnTrack`) and arms it; the pads keep the synth. The press comes again next period, when the armed track plays the pads' synth (`App::plays_pads_synth`, from `TrackKits::on_synth` each period), so it is REC's own, as is every later press there: a second take goes on the same track, not on another free one.
- **The arp** (since the sampler refactor's step 15c): it never gives the pads back. While the pads play on their own, the pads' arp (`main.rs` `PadsArp`, [arpeggiator.md](arpeggiator.md)) plays what they play, with the selected track's arp settings; over a track that plays the synth the track's arp plays it, as before. Before step 15c the arp coming on gave the pads back to the track, and the button wouldn't choose the synth while the arp played.
- **On screen:** the header's **Pads: Poly** chip on every page while the synth is chosen (warn, the shared synth's name from the list of kits; a tap is Follow track, `FromUi::FollowTrack`); Play's transport line reads "Pads: Poly · C3 to D#4" (`PadsPlay::chosen`) whichever track Play's rows show; the Synth tab's button reads **Follow track** over "The pads play Poly on every track". The Synth tab still shows the selected track's own instrument when it has one; which instrument the tab edits is the picker's (step 6).
- **Saved** in the `pads` part, written with every save:

  ```text
  mpfree-pads 2
  play instrument 1
  ```

  (`play track` while the pads follow the track; version 2 since the sampler refactor's step 15b, the pad map with its banks and layers: [browser.md](browser.md)). No part (New project, or a project saved before) follows the track, saying nothing; a part that doesn't read (another first line, a line it doesn't know, two `play` lines, none, or an instrument the project's list hasn't got: before step 6, any but the shared synth) is said, and the pads follow the track (`App::open_pads`). An older build keeps it as a part it doesn't know.
- **Tests:** `app::tests::the_synth_chosen_for_the_pads_stays_until_follow_track`, `the_pads_focus_is_saved_and_a_part_that_doesnt_read_is_said`, `the_arp_never_gives_the_pads_back`; `desk::tests::rec_while_the_pads_play_the_synth_records_it_on_a_track_nobody_set_up` (the second press records on the same track); `gui::tests::the_synth_chosen_for_the_pads_is_named_and_follow_track_gives_them_back` (the chip, the button, Play's transport, the chip's tap); `project::tests::what_the_pads_play_is_kept_and_comes_back`; `sampler::tests` (`TrackKits::on_synth`); and through the whole session, the design's proof, `smoke::workflows::tests::the_pads_play_poly_while_a_drum_clip_plays_on_whichever_track_is_selected` (a drum clip plays on Track 1 while the pads play Poly with Track 1, Track 3 and, from REC's list, Track 2 armed: every hit on the synth, none on a kit or a track, no track or clip changed; then a kit picked and the chip give the pads back), and `smoke::song_workflows::tests::the_synth_chosen_for_the_pads_comes_back_with_the_project` (saved, New project follows the track, opened again with Poly on the pads).

## Step 4: tracks send to instruments

A track's output is any instrument: several tracks share one, and the pads and a clip can play one Poly together. The step goes in slices: **4a** a track plays another track's own instrument; **4b** knob owners (each knob has one owner at a time, by a fixed priority over lasting track ids; the hand wins while it touches the knob); **4c** Duplicate and New instrument track; **4d** instrument channels in the mixer, after the audit of its strips.

### 4a as built

- **A send** (`Instruments::set_send`): a track with no instrument of its own plays another track's. Its clip's notes, the pads and the arp while it is selected, a keyboard's keys, pedals, wheel and pressure, and its clips' locks all go to that instrument (`Instruments::owner`, `Instruments::plays`); the sound stays in the instrument's own track's channel, so the track that sends needs no channel or slot of its own. `holds`, `held`, `node` and `stats` stay about a track's own instrument, so what saves, counts places (16 at once) and shows channels is unchanged. A send to a track with no instrument, to itself, or from a track that has its own is refused (`SendRefused`), so there are no chains.
- **Notes end where they started.** The notes a track's pads started, and the keys it holds by number, are kept by the track that played them, not by the slot (`started`, `keys`, fixed arrays made with the host). Two tracks on one instrument each end their own, even on the same pad moved apart by their transposes. A send that moves or ends first ends the notes the track left sounding on the old instrument. A panic on a track that sends ends only its notes; on the instrument's own track, every note on it.
- **The shared synth too** (`synth::Player`): the notes its pads start are kept by who started them, the pads straight on it (`Player::Pads`: the synth chosen for the pads, a step's or scale row's audition) or a track (its clip, or the pads through its arp; since step 5a two sources, below), in fixed tables made with the synth (`Synth::note_on_shifted`, `note_off_by`, `pressure_at`). Two Synth-kit tracks on one pad, or a track and the pads, each end their own; a note two of them hold ends with the last. The pad's own pressure still reaches every note held on that pad. Found by the step sequencer refactor's review (20 Nov 2026).
- **What ends a send:** taking the instrument out (Kit on its track: its id is gone for good, so the tracks that sent to it play their kits again, said: "Track 3 plays its kit again: Track 2's instrument is gone"); the track getting its own instrument (`Instruments::put` ends its send); Kit on the track itself; a project opening (`Instruments::clear_sends`, then the project's). A new sound or preset in the instrument keeps its sends, as it keeps its id.
- **Locks** from a track that sends move the instrument it plays, kept under its own track (`lock_key(owner, id)`), so two tracks on one knob share the value under it; which of them moves it is 4b's knob owners (below).
- **On screen:** the Synth tab's Instrument row offers the other tracks' own instruments right after Kit ("Track 3's FM", `SoundChoice::Track`, `app::sent_name`), then the engines and plugin synths for one of its own. A pick sends `Effect::TrackSend`; the loop takes the track's own instrument out first (its senders go back to their kits, said) and sets the send (`send_track`, main.rs and the test session). The Synth tab, its presets, a plugin's skin and the knobs then show and edit that instrument (`instrument_track`), titled with its own track. Arrange writes "Track 3's FM" under the track, the Pads tab's Kit row reads "Track 3's FM, no kit" (`ToUi::TrackSends`, `gui::played`), and the app keeps a copy of every send (`App::set_track_sends`). The pad focus button counts a track that sends as one that plays a synth already.
- **Saved** in the track kits part, beside the track's kit (which plays again if the send ends), by the instrument's id in the project's list:

  ```text
  mpfree-track-kits 2
  track 6 kit 3
  track 6 instrument 2
  ```

  (`project::track_kits_text`; read into `Opening::sends`, set by the loop once the project's instruments are in, `open_sends`). A send to an instrument the list hasn't got (its sound wasn't made) plays the kit, said; one to the track's own instrument is said and left out. A step 2 or 3 build reads `track 6 instrument 2` as an instrument it can't play from another track, says so and plays the kit.
- **Not yet:** an export's stem of a track that sends plays in the instrument's track's channel (step 8 makes stems by instrument); muting a track that sends ends its notes but not the instrument's own (mute scopes are step 8); the pads can be chosen to play only the shared synth (the picker is step 6).
- **Tests:** `instruments::tests::a_track_plays_another_tracks_instrument_into_that_tracks_channel`, `a_send_is_refused_where_it_cant_play`, `two_tracks_on_one_instrument_each_end_their_own_notes`, `a_send_that_moves_ends_its_notes_where_they_started`, `a_new_sound_keeps_the_sends_and_taking_it_out_ends_them`, `a_track_given_its_own_instrument_stops_sending`, `a_panic_on_a_sending_track_ends_only_its_notes`, `a_lock_from_a_sending_track_moves_the_instrument_it_plays`; `synth::tests::two_tracks_on_one_pad_each_end_their_own_notes`; `app::tests::the_instrument_row_plays_another_tracks_instrument`; `project::tests::a_track_that_plays_another_tracks_instrument_is_kept_and_comes_back`; and through the whole session, `smoke::song_workflows::tests::a_track_plays_another_tracks_instrument_and_the_project_keeps_it` (Night Market's Track 3 picks "Track 2's Poly", Arrange names it, saved by id, back after New project, and Kit on Track 2 gives Track 3 its kit, said).

### 4b as built

Jay's answer to question 5: each knob has one owner at a time, chosen by a fixed priority, never by timing; when the owner stops the next takes over the way the knob allows; the hand wins while it touches the knob.

- **Where:** `locks::Under`, the table the shared synth (`Synth::lock`) and the tracks' own instruments (`Instruments::lock`) already kept the value under each lock in, now with each track's lock (128 at once per table, beside its 64 parameters). A lock comes with the track whose clip played it (`Owner::Inst(t)`; `Synth::lock` takes the track now). Nothing allocates or locks on the audio thread.
- **The owner** is the locking track with the lowest lasting id (`mpfree_seq::TrackId`, step 2c), handed to both tables every period by the loop (`track_ranks`, `set_ranks`); a track without one comes after every track with one, then by place. It doesn't change when tracks move, and timing never decides. The owner's locks play with their exact value and timing, a better-placed track's first lock taking the knob on its own frame; every other lock is kept with its value (`Under::waited` counts them), not played.
- **When the owner stops**, the next owner's value comes back: on a smooth knob by a glide of 30 ms (`GLIDE_S`, run once a block by `Under::tick` before the instrument plays), on a stepped one (`Curve::Stepped`, `Choice`, `Toggle`: `locks::glides`) at once on the end's frame. The last lock's end gives the value under them back on its frame, as before, and stops any glide.
- **The hand** (`Under::hand`: the Synth tab's knobs, a learned control, UNDO of a turn) is the value under the locks from then on, and holds the knob for 0.5 s after its last move (`HAND_S`), even with no lock on it yet: a lock then is kept, not played, and a lock's end sets nothing. When the hand lets go, the owner takes the knob back the same way, gliding or at once. The Q-Links' touch sensor (`qlink_touch.rs`) doesn't hold it longer yet: the rule for a controller that can't sense touch (while it moves and a moment after) holds for every hand now.
- **The XY pad's hand** (`Under::press`, `Under::release`; the XY controller's phase 2, [xy.md](xy.md)): a layout of yours that plays a synth's knob by Set holds it while the pad does, never timing out. Its value plays and the value under the locks is left alone, so its let-go gives the knob back to the owner's lock or the sound's own value as they are by then (`Under::beneath`). While a track records it holds over the locks (a lock then is kept, and its end sets nothing); otherwise a step's lock plays over the pad and its end gives the pad's value again. A turn by hand takes the knob from the pad (the pad's later presses are kept until it lets go), and a press takes it from a turn still holding it. Stay is a turn by hand (`Under::hand`).
- **Counted on the status line** (added with the strip locks): the locks kept waiting and any lost to a full table, `Under::describe`, at the end of the synth's part and of the tracks' own instruments' part (`synth_status`, `Instruments::describe`). They were counted from the start but not written anywhere.
- **Not yet:** the screen doesn't yet say that a locked knob is shared (the design's last line on this; it belongs with step 6's instrument card); priorities you can set are for later, the lasting id is the written-down stopgap.
- **Tests:** `locks::tests::two_tracks_on_one_knob_take_turns_by_their_lasting_ids` (whichever locks first, the lower id owns; the next owner at once on a stepped knob, by a glide on a smooth one; the last end gives the value under back and stops a glide; a track without an id last), `the_hand_holds_a_knob_then_gives_it_back_to_its_owner`, and the earlier three on the value under; `instruments::tests::two_tracks_locking_one_knob_take_turns_by_their_lasting_ids` (Poly on track 2 played by track 5 too: track 5's lower id owns, its end glides back to track 2's value, the last end gives the sound's own) and `a_lock_from_a_sending_track_moves_the_instrument_it_plays`; `synth::tests::a_clips_lock_plays_on_its_frame_and_its_end_puts_the_sound_back` (a turn holds the knob, then the lock takes it back by the glide).

- **What the Mix tab shows.** The engines' strips, then a track's channel only while in use (its own instrument, or a kit it homes), named "Track 3" whatever plays there (`mixing::name`). - **Saved.** The mixer part keeps each strip that isn't plain by its strip number, with a `track1` line for where track 1 plays (`project::mixer_text_lent`). Instruments are saved per track, with lasting ids (step 2). Opening an old project puts every setting back where it was.
- **Locks.** A clip's lock on "this track's strip" reaches its own track's channel (`OnTrack::Strip`, `track_strip`). (Now it follows the instrument: "Strip locks follow the instrument" below.)
- **Stems and takes.** A stem or a frozen take of a track's channel holds its instrument, with every track that plays it; a track that sends has no stem of its own.

**What follows** (asked of Jay, 20 Nov 16:20Z; his reviewer's answer 16:36Z, which this follows): the strips stay where they are and nothing moves. 160 is the mixer's most (`mpfree_fx::mixer::MAX_TRACKS`) and 132 are built, so the 16 strips instruments without a track will need (step 6) fit under it. A separate slice makes "this track's strip" locks on a track that plays another's instrument reach that instrument's strip: only those relative targets follow (a named strip and the synth's own knobs stay put), the lock is resolved where its track is still known and ends on the strip it began on, and two tracks on one strip follow the knob owners' rule (4b).

### Strip locks follow the instrument (as built)

The slice the reviewer asked for (20 Nov 16:36Z; Jay's go 16:47Z): a clip's "this track's strip" reaches the strip the track is heard on.

- **Where it is resolved.** In the time engine's audio half, as each lock leaves the player (`TimeAudio::send_to_mixer`), where the track it came from is still known (`Out::track`); the mod engine further on sees only the address. The host says where a track is heard (`time::Pads::sounds_on`; `SeqPads` asks `Instruments::heard_on`, the strip of the instrument the track plays, its own or the one it sends to). `time::strips::StripLocks` keeps each lock with the strip it went to.
- **Only "this track's strip".** A lock is moved when it names the playing track's own strip (`Owner::Strip(track_strip(t))`), which is how a clip recorded on that track names it (`Target::for_track`). The player gives no other sign of a relative target, and a fixed target only names the playing track's strip when a clip was copied onto the track whose strip it names, where the two mean the same strip. Fixed targets elsewhere (the master, a submix, a return, another track's strip, the old projects' rewritten lanes, which name Synth, Loop, Audio or a strip four tracks down) go on as they came. So do locks on a strip's effects (`OnTrack::Insert`): an effect's number is its own strip's, so the same number on the instrument's strip would be another effect. They stay on the track's own strip, where they change nothing heard, as before.
- **A lock ends where it began.** The end goes to the strip the lock went to, even when the instrument moved in between. A new value for a lock still held after the instrument moved (a move's next point, the next locked step) ends it on the old strip first, then starts it on the new one.
- **Two tracks on one strip take turns** by the knob owners' rule (4b): the lowest lasting id owns the control (`TrackSet::id`, sent to the audio half with the tracks' settings), and the others' locks are kept, not sent. The instrument's own track's locks on its strip are in the same line. When the owner's lock ends, the next owner's value is sent as a lock (the mixer smooths its levels over 4 ms, so there is no glide of our own); when the last ends, the control is let go. A hand on the control is the mod engine's, as before.
- **Not moved:** a track that plays a kit another track plays first, or the shared synth, is heard on that kit's home channel or the Synth strip, and its "this track's strip" still reaches its own strip, which carries nothing. The same rule could take them in (the host would say where they are heard); it is left for the step that gives kits and the synth their instrument cards (step 6), since it would change what the many tracks that share kit 1 do today.
- **Counted:** locks moved, kept waiting, and the ones that found the table (256, the player's own most) full and went on as they came, on the time engine's status line (`StripCounts`, sent to the control half when they change, `ToControl::Strips`, as the clock-follow counts are; the period-end message had no room left under its 128 bytes).
- **Real-time safe:** fixed tables, boxed once with the audio half; the end-to-end test counts no allocation.
- **Tests:** `time::strips::tests` (moved and ended there, only the track's own strip, a reroute mid-lock, turns by lasting id, no id last, a full table), `time::tests::a_lock_on_this_tracks_strip_goes_where_its_instrument_sounds` (from a clip, with an effect lock that stays, the counts on the status line), `instruments::tests::a_track_is_heard_where_the_instrument_it_plays_sounds`, `rt_safety::a_lock_on_this_tracks_strip_moves_the_strip_its_instrument_sounds_on` (track 5 playing track 3's Poly moves track 3's strip's volume and lets it go, track 5's strip untouched, no allocation).

## Step 5: recording by source and destination

The plan's step 5: every note carries its source; REC records the sources chosen into the place the recording plan gives, named on the REC chip before you press; pad hits stay pad hits and own-instrument takes become notes; playing and recording take one path. It is built in slices.

### 5a as built (sources on the shared synth)

- **The key a note ends by** is its instrument, its source and its note, agreed with the step sequencer refactor (20 Nov), whose phase C builds on it. The sources: the pads straight on an instrument (`Player::Pads`: the synth chosen for the pads, an audition), a track's arp (`Player::Arp(t)`: the pads through it, or a clip through it) and a track's clip as recorded (`Player::Clip(t)`). In the time engine's sink the arp's events are the ones its table marks as the arp's (`SeqPads::live`); the rest are the clips' (`hit_at`, `release_at`, `pressure_at`).
- **The shared synth** keeps a row of pads for each source (`synth::Player`, `PLAYERS`: the pads, then each track's clip, then each track's arp), boxed once in `Synth::new`, so a clip on track 1 and the pads played live through track 1's arp, on the same pad, each end only their own notes; a note both hold ends when both have let go. Pressure goes by source too (`SeqPads::pressure_by`): a clip's recorded pressure to its notes, the live pads' to theirs. A kit pick while the pads hold the synth lets go of the arp's notes.
- **Tests:** `synth::tests::a_tracks_clip_and_its_arp_end_their_own_notes`, `tests::a_clip_and_the_live_pads_on_one_track_keep_their_own_notes` (both orders, and each note ending once when both let go), and the finding-9 tests as before.

### 5c as built (clips of notes, playback)

Agreed with the step sequencer refactor and the arrange refactor (20 Nov): the kind belongs to the clip, and travels with every event.

- **The model:** `mpfree_seq::Clip::holds` (`Holds::Hits`, the default, or `Holds::Notes`), saved as a `holds notes` clip line, unknown words reading as hits. The arrange refactor's clips carry it over. See [playback.md](playback.md).
- **The player:** each note event says its kind (`Out::notes`); a note's end, ratchet hits and pressure keep it. The arps keep a clip of notes apart from a clip of hits (`arp::CLIP_NOTES`).
- **The app's host** (`SeqPads::out_note`, reached from `Pads::out`, so the pad methods never see a note): a clip of notes plays wherever its track plays. MIDI out, CV and an export's MIDI file get the note as a note. A track's own instrument, or another track's it plays, starts and ends the note itself (`Instruments::note_on`, `note_off`, `note_pressure_at`), and so does the shared synth (`Synth::note_on_note`, `note_off_note`, `pressure_note_at`), not the notes of the pad with that number. A note-on flashes the pad that plays its note in the pads' layout, while that track's pads are shown.
- **Live transpose (S4)** moves a clip's notes unless they go to the Drums engine or a kit (`SeqPads::note_shift`). - **Through an arp:** a clip of notes through a track's arp came with 5d (below), and the step grid's rows with the recording.
- **Tests:** `holds::a_clip_of_notes_sends_notes_and_one_of_hits_sends_pad_hits` (mpfree-play: the same events, only the kind differs, for any period size), `tests::a_clip_of_notes_plays_the_synth_by_note` (with the transpose moving mid-note), `tests::a_clip_of_notes_plays_a_tracks_own_instrument_by_note`, `tests::a_clip_of_notes_plays_a_kits_pad_by_its_note`, `save::tests::a_clip_of_notes_reads_back_and_one_of_hits_saves_as_before`, and `shift::tests::a_note_remembers_where_it_went_after_it_ends`.

### 5c as built (takes of notes)

- **Which takes hold notes.** The main loop tells the time engine each pass which tracks play notes (`TimeHost::set_notes_tracks`, from `Instruments::note_tracks`: a track's own instrument or the one it plays of another track's, not the Drums engine). A new clip REC makes for a take on one (`TimeControl::take_holds`: the armed track's new clip, `TimeAction::NewClip`) holds notes (since 5d, through its arp too). - **What a live note records.** `Pads::live` returns what it heard both ways (`time::Heard`): the pad, for a take of hits, CAPTURE's history and the Play page's timing, and the notes as written (before the live transpose), up to a pad's four. `SeqPads` gives the layout's note (one no pad has still records), a pad's notes from the track's pad map (`Instruments::pad_notes`), the synth's layout while the track plays the synth, or a kit pad's note as MIDI in and out read it (`SeqPads::pad_notes`). ### 5c as built (the step grid)

The Play page, the Sequencer's Steps, the step pads and the Q-Links all name a pattern's rows by pad (0 to 15), as they did. A clip of notes is shown and edited through the notes the track's pads play, so none of them changes, and the takes above ship with it.

- **Each pad's notes.** The main loop tells the time engine each pass what notes each track's 16 pads play (`TimeHost::set_pad_rows`, a fixed `time::PadRows` from `pad_rows` in main.rs): the pad's notes on the track's own instrument or the one it plays (`Instruments::pad_notes`, a chord's root last), on the shared synth (`Synth::notes`), or a kit pad's note in bank A (`mpfree_midi::profile::kit_note`), as a clip of notes plays them back. - **Edited by note.** On a clip of notes `step_action` takes an action on a pad's row on each of the pad's notes, root first, as one edit with the root's undo key (`on_notes`, joining with `Edit::join`, now public), so a pad of chords steps its whole chord as a pad hit would: a step's change, locks, clears and copies take every note; a tap takes the chord off when its root is on the step, else puts the missing notes on; the Note knob puts the step on the other pad's notes, each as the root was, its locks with it (`swap`); and keys entered on the line are their pads' notes, up to eight. ### 5d as built (one path through the arp)

Before 5d a track's arp worked in whatever numbers reached it: the live pads pressed pad numbers on a track with its own synth (its pad map turned them into notes after the arp), notes on the shared synth (its layout), and a clip went in as it was. So a clip of notes couldn't go through an own synth's arp, and an old clip of pad hits through the shared synth's arp met the live pads' notes in one arp as if they were notes.

- **An arp works in notes** when its track's synth plays notes (`TrackArps::set_on_notes`, from `arp_on_notes` in main.rs each pass: a track's own synth, or the one it plays, unless it is the Drums engine; the shared synth; never while the track's notes go to MIDI out, which plays pads by its profile). What it plays then goes out as notes (`Out::notes`), so `SeqPads::live` plays it by note on whichever synth (`live_own`'s note path for an own synth), whatever the pads show now. A change ends what the arp holds, as the "Clips through the arp" switch does; those endings go out as what they end (`notes_out` takes the new kind after that period).
- **The live pads press notes** into an own synth's arp: the app keeps the selected track's own synth's pad map (`App::set_arp_layout`, from `Instruments::pad_map`) beside the shared synth's layout, and presses, lets go and gives pressure by it (`App::press_layout`); nothing else about the pads changes (STEP SEQ, the kit's banks, the keys). Pads held as it comes or goes are let go, as with the synth's layout. Not while the pads play the synth chosen for them.
- **Clips meet the pads in notes:** a clip of notes goes in as it is (`arp::CLIP_NOTES`); a clip of pad hits goes in as its pads' notes (`TrackArps::set_pads`, the same `PadRows` the step grid reads, now in mpfree-play), each hit remembering what it pressed so its end and pressure reach those notes though the layout changes (`TrackArps::hit_as_notes`). A panic lets go of them all.
- **Recording takes one path.** The recorder only listens: off, it takes what the track's arp played, now notes; on ("Clips through the arp", from a project file), the pads as pressed, which now carry the pad's notes (`TimeHost::pad_pressed_notes`, from `PadRows`), so a take through the arp holds notes too (`take_holds` no longer leaves those tracks out). A clip playing the same synth, on any track and through any arp, never reaches the take.
- **Tests:** mpfree-play's `on_notes` tests (a clip of pad hits into an arp of notes plays its pad's chord as notes for any period size, a clip of notes goes in as it is, an arp of pad hits plays as before, a new layout reaches new hits while each hit ends what it pressed, switching back ends its notes as notes), `time::tests::pads_as_pressed_into_a_clip_of_notes_record_their_notes`, `time::tests::a_take_of_the_pads_leaves_out_a_clip_playing_the_same_synth` (the plan's proof, through the arp and not), `app::tests::an_own_synths_pad_map_presses_its_notes_into_the_arp` and `tests::an_arp_of_notes_plays_a_tracks_own_synth_by_note`.

## Step 6: synths with no track, and the list of synths

The plan's step 6: "The list of synths and bank mappings. The list from PR #715's history, with its Cancel fix, picks for the pads; banks A to H point at instruments." Rule 1 holds throughout: trying a synth on the pads never changes a track. The bank mappings (6c) are built on the recommended answer to Jay's card about where a bank is given its instrument ("In the list of synths"); the card is still open, and its other answers (a Plays button on the Pads tab, or holding PAD BANK) change only the way in.

### 6a as built (free places)

- **Holders.** The instrument host (`instruments.rs`) holds an instrument for a holder: tracks 0 to 127, then 16 free places (`instruments::FREE`, holders `TRACKS..HOLDERS`, `instruments::free(n)`), each an instrument no track has. The pads are a player like a track with no instrument of its own (`THE_PADS`): they play a holder's instrument by a send (`Instruments::pads_play`, `pads_play_at`), their notes ending where they started when it moves. A track can play a free place's instrument by a send too (step 4's `set_send`). The 16 instruments loaded at once count both (`count`, `has_room`); `in_use` and `held_tracks` stay the tracks', `held_free` and `free_in_use` are the places'.
- **Strips.** Each free place sounds on a mixer strip of its own past every track's (`mixing::FREE_FIRST`, `free_channel`; the mixer builds `TRACKS + HOLDERS` track strips, the 16 under the 160 the plan allowed). It shows on the Tracks view and in the lists that pick a strip while in use, and is named for who plays it (`MixHost::own_instrument_on`): "FM · Pads" while the pads play it or nothing does, else "FM · Track 2" for the first track that does, so two places never share a name. `name` calls an unused one "Free 2".
- **The list of instruments** (`instrument_list.rs`) gains them: `Kind::Free { place }`, `InstrumentList::follow_free` (a place that gets an instrument gets the next id and keeps it while it holds one, whatever sound goes in), the line `instrument 4 free 1`.
- **Saved:** the instruments part writes a free place's instrument as `free N id` and its settings, as a track's (`project::instruments_text`, read back by `instruments_load`); the list names it by id; a track that plays it is `track T instrument ID` in the track kits part, as in step 4; the pads part is `play instrument ID` (`PadFocus::parse` takes any id the list has). While a free place holds a synth, `project.txt` says `needs = synths-without-tracks` (`project::SYNTHS_WITHOUT_TRACKS`, in `KNOWN_NEEDS`), so a build without free places refuses the project ([project-safety.md](project-safety.md)) rather than read the `free` block's settings as the last track's instrument's.
- **The app** keeps a copy of the list (`App::set_instrument_list`, sent by the main loop when it changes, and on opening before the pads part), so `PadFocus::Instrument(id)` can be any instrument in it (`App::pads_holder`); an id that has gone gives the pads back to the track, said (since the sampler refactor's step 15b its entry stays in the pad map, missing, and the pads play the track's instrument meanwhile). A free place nothing plays (not the pads, no track, not waiting to be the pads', not kept for Cancel by the open list) is put away once a pass (`App::put_away_free`, `Effect::TrackSound` with no id), said.
- **How the pads play it** (`main.rs`, `PadsPreview::Host`, mirrored by the smoke session): once a pass the loop points the host's pads player at the pads' holder once it is in (`pads_play`). While the selected track doesn't play that instrument, pad hits, let-gos and pressure go to it (`pad_on_shifted(THE_PADS, …)`), before the arp and the recorder, as the shared synth's try-out does; while the track plays it, through the track. The Synth tab shows and edits the pads' instrument while the selected track doesn't play it (`tab_who`), its subtitle reading "Pads, no track", and the screen hears its name (`ToUi::PadsHost`).

Built on the recommended answer to Jay's card of 20 Nov ("Where should you choose which instrument a pad bank plays?", recommended "In the list of synths": "The list gets 'All banks' or 'Bank B only' at its top, so the synth you tap plays on every bank or only on the bank the pads are on").

### 7a as built (keys play a track's own synth by note)

## Step 9: recording points for audio

The plan's step 9: "Recording points for audio. Record an instrument, a submix or the master into an audio track, before or after its effects, without hearing it twice."

- **What exists.** The mixer's capture points (`mpfree_fx::Mixer::set_capture`, `CapturePoint`: before a strip's inserts, after them, after its fader) already feed the Looper's Records from (`MixHost::loop_take`), the sampler and Freeze and Print (`takes.rs`), each lined up by `capture_latency`. Audio tracks (`mpfree-audiotracks`) record only the line input (`Input::Left`, `Right`, `Stereo`, `Sum`).
- **The slice.** An audio track's input can be a mix: the master, a submix, or a channel, which since steps 4d and 6 is an instrument's ("FM · Pads", "Poly · Track 1", Drums, Synth), before or after its effects. The host points one capture per armed audio track with an inside input (slots below `TAKE_CAPTURE`), hands its last block to that track in place of the line input, and makes up its latency as the looper does.
- **Never heard twice.** A track with an inside input never plays it through (the panel's monitor modes give way to "Not played through": you already hear the instrument where it is). Nothing is refused: the master or a submix records what it carries, the audio tracks' playback included, as a resample does. No loop can form, since no track plays its inside input, and the take is a period behind the mix anyway.
- **On screen (proposed for Jay, layout spec 4c).** The track panel's **Input** row offers, after L, R, L+R and ST, **Mix**. With Mix picked a row more shows **From** (‹, the name, › round `MixHost::inside_sources`: each instrument's channel by its instrument's name, as the mixer panel names it, Drums, Synth, Loop, each submix, Master) and **Effects** (Before, After; After by default). The header's chip reads "Mix". Before or after, the capture is before the fader (`CapturePoint::BeforeInserts` or `BeforeFader`), so the channel's level never changes the take.
- **Saved** on the track's line of the `audiotracks` part: `from=t130:after` (the strip as the mixer's part writes it, then before or after), kept by the engine for the app (`TrackFile::from`). A track with only that set is saved too.
- **Tests.** See 9b and 9c below.

### 9a as built (the engine)

- `mpfree-audiotracks`: `Command::SetInside(track, Some(latency))` makes a track record from inside, `None` puts it back on the line input (refused while it records). The host fills the track's block from inside before each period (`AudioTracks::inside_input`, a fixed buffer of `MAX_PERIOD` stereo frames a track, lent to the runs without allocating). A take from inside is stereo, is placed by the track's own latency in place of the line input's (`punch_in`, and the tail after a punch out), and is never heard through the track, whatever its Monitor says.

- **Tests:** `mixing::tests::an_audio_track_takes_a_strip_before_its_fader`, `the_mix_offers_instruments_submixes_and_the_master`; `audiotracks::tests::mix_and_from_pick_what_the_mix_offers_and_the_project_keeps_it` (round the list, Effects, names that follow, saved with only that set, the text back, opened again, an input chip back to the line input); `mpfree-time-ui` `song::tests::mix_gives_the_panel_from_and_effects_and_the_monitors_give_way`, `view::tests::the_track_panel_asks_for_its_track`; `mpfree-audiotracks` `save` tests (the `from` word round trip); and through the whole session, `smoke::workflows::tests::an_audio_track_records_the_synth_the_pads_play_from_inside_the_mix` (FM picked for the pads, Mix and From + to it on the panel, a pad held for a second while the transport records: the track plays nothing of it, the take is stereo with FM in it, starting within 32 frames of where the pad's note did (9 frames in practice, a tick's rounding), and the project's text names `t<FM's channel>:after`).

## Step 10: pad mutes on every kit and bank

Added on 22 Nov 2026 from the Reddit feedback; Jay chose "One track at a time" on its card (22 Nov, 02:42 UTC). Before it only kit 1's pads A1-A16 muted, because only they had mixer pad channels; another kit's track, or bank B to H, said "mute the track". Akai's MPC 3.10.1 mutes pads within a track (the 3.10.1 guide's Pad Mute and Track Mute modes, pp. 261-264); so does this. Rule 1 holds: a pad mute is what a track plays back, not the instrument's sound, so it lives on the track and never in the mixer.

### 10 as built

- **The model** (`mpfree-seq`): `Track::pad_mutes` and `pad_solos`, a bit for each of the track's kit's 128 pads (A1 is bit 0, H16 bit 127). `Track::silent_pads` is the mutes, and with any solo every pad not soloed. `same_settings` compares them.
- **The player** (`mpfree-play`): `TrackView::pads` is the track's `silent_pads`, compiled with the view; `collect_run` leaves out a note on a silent pad as it leaves out a muted track's, for clips launched, cued, played by a scene or placed in the song. A clip of notes (`holds_notes`) plays every note: its numbers are notes, not pads. Live hits still sound: a pad mute leaves out what the track plays back.
- **Saved** on the track in the clips part: `padmutes A3 B5 H16` and `padsolos ...`, by pad name, only when any is set (`mpfree_seq::save`; an older build skips the words, as its reader skips any it doesn't know).
- **The keys** (`main.rs`): SHIFT + TRACK MUTE's pads mute the selected track's pad on the bank PAD BANK shows (`mute_key`: `App::track`, `App::pad_page`). `no_mutes` (`gui::no_pad_mutes`) is only for a track that plays notes: the synth, or an instrument (`Instruments::note_tracks`). The lights read the track's bits (`pad_light`).
- **The mute queue** (`time/control.rs`): `MuteWait::pads` holds (track, pad, mute, solo); `pad_mute_or_solo` toggles from the track's bits or what waits; `pads_due` lands them in the period before their line by setting the tracks and compiling, so the view compiled then plays the period that holds the line (at most a period and a tick early, as before); `pad_mute_now` is the step grid's speaker. WAITING and the log name the track and pad ("Drums B5 at 9.1", "pad mute: at 2.1, Drums B5 muted").
- **The step grid** (`gui.rs`, `play_page.rs`): `PlayPage::pad_mutes` is the shown track's (`refresh_play`, from `TimeState`'s `TrackInfo::pad_mutes`); a speaker sends `FromUi::PadMute { track, pad, mute }`, which the desk gives `TimeHost::pad_mute_now`. The bit flips on screen at once.
- **The mixer** keeps only kit 1's pad channels' mutes from the Mix tab (`MixHost::pad_mutes`, saved as `chanmutes`), with Drop drums' over them. `PlayFx::PadSolo`, `pad_solos` and `solo_silent` are gone.
- **Old projects:** a mixer part with `padmutes` and `padsolos` (kit 1's A1-A16, for every track) reads them as `MixLoad::old_pad_mutes` and `old_pad_solos`; `MixHost::open` keeps them, and the open (`carry_pad_mutes`, in `main.rs` and the session) gives them to each track on kit 1 that doesn't play notes (`TimeHost::carry_pad_mutes`, one compile), with a log line ("project: kit 1's pads (muted A3) are now each of its 4 tracks' own"). No channel is muted by them.
- **Tests:** `mpfree-play/tests/pad_mutes.rs` (3 tests at periods 1 to 4096: a pad muted on one track is left out there alone, on any bank, cued too, and a muted pad stays silent soloed; a song placement leaves its track's muted pads out; a clip of notes plays every note); `mpfree_seq::save` `a_tracks_pad_mutes_and_solos_come_back_by_pad_name`; `time::tests::a_tracks_pad_mutes_wait_for_the_line_and_leave_its_hits_out_alone`; `tests::the_mute_keys_mute_tracks_or_pads_and_light_what_plays` and `tests::an_old_projects_kit_1_pad_mutes_go_to_each_track_on_kit_1` (`main.rs`); `mixing::tests::old_pad_mutes_open_for_the_tracks_and_the_mix_tabs_mutes_stay`; `project::tests::the_mixer_part_reads_back_into_the_same_mixer` (`chanmutes`, and the old words); `gui::tests::a_track_on_another_kit_mutes_its_own_pads`; the time-ui WAITING test; and through the session `smoke::workflows::tests::a_speaker_on_play_mutes_the_tracks_pad_and_m_in_the_pad_mixer_its_channel`, `in_perform_a_tracks_pad_mute_waits_for_the_bar_and_held_it_solos` and `smoke::phases::tests::track_mute_banks_reach_past_16_tracks_and_shift_mutes_the_tracks_pads`; the session's walker checks each lane's speaker against the shown track's bits.
- **Not built:** a pad mute lands up to a period before its line rather than on its tick, as the mixer's did; a scene does not store pad mutes.

## Step 11: layers, a track's line, routes that name a synth

The rest of steps 7 and 8, left out until each had a place on the screen or a way to record (the plan's "Step 11"; Joshi chose "Build all three" on 22 Nov 2026, 06:19 UTC).

### 11a as built (layers in the engine and the project)

- **Players** (`instruments.rs`): the instrument host has a player for each of the pads' layers beyond the first, `instruments::layer(i)` (rows after `THE_PADS`, `PAD_LAYERS` = 4 in all). Each plays a held instrument by a send, as the pads' player does (`Instruments::set_layer`, `layer_at`), so every note it starts ends where it started when the layer moves on, a key's too (`Instruments::key_held`). The status line says "pads layer 2 plays free 2".
- **The app** (`app/pad_layers.rs`): `App::pad_layers` (the layers beyond the first, packed in order) and `layer_to` (a free place given a layer's synth, waiting for its id, as `pads_to`). Layers go with a synth chosen for the pads (`PadFocus::Instrument`): `App::toggle_pad_layer` adds or takes off a synth of the list; while the pads follow a track that plays a synth it chooses that synth first; over a kit the synth is what they play, alone. Taking off the first moves the next up; the last one stays. Follow track (`pads_back`) and a project opened give them up; the arp starting no longer does (sampler refactor step 15c: each hit plays the layers once, as pressed). A bank with its own (6c) plays it alone (`App::layers_apply`). A free place a layer plays is never put away (`layers_hold`); a layer whose synth leaves the list is taken off, said (`layers_follow_list`).
- **The audio loop** (`main.rs`, `PadLayers`, mirrored by the session): each period it points the host's layer players at `App::pads_layer_holders` and turns the shared synth's layer on or off (`App::pads_layer_synth`), lifting the pedals the keys put down on the layers and ending the shared synth's notes first. A pad hit plays the layers in both the "on their own" arm and the through-the-track arm (not a hit that waits for a Loop pad's line); a let-go and pressure reach them before any arm takes the event. A keyboard's key plays them by note; a key that becomes a pad event doesn't play them twice (`Incoming::music`).
- **Saved** in the pads part since the sampler refactor's step 15b (`PadMap`): `layer instrument 4` for each layer beyond the first, after the bank lines; `bank B` after it for a layer on one bank, `from A` for a kit's source bank. A layer the list hasn't got is kept, missing and silent, and said. (Step 11a wrote a part of its own, `pad-layers`; project format 4 refuses projects that have it.)
- **Tests:** `instruments::tests::the_pads_layers_play_each_hit_on_each_strip_and_end_where_they_started`; `app::tests::a_layer_goes_with_a_chosen_synth_and_never_with_a_kit_or_the_arp`, `the_layers_save_and_open_with_the_pad_map`; through the session `smoke::song_workflows::tests::layers_play_two_synths_from_the_pads_and_save_with_the_project` (a hit and a key reach both, saved, gone at New project; the session builds no instruments at an open, so the reopen is app.rs's test) and `smoke::workflows::tests::the_shared_synth_layers_with_a_synth_picked_in_the_list`.

### 11c as built (REC records each layer on its own track)

- **The plan** (`app/pads_focus.rs`, `RecPlan`, `LayerTake`, `layer_takes`; `desk.rs`, `rec_plan`): beside the first's take, a take for each layer (`App::pads_layer_sounds`, its name and sound; since sampler step 15c every layer of the map, whatever bank the pads are on), worked out as the first's: an armed track that plays its synth already (one of the other armed tracks, or the armed track when the first moves the arm from it) keeps it; else the first free track the first and the layers before haven't taken (`unfit`); else a new one after the first's while the project holds more; else `Full`. `RecPlan::ready` says the armed track plays the first already. - **REC** (`desk.rs`, `rec_synth`): the new tracks go in first, the first's then the layers' (adding a track lets the other armed tracks go and arms the new one), then the arm goes to the first's track, each track that doesn't play its synth gets it as the first's does (`rec_send_on`, `rec_synth_on`), and each layer's track is armed too through `RecArm::add_track` (#639's multi-arm, the same as SHIFT + a tap in REC's list), with the tracks armed before kept; `TimeAction::ArmAlso` goes straight to the time engine, so the press that comes again sees them armed. ### 11e as built (a MIDI route that names a synth)

- **The route:** `mpfree_midi::setup::TrackRoute::synth`, the project's instrument number of a synth no track has of its own (the shared synth, 1, or a free place's), saved as `route = instrument 4 < MIDI · ch 2`. The Routing card's track stepper goes on past the tracks to `mpfree_midi_ui::Live::synths` (the shared synth, then each held free place, by channel name: "Synth", "FM · Pads"; the audio loop fills it twice a second with the tracks' names, `io_route_synths`). A fifth synth is refused there (`setup::ROUTE_SYNTHS`, 4).
- **MIDI in** (`midi_in.rs`): each synth the routes name, once, gets a row (`route_synths_of`); its routes come as routed keys on track `SYNTH_ROUTES` (128) + the row, past every track a route may name. Routes naming a fifth are left out and counted in the summary. The monitor says `To::Synth { id }`. The app takes the rows once each time the setup changes them (`MusicIn::take_route_synths`), and on a project's opening straight from the I/O setup the screen opened first, so a free place only a route plays isn't put away while MIDI in catches up.
- **Playing** (`main.rs`, `RouteSynths`, mirrored by the session): the instrument host has a route row per synth (`instruments::routed`, `set_routed`, after the pads' layers; `WHO` grew by 4), pointed each period at the app's holders (`App::route_synth_holders`); a row naming the shared synth plays it by note (`App::route_synth_shared`). A key's let-go, pressure and slide reach where it went; a row that moves on ends its notes and lifts its pedals there. The keys go through no arp, and the pads and the armed track don't hear them.
- **Kept:** `App::put_away_free` keeps a free place a route names (`App::routes_hold`).
- **REC:** the recording plan takes the route synths after the pads' layers (`App::rec_more_sounds`, `app::REC_MORE` = 3 + 4, and since sampler step 15c the pad map's base and banks' own after them, + 9), so each gets a track of its own as a layer does (an armed track that plays it, a free one pointed at it, a new one), armed with the first in one take; over a kit (the pads follow the armed track) the first is the armed track as it is, and the chip reads "REC → Track 1 + 1 more" (`RecPlan::synth` empty). The audio loop gives each route key to the track recording its synth (`App::route_recs`, the same track picking as the layers', `App::rec_more_tracks`), as the pad its note plays there (`midi_file::pad_on_track`), by `TimeHost::routed_played`.
- **Choices made in building:** the stepper lists every synth with no track of its own, the shared synth too, whether or not a track sends to it, so the list doesn't change under you while you step; a route synth that is also the pads' synth or a layer still gets its own track (two players, two tracks), so its take never mixes with the pads'.
- **A route to a kit** (sampler step 15f, part 3; [sampler-architecture.md](sampler-architecture.md#kits-play-like-any-instrument-step-15)): a route's instrument number can name a kit of the list too (`App::route_synth_kits`). The Routing card's stepper names the kits with sounds past the synths ("Big Kit · kit 2"; step 15e's follow-up).
- **Tests:** `mpfree_midi::setup::tests::a_route_to_a_synth_reads_back_by_its_number`; `midi_in::tests::a_route_that_names_a_synth_plays_it_and_the_monitor_says_so`; `instruments::tests::a_route_row_plays_the_synth_its_route_names`; `mpfree_midi_ui::routing::tests::a_routes_track_steps_on_past_the_tracks_to_the_synths`; the monitor's words in `mpfree_midi_ui::monitor::tests`; `smoke::workflows::tests::a_route_that_names_a_synth_plays_it_and_rec_records_it_on_its_own_track` (FM kept by the route after Follow track, its key on the row, the chip, REC on Track 2 in one take with Track 1's kit).

## Tunings (tunings step T5)

A tuning is the instrument's, not the track's ([tunings.md](tunings.md)): every instrument in the list (the shared synth, each track's own, each free place's, each kit) plays the song's tuning ("Song's") or one of its own, kept by its id (`tunings.rs`). Tracks that play one instrument share its tuning; Duplicate copies it; a preset doesn't carry one. An instrument taken out loses its tuning with its id, so whatever takes its place starts on Song's. The audio loop hands each its tuning when its pick changes (`TuningsHanded`); the project keeps the picks in the `tunings` part, by id. The Synth tab's Tuning row sets its instrument's, and the Tunings page every instrument's and the song's. As built: [tunings.md](tunings.md#t5-as-built) and [the screens](tunings.md#t6-as-built-the-screens).
