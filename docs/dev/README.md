# MPFree developer documentation

> **MPC One hardware and boot:** Start with the [operating-system architecture](hardware/mpc-one-boot-architecture.md) and the dedicated [bootloader guide](hardware/bootloader.md). The [hardware index](hardware/README.md) groups specialized interfaces; [firmware 3.10.1](hardware/firmware-3.10.1-compatibility.md) and the [installer/recovery contract](hardware/installer-recovery-contract.md) have focused references. For people who build MPFree: how it is designed, why, how to build and test it, and what we know about the MPC One's hardware. If you make music with MPFree, read the [user manual](../user/README.md) instead. Rules for AI agents working in this repository are in [docs/agents](../agents/README.md).

Every design page opens with a **Status** line (proposal, approved, built, on main, ran on the device) and a date. Trust the code and the tests over a page whose status is old. When a pull request changes how something works, it updates its page here in the same PR ([how docs stay current](../agents/docs-system.md)).

**Older names in the code and the design pages:** *Beats* (`play_page.rs`, `PlayPage`) is the Sequencer tab's **Steps** view, which was a view of the Play page until the Play, Pads and Sequencer redesign (18 Nov 2026); Play now has one view (`PlayView::All`). *Hello MPC* (and the crate `mpfree-hello`) was the app's early name; the app is **mpfree**.

- [Project safety](design/project-safety.md): opening is all or nothing: the project format and `needs` gate, every part read before anything changes, missing parts taken from New project, a refused open leaving the open project and the browser as they were; then saves committed in order and tests under pressure (phase 0 of the audio tracks rethink, `rust/mpfree-app/src/project.rs`, 2026-11-22).
- [Live shows](design/live-shows.md): patches, keygroups, set lists and the Show view: the approved plan's roadmap and what is built of it, starting with stage A (the engines an open replaced always freed off the audio thread, 2026-11-22).
- [Importing MPC projects](design/mpc-import.md): what MPC project files hold, how confidently each part maps onto MPFree, the staged importer, the inspector (`tools/mpc-import/inspect-xpj.py`), the workstation tool that copies your own projects out of the eMMC backup (`tools/mpc-one/offline-mpc-projects.py`, 2026-11-12), stage 1's keygroup program converter (`rust/mpfree-mpc-import`, 2026-11-15), reading projects straight from the owner's card, and the plan for expansions, factory content in place and Akai's instruments (2026-11-16).
- [Storage](design/storage-spec.md): where settings and user files are saved (the test stick's fourth partition; the eMMC and SD card are never written).

### Time: transport, sequencer, looper

- [Transport](design/transport.md): the time engine's clock that the sequencer, looper, arp and sampler follow: ticks, tempo map, loop, count-in, punch, the same output for any period size, and following another device's MIDI clock (engine in `rust/mpfree-transport`, 2026-11-11; clock follow 2026-11-12).
- [Playback](design/playback.md): clips of any length on the transport: the model, compiling, sample-exact playback, note-offs, a track through the arp, the clip recorder with replace, overdub and take undo, pad pressure recorded onto notes and played back, and a WAV renderer (engine in `rust/mpfree-seq` and `rust/mpfree-play`, 2026-11-12).
- [Scenes and follow actions](design/scenes.md): a row of the launch grid launched together on the bar line, with its mutes switching there, and follow actions that chain clips and scenes (`rust/mpfree-perform`, 2026-11-11).
- [The arrangement, the Song view and live to song](design/arrange.md): clips on a timeline, each block naming its clip until made unique, song mode (a launched clip takes a track over until it stops), scenes into a song and back, the Song view on the Arrange page, and Rec song, which records what the grid plays (and the morph) into the song (`rust/mpfree-seq`, `rust/mpfree-play`, `rust/mpfree-perform`, `rust/mpfree-time-ui`, 2026-11-12).
- [Step sequencer](design/steps.md): steps as a grid over a clip: velocity, length, nudge, chance, pages, chords and the Euclidean fill (`rust/mpfree-seq`).
- [REC records what is armed](design/rec-arm.md): REC, OVERDUB and PLAY act on one armed target from every page, the notes of the track the pads play or the picked loop; REC from stopped readies and PLAY starts; the REC chip on the transport bars names it (sequencer roadmap S2, `rust/mpfree-app`, 2026-11-17).
- [Make and Perform](design/make-perform.md): one project, two ways of answering the hands: the top bar's MAKE / PERFORM chip and SHIFT + MAIN, saved with the project; in Perform MAIN goes to the Clips grid, the arm is pinned, mutes wait for the launch line, REC brings Rec song, deletes need a hold, and the grid and transport bar show what comes next; Back to song, Save state / Reload clip, the song list (Arrange's third view), the scene editor, one-shot clips and live transpose (16 Levels' Transpose, the Record tab's Transpose and Follows) in both (sequencer roadmap S4, `rust/mpfree-app`, 2026-11-17).
- [STEP SEQ: the pads as steps](design/step-pads.md): STEP SEQ makes the pads one sound's steps over any page, with pages on PAD BANK, Follow, ERASE, the lights and the "Pads: steps" chip; a step held takes the Q-Links, a later step tapped is a tie, and COPY copies steps and pages (sequencer roadmap S1, `rust/mpfree-app`, 2026-11-16).
- [The note editor](design/note-editor.md): one clip's notes on a piano roll, as a list or as a tracker on the Arrange page, with Timing Correct, nudge, transpose, chance, condition, ratchet, draw and erase and the Euclidean fill, each edit an undoable take (the MPC's Grid View, List Edit and TC; `rust/mpfree-seq`, `rust/mpfree-time-ui`, 2026-11-12).
- [Capture](design/capture.md): keep what was just played, even without recording: the always-on history, the last phrase cut to whole bars, a new clip in place or notes into the playing clip, undo, the Capture button, and a WAV demo (`rust/mpfree-capture`, 2026-11-11).
- [Audio tracks](design/audio-tracks.md): recording the line input in a line along the song: takes, a take per pass of a loop recording, comping between takes with crossfades, punch in and out, latency, one memory, and a demo (`rust/mpfree-audiotracks`, 2026-11-12); in the pad player on the Song view's lanes, with the track panel, take lanes and saving in the project (`rust/mpfree-app/src/audiotracks.rs`, 2026-11-13).
- [Disk streaming](design/disk-streaming.md): playing long audio from the USB stick and the SD card without a dropout: the seven problems a review found in the audio tracks' engine, the shared stream layer `mpfree-stream` (sources with generations, fixed work on the sound thread, a coordinator that plans by deadline, admission to a RAM budget, recordings that survive a cut), the SD card read-only behind protection steps and the two kinds of card, the device tests, the roadmap, and P0's tools: the streaming survey and `mpfree-disktest --read-only` (plan approved 2026-11-22; tools built, not yet run on the MPC).
- [Looper](design/looper.md): up to eight audio loops on the transport, recorded from the line input or the pads, with layers, overdub and shared memory (`rust/mpfree-looper`).
- [Loop tracks](design/loop-tracks.md): a loop is a clip on a Loop track in the Clips grid, launched, cued, in scenes and in the song like any clip while its audio stays in the looper; the looper's launches on a bar line's frame, and the plan for the model, the grid, recording from a cell and the song (sequencer roadmap S2, `rust/mpfree-looper`, 2026-11-17).
- [MIDI clock](design/midi-clock.md): clock and song position in and out, as bytes (`rust/mpfree-midi-clock`).
- [MIDI I/O, sync, MIDI Learn and CV/gate](design/midi-io.md): the DIN jacks, USB keyboards, MIDI tracks out with DIN pacing, MIDI Learn onto any parameter and the eight CV/gate outputs: hardware facts, the read-only capture that settles the DIN cable and the CV path, the steps that touch the MPC, what musicians ask for and where Akai's MPC falls short, a project's MIDI settings and the I/O view's MIDI, Sync and CV sections, and the proposed screens (`rust/mpfree-midi`, `rust/mpfree-midi-ui`, 2026-11-12).
- [Arpeggiator, Latch and latch patterns](design/arpeggiator.md): research, design and plan for Note Repeat, Latch, the arpeggiator and custom patterns (engine in `rust/mpfree-arp`).
- [Scales](design/scales.md): one scale model for every part that plays in key: the arp, the synth pads and a track's live transpose (`rust/mpfree-scale`).
- [MIDI effects per track](design/note-fx.md): a track's Notes stage before its sound: Transpose, Scale, Chord, Velocity, Note length, Echo, Humanize, Chance, Strum, Latch and Randomize on sample-timed notes, a key's notes ending where they went, with no allocation in the sound loop (`rust/mpfree-notefx`, the engine, 2026-11-17).

### Sound: instruments, effects, mixing, modulation

- [Synths](design/synths.md): the node interface every instrument and effect uses, and the native synths: Poly (subtractive), FM (DX7-style, with DX7 banks), Drums (a model per pad: kick, snare, clap, hats, toms, percussion, with choke groups and kits) Pluck (Karplus-Strong strings and modal mallets), Wavetable, Keys, Drawbar, Squelch, Analog, Strings (a string machine, 18 Nov), Bass (synth basses, 808s and reeses, 18 Nov), Wind (a clarinet, a sax and a flute as waveguides played by the breath, 19 Nov) and Choir (sung vowels through formant filters, 19 Nov), pad pressure, tunings and per-note slides, the Synth tab and its options, demos and costs (`rust/mpfree-node`, `rust/mpfree-synth`, `rust/mpfree-tuning`, `rust/mpfree-synth-ui`, 2026-11-12).
- [Sound presets](design/presets.md): any instrument's sound saved as a plain-text preset file and loaded back to the bit, with settings by name, so old and new presets load (`rust/mpfree-instruments`, 2026-11-11).
- [Wavetable synth](design/wavetable.md): the third native synth: table oscillators scanned by Position, warps, band-limited tables, factory tables, WAV and sample import, memory and device cost estimates (`rust/mpfree-synth`, 2026-11-11).
- [Instruments apart from tracks](design/instruments.md): the instrument redesign (Jay's rule: instruments make the sound, tracks record, the pads play instruments) and its steps: Play on the pads (step 1); lasting instrument ids in a list in the project, with the shared synth as instrument 1, the tracks' own instruments after it and the project format 2 (steps 2a and 2b, `rust/mpfree-app/src/instrument_list.rs`), and lasting track ids (step 2c, `mpfree_seq::TrackId`); pad focus, the synth chosen for the pads staying whichever track is selected, saved with the project (step 3, `rust/mpfree-app/src/app/pads_focus.rs`); a track playing another track's own instrument (step 4a, `rust/mpfree-app/src/instruments.rs`), and knob owners, one track's locks moving a shared knob at a time (step 4b, `rust/mpfree-app/src/locks.rs`), 2026-11-20.
- [Track host](design/track-host.md): each track's instrument fed by the sequencer, the pads and the arp and rendered into its mixer track: slots, routing, notes counted per source, sample-exact parameters, swaps that ring out, STOP, and no allocation on the audio thread (`rust/mpfree-track`, 2026-11-11); in the app, each track can play its own native synth or plugin synth (`rust/mpfree-app/src/instruments.rs`, 2026-11-13).
- [Plugins: the CLAP host](design/plugins.md): CLAP instruments and effects as nodes on a track or in an insert chain: loading `.clap` files, parameters, sample-exact events, state, the main and audio threads, and our own test plugins (`rust/mpfree-clap`), and the sandbox that runs each plugin in its own process with a deadline each block and a restart after a crash (`rust/mpfree-plughost`, 2026-11-11); in the app as mixer inserts and track instruments (2026-11-13).
- [Akai's own instruments](design/akai-instruments.md): how MPFree could play the owner's activated Akai instruments: MPC-format plugins in the sandbox, guest mode (Akai's unmodified app as a background sound engine), the laptop route over offload, what is ruled out and why, and the two read-only checks (`tools/mpc-one/offline-akai-scan.py`, `tools/mpc-one/read-only-akai-app.sh`) (plan, 2026-11-16).
- [The plugin pack](guides/plugin-pack.md): free plugins built for the MPC as CLAP files (Airwindows, Dexed, Surge XT): the recipe in `tools/plugin-pack`, what each file may need from the MPC, the licenses and sources that go with them, and the checks that play them under qemu (2026-11-14).
- [MPC plugins in MPFree](design/mpc-plugin-host.md): `rust/mpfree-mpchost` loads MPC-format plugins (the VST 2.4 binary interface MPC OS uses, as in the Open MPC catalog) in the plugin sandbox next to CLAP: plugin folders, the MPC host's call order, ids, state, and the CI check that runs five catalog plugins as ARM under qemu (2026-11-15).
- [MPC plugin skins](design/mpc-plugin-skins.md): `rust/mpfree-mpcskin` reads an MPC plugin's `Plugin Skins` (TUI.json, Q-Links.json, PNGs) and draws them as MPC OS 3.10.1 does; the Synth tab shows an instrument's skin full width; what it costs and what is left (2026-11-15).
- [Installing MPC plugins: the Plugins place](design/mpc-plugin-store.md): `rust/mpfree-mpcstore` and BROWSE's Plugins place: the Open MPC catalog fetched over Ethernet, release zips checked against their SHA-256 and unpacked safely, zips copied to the stick, updates that keep the musician's files, and new plugins offered without a restart (2026-11-16).
- [MPFree engines as MPC plugins](guides/mpc-plugins.md): `rust/mpfree-mpcplug` wraps an MPFree effect or instrument as an MPC OS 3.10.1 plugin (VST 2.4 binary interface), the ARM build and its checks, and the SDK's host simulator (2026-11-15).
- [FX and the mixer](design/fx.md): the effects and their registry, the insert chain and its picker, Perform FX, the mixer, its routing and the I/O view (`rust/mpfree-fx`, `rust/mpfree-fx-ui`, `rust/mpfree-mixer-ui`, `rust/mpfree-chain-ui`).
- [fxlab and FXProbe](design/fxlab.md): measuring our effects against the MPC's AIR effects: report cards, quality gates, the capture programme, sweep plans, and the two SDK plugins and script that record the factory MPC unattended (`rust/mpfree-fxlab`, `tools/fxprobe`, 2026-11-15).
- [FX quality](design/fx-quality.md): what "good" means for delay, EQ and reverb as measured targets, the `fxlab quality` report, and the ratchet on CI that keeps every number from getting worse (`rust/mpfree-fxlab`, 2026-11-16).
- [Synth quality](design/synth-quality.md): the synth library's measure-only baseline: every engine and factory preset against the research pages' targets (tuning, aliasing, velocity, level, stereo, the filter, glide, envelopes, FM sidebands, drum decays, and the Choir's formants, vowel morph and ensemble), where today's synths stand, the ratchet on CI, and the bench's voice costs for every engine (`rust/mpfree-fxlab`, `rust/mpfree-bench`, 2026-11-18).
- [Synth library: architecture and roadmap](design/synth-architecture.md): stage 3 of the synth library: the engine for each instrument type (new Bass, Strings, Wind and Choir engines, Wavetable folded into Poly, an Ensemble effect), the shared voice core, voices and synth tracks spread over the sound cores, the NEON decision, what a preset carries (macros, effects, play settings), the controller event (sustain pedal, mod wheel, breath) and the roadmap in three waves (design 2026-11-18; the Ensemble, Strings, Bass and Choir are built).
- [Drive and Character](design/fx-drive.md): the design of the curve distortions: what drive is for, the harmonics a curve's shape makes, aliasing and the antiderivative antialiasing inside 2x and 4x oversampling, auto gain on music-like noise, Bias, Character's four colours, the presets, the G targets and the cost (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-17).
- [Crush](design/fx-crush.md): the design of the bit crusher and decimator: bits, dither and the quantiser's shape, sample and hold at exact times with a minimum-phase band-limited step, the filters tied to the rate, jitter, the vintage presets, the B targets and the cost (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-17).
- [Amp](design/fx-amp.md): the design of the guitar and bass amp: the gate, the boosts, tube-like stages with antialiasing, the circuit's tone stack, sag, depth, presence, the filter cabinets, trims fitted on noise, the presets, the A targets and the cost (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-17).
- [Fuzz](design/fx-fuzz.md): the design of the fuzz pedal: Face, Muff and Octave as circuits, the starved Gate, Low keep's crossover for a clean sub, antialiasing, the presets, the targets and the cost (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-17).
- [Delays](design/fx-delay.md): the design of the Digital, Tape and Multi-tap delays: research, principles, algorithms and why, measuring, cost, defaults and presets (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-17).
- [EQ](design/fx-eq.md): the design of the eight-band EQ: matched bells and shelves, the HQ 2x Quality, measuring, cost, defaults and presets (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-17).
- [Reverbs](design/fx-reverb.md): the design of the Room, Plate, Spring and the Cloud engine (Hall, Cloud, Echoes): research, feedback delay networks, decay and level, measuring, cost, defaults and presets (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-17).
- [Chorus, flanger and phaser](design/fx-modulation.md): the design of the modulation effects: research, the moving read and its pitch, width, sync, measuring, cost, defaults and presets (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-17).
- [Movement: tremolo, auto-pan, rotary, ring mod, frequency shifter and auto wah](design/fx-movement.md): the design of the movement effects: what moves in each, the shared LFO (shapes, edges, sync and jumps), the Leslie, ring and single-sideband modulation, the wah, measuring, cost, defaults and presets (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-17).
- [Pitch: pitch shifter, pitch correct, harmonizer and tuner](design/fx-pitch.md): the design of the pitch effects: the delay-line shifter and where it splices (WSOLA), the sinc read, PSOLA for a voice, the tuner, measuring, cost, defaults and presets (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-18).
- [Voice: talk box and vocoder](design/fx-voice.md): the design of the voice effects: formants and a mouth with no notches, Klatt's parallel bank with alternating signs, levels fitted on a saw, a make-up per vowel; the vocoder's alternating-sign bands, envelopes in quadrature, a level that holds as the bands change, and its carriers; measuring, cost, defaults and presets (`rust/mpfree-fx`, 2026-11-18).

### Performance

- [Profiling and performance budgets](design/profiling.md): the shared profiling API, the ARM cost model, per-engine budgets and the bench; the 14 Nov stability review, what it built and what is left; load shedding, the memory floor, the screen's cost and the CI gates, the soak among them (`rust/mpfree-prof`, `rust/mpfree-bench`).
- [NEON kernels](design/simd.md): `rust/mpfree-simd`, the only unsafe code the synths reach: hand-written NEON assembly kernels for the MPC's ARMv7 cores, each with a plain Rust twin that is its reference, checked bit for bit under qemu-arm, counted as ARMv7 instructions and timed by the bench; the supersaw's saws, the Choir's formant filters and the divide-down bank so far, and a kernel stays only if the device round says it wins (2026-11-19).

- [Concurrency](design/concurrency.md): using every core of the MPC One: where threads run today, the core plan, and lanes, which run part of each audio period on another core with a deadline (`rust/mpfree-lanes`, 2026-11-11).
- [Offload](design/offload.md): render partitions on another computer over UDP, answered L periods later with the MPC as the only clock: the datagram, the server, the client, `mpfree-offload measure`, and the offloaded node that falls back to a local Eco stand-in when the link drops (`rust/mpfree-offload`, desktop prototype, 2026-11-12).
